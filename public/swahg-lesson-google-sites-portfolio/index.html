Google Sites Portfolio

Build your first legitimate work sample in 48 hours using everyday tasks you already complete.

Length: 35 minutes For: Fresh Starters with no paid client samples yet Updated: 2026-05-18 Practice output: One published Google Sites portfolio

"Wala pa akong client. Ano ang ilalagay ko sa portfolio?"

Common Fresh Starter problem

Your first proof does not have to be impressive. It has to be real. A beginner portfolio is not meant to prove senior-level expertise. It is meant to prove readiness, honesty, learning capacity, and the ability to produce something useful.

The problem is not Google Sites. The problem is traditional portfolio advice that assumes you already have clients, testimonials, case studies, and measurable results. If you are still starting, your proof can come from learning notes, household systems, school tasks, church service, volunteer work, community help, and self-directed practice projects.

What counts as legitimate proof?

A work sample does not become legitimate because someone paid you for it. It becomes legitimate when it is original, honest, relevant, explainable, and presented with context.

QualityMeaningBeginner check
HonestIt is labeled correctly.Call it a self-directed sample, practice project, learning project, volunteer project, school project, or personal system.
RelevantIt connects to a real remote-work skill.Documentation, data tracking, presentation support, research, organization, or communication.
FinishedIt has a clear version 1 output.A simple finished sample beats a sophisticated draft.
ContextualizedThe viewer understands what they are seeing.Explain the goal, tool, steps, skill shown, and next improvement.
Easy to openThe file is shareable without friction.Set Google Drive files to Viewer access before publishing the portfolio.
Trust builder

Do not pretend a practice sample is paid client work. Write this instead: "I am not presenting these samples as paid client work. They are self-directed practice outputs created to show my current skills and learning process."

The five sections

Do not use a portfolio structure built for experienced freelancers yet. No Previous Clients. No Testimonials. No fake case studies. Use a structure built around learning proof.

Google Sites page structure

1. Learning Journey
How you are becoming capable. Include tools, learning focus, outputs, and next steps.
2. Skills in Action
What you can actually do. Connect every skill to evidence from a sample.
3. Sample Work
The center of the portfolio. Show three beginner-friendly proof assets.
4. Current Focus
The kind of entry-level remote work you are preparing for.
5. Contact
Your professional email, location or time zone, and opportunities you are open to.

Build three work samples first

The homepage sentence is not your proof. The samples are your proof. Build the samples before polishing the hero section.

Sample 1. Weekly Meal Planning Process Document

Tool: Google Docs. Skill: process documentation.

Document structure
  1. Objective: create a weekly meal plan that fits schedule, budget, ingredients, and preparation time.
  2. Inputs: available ingredients, weekly budget, number of meals, schedule, preferences, cooking time, grocery list template.
  3. Constraints: busy days, budget limit, ingredients that must be used first, quick meals, shared household preferences.
  4. Process: review the schedule, count meals, check ingredients, choose meals, group the grocery list, check against budget, adjust, save notes.
  5. Quality check: ask if another person can follow the process without extra explanation.
Portfolio card: This self-directed sample turns a repeated household task into a clear process document. It demonstrates process thinking, documentation, task sequencing, checklist creation, and clear written communication.
Sample 2. Personal Budget Tracker Sample

Tool: Google Sheets. Skill: data organization.

Spreadsheet tabs
  1. Expense Log: date, category, description, amount, payment method, need or want, notes.
  2. Category Summary: category and total.
  3. Monthly Overview: total income, total expenses, remaining balance, top spending category, one simple chart.
  4. Notes: explain that the tracker uses sample or anonymized data.
Portfolio card: This self-directed sample uses anonymized or sample financial entries to demonstrate spreadsheet organization, categorization, basic totals, visual summary, and clean labeling.
Sample 3. Learning Notes Presentation Sample

Tool: Google Slides. Skill: presentation support.

Slide structure
  1. Title slide.
  2. Why this topic matters.
  3. Key idea 1.
  4. Key idea 2.
  5. Key idea 3.
  6. Example or mini walkthrough.
  7. Checklist.
  8. Summary and next step.
Portfolio card: This self-directed sample turns learning notes into a short presentation. It demonstrates information organization, slide structure, beginner-friendly explanation, and visual communication.

Write the About section without pretending

Do not start with a title you cannot support yet. Start with direction.

Copy-ready formula

"I am a beginner remote-work learner building practical proof in documentation, spreadsheet organization, and presentation support. I am using Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Sites to create self-directed samples that show how I organize information, follow a process, and communicate clearly. I am currently preparing for entry-level support work, and this portfolio shows three completed practice outputs."

Do not write that you are highly experienced, seasoned, proven, or an expert if you cannot support it yet. A Fresh Starter builds credibility by being specific, not by sounding bigger.

Connect skills to evidence

SkillEvidenceRemote-work connection
Process documentationMeal planning process document with objective, inputs, constraints, steps, checklist, and quality checks.SOPs, onboarding checklists, admin processes, and task instructions.
Spreadsheet organizationBudget tracker with sample entries, categories, totals, overview, and chart.Trackers, lists, lead sheets, content calendars, and weekly summaries.
Presentation structureLearning notes deck with purpose, key ideas, example, checklist, and summary.Training materials, recap decks, onboarding guides, and client explainers.
Written communicationEach sample includes purpose, process, skill, and reflection notes.Async updates, documentation, application messages, and handoffs.

Practice. Build your Google Sites portfolio in 48 hours.

  1. Hour 1: Open Google Sites. Choose Blank. Add section headers: Home, Learning Journey, Skills in Action, Sample Work, Current Focus, Contact.
  2. Hours 2 to 5: Open Google Docs. Create "Weekly Meal Planning Process Document."
  3. Hours 6 to 9: Open Google Sheets. Create "Personal Budget Tracker Sample" using sample or anonymized data.
  4. Hours 10 to 13: Open Google Slides. Create "Learning Notes Presentation Sample."
  5. Hours 14 to 18: Write a short portfolio card for each sample: title, tool, purpose, what you made, skills demonstrated, what you would improve next.
  6. Hours 19 to 24: Add the three sample cards to Google Sites and set every linked file to Viewer access.
  7. Hours 25 to 30: Add Learning Journey and Skills in Action. Every listed skill needs evidence.
  8. Hours 31 to 35: Write About and Current Focus. Keep the wording honest and beginner-friendly.
  9. Hours 36 to 40: Clean the design. Use short paragraphs, consistent headings, and enough white space.
  10. Hours 41 to 44: Preview on desktop and mobile. Check that links open and permissions are Viewer, not Editor.
  11. Hours 45 to 48: Publish and write a short application description explaining the three samples.

Before you publish

  • The site has all five sections.
  • The three samples are finished enough to inspect.
  • Every sample says it is self-directed, not paid client work.
  • Every sample link opens.
  • Every Google file is set to Viewer access.
  • The Contact section uses a professional email address.
  • No sentence claims expertise you cannot explain.
  • The mobile preview is readable.
Application description

"I created a beginner Google Sites portfolio with three self-directed work samples: a process document, a personal budget tracker, and a learning presentation. These samples show my current practice in documentation, spreadsheet organization, and clear presentation structure. The portfolio is honest about my beginner stage and includes notes on what each sample demonstrates."
