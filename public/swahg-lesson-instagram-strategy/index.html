Instagram Strategy

Yung 5-pillar IG framework. Hindi viral lottery. Yan ang sistema.

Length: 20 minutes For: SMM specialists managing client IG Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Posting ako 3x/week sa IG ng client. Pero stuck sa 800 followers. 6 months na."

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, hindi sa cadence ng post yung problem. Walang strategy. Yung IG ay ecosystem ngayon: Feed + Reels + Stories + DMs + Hashtags + Engagement. Pag may lang ng isang lane, freeze. Today natin sasamahan ang 5-pillar approach.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Ilang post per week dapat?"

Tama: "Anong content mix sa 5 IG pillars ang nakakabuo ng community at sales para sa client ko?"

The 5 IG content pillars (2026)

PillarWhat it doesCadence (per week)
1. ReelsDiscovery + reach. Algorithm favors them in 2026.3-5 Reels/week
2. CarouselsEducation + saves. High engagement, info-dense.2-3 carousels/week
3. StoriesDaily engagement. Behind-scenes. Polls + Q&A.5-7 stories/week
4. DMs + RepliesCommunity + sales. Algorithm boosts engaged accounts.15 min/day minimum
5. Single image postsBrand consistency. Quote / product highlight.1-2/week max

Yung warning sa "viral chase"

Stop chasing viral

Yung 1 viral Reel without infrastructure = 0 conversion. Steady consistency (5 Reels/week for 6 months) > random viral attempt. Yung algorithm rewards reliability.

Practice. Build 1-week IG plan. 45 min.

  1. Pick a client (or your own brand) IG.
  2. Audit current pillar coverage. Which pillars are missing?
  3. Build next week's calendar in Google Sheets. Use the sample above.
  4. Pre-batch 3 Reels in CapCut. Save 4 hours of daily work.
  5. Schedule via Meta Business Suite.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

Do this week

Do this week
  1. Build Reels-first strategy.
  2. Use Canva templates for carousels.
🛒 Solo

E-com IG = your home. Apply to clients.

Do this week
  1. Replicate shop IG growth tactics for clients.
  2. Carousels for product education.
📋 Generalist

SMM is a wedge option. Structure-driven.

Do this week
  1. Build IG calendar template in Notion.
  2. Sell as reusable client deliverable.
🌱 Fresh Starter

Master IG on your own account first.

Do this week
  1. Apply 5-pillar to your personal IG for 30 days.
  2. Track results. Then pitch to clients.
