Discover Your Professional Skills

16 Personality Test + career path mapping. The wedge decision.

Length: 60 minutes For: Active Freelancing Journey learners Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"I know I want to freelance. But what is my real skill? I am not sure."

Common Freelancing Journey Lesson 2 question

Friend, your skill has two components: personality + experience. The 16 Personality Test helps you see personality fit. Your experience is already there. Today we will combine both into a wedge.

How to read your 16 Personality results

4 letter codes + career path implications

Introvert (I) vs Extrovert (E)
I = async/text-heavy roles. E = sales/SMM/customer-facing.
Sensing (S) vs Intuition (N)
S = data, admin, bookkeeping. N = strategy, content, creative.
Thinking (T) vs Feeling (F)
T = technical, analytics. F = customer success, community.
Judging (J) vs Perceiving (P)
J = project mgmt, admin. P = creative, exploration.
Common patterns among SWAHG students

ISFJ → Customer Support · INFJ → Content Strategist · ENFP → SMM Manager · ISTJ → Bookkeeping VA · INTP → Data Analyst · ENFJ → Virtual Assistant (Executive). This is not rigid. 16P is a starting point, not destiny.

The warning about personality test

Tool, not answer

The 16 Personality Test is popular, but it is not hard science. Use it as a conversation starter, not a prescription. If the result goes against your lived experience, trust your experience.

Practice. Take test + map career path. 60 min.

  1. Take 16 Personality Test. Honestly, no pretending.
  2. Note your 4-letter type. Read career path section.
  3. List 5 careers suggested by your profile.
  4. Cross-reference with Specialized Business Skills + Creative + Artistic Skills + My Curriculum.
  5. Pick top 1 wedge. Write a paragraph why.
  6. Submit Google Doc via ASSIGNMENT TRACKER.

Action items, based on your archetype

🌟 Polished

Validate current wedge against personality.

Do this week
  1. Reconfirm wedge fit.
  2. Note 1 adjacent skill that personality suggests.
💼 Transitioner

BPO defaults often: ISFJ / ISTJ. Customer Support fit.

Do this week
  1. Look at non-BPO suggestions in your personality results.
🎨 Creative

Likely INFP / ENFP / INFJ. Confirm creative path.

Do this week
  1. Cross-ref with creative path from Lesson 18.
🛒 Solo

Likely ESTJ / ENTJ / ENTP. Operations bias.

Do this week
  1. Operations VA / e-com Manager paths fit.
📋 Generalist

Use test to FORCE wedge decision.

Do this week
  1. Pick top 1. No "I'll do everything."
🌱 Fresh Starter

Use as exploration tool. Discover, don't commit yet.

Do this week
  1. Take test. List 3 paths to research more.
