SMM Core Strategies

The 5 strategies used by successful Social Media Managers. WHO, WHY, WHERE, WHAT, HOW.

Length: 18 minutes For: Anyone considering or growing in SMM (43 jobs / 18% of Job List) Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"I have a client as an SMM. But I am not sure what I should post. I am just winging it."

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, the post itself is not the problem. The problem is no foundation. No strategy. The 5 SMM Core Strategies are the foundation. Without them, even your most polished Reel will miss the audience. You know WHO you are now and you know WHO you want to become. That is the beginning.

The wrong question vs the right question

Wrong question: "Which viral trend should I copy?"

Better question: "WHO am I, WHO is my client, WHO is my audience, WHY are we doing this, WHERE are they, WHAT do they need, HOW do I deliver?"

The 5 SMM Core Strategies

#StrategyWhat it answers
1WHO (you)You know WHO you are now and you know WHO you want to become. For your personal brand and for the client side.
2WHY (motivation)You know WHY you are doing this and WHY you will not stop just to get there.
3WHERE (audience)You know WHERE your clients are and you understand their culture.
4WHAT (need)You know WHAT exactly your clients need and you can deliver.
5HOW (execution)You know HOW you can achieve those business needs and achieve success.

Example: a 5-strategy audit for an early SMM gig

Example first-SMM-client audit

WHO (you)
Generalist VA wanting to specialize into SMM
WHO (client)
e.g. a Filipino food brand, ~5K IG followers, wants to scale
WHY
The values you'd bring to the work (e.g. faith-grounded VA building a sustainable lifestyle)
WHERE
Instagram + Facebook PH market 25-45 yo
WHAT
Branded daily content + weekly Reels + monthly stories
HOW
Canva + Later / Buffer + Google Sheets calendar + monthly review
The honest moment

Most beginners do this audit AFTER 6 months of fumbling. That wastes time. Run the 5-strategy audit BEFORE applying to SMM clients. Save yourself 6 months of "post and pray."

The warning about "I'm just posting"

The "post-and-pray" trap

Without the 5 strategies in place, your SMM work becomes "post-and-pray." Often no engagement. No growth. No impact. Your client may disengage after 90 days. The 5 strategies are required infrastructure.

Practice. Audit yourself OR your next client. 25 minutes.

  1. Open Google Doc. Title: "SMM 5-Strategy Audit."
  2. Answer each of the 5 strategies in 2-3 sentences. On the client side, ask the client these questions explicitly.
  3. Identify gaps. If there is an "I don't know," that becomes the priority area for the next 30 days.
  4. Download the SMM Handbook. Get password from Work At Home Geek FB page.
  5. Use SMM Handbook templates to flesh out each strategy.
