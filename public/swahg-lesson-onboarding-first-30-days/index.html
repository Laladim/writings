Onboarding · First 30 Days

Yung 30 days after hire ay where most VAs lose the client. Sayang sa lahat ng prep. Yung framework ay 4 weeks, 1 theme each.

Length: 25 minutes For: VAs starting their first or new client engagement Updated: 2026-05-15 (v1) Best after: Resume Builder · Interviews · Common Mistakes

"Na-hire na ako. Wala akong alam paano magsimula. Ano ba ang dapat kong gawin sa first week?"

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Most VAs improvise the first 30 days. Result: ~40% client churn rate sa first 90 days. Hindi sa kulang ang skill. Yung kulang ay onboarding discipline. Yung first 30 days ay para sa learning ng client, hindi sa display ng skill. Kapag binali mo yung order, magiging dahilan na para mawalan ka ng tiwala. Yung lesson ay 4-week structure that protects retention.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong dapat kong gawin para magaling ako sa unang araw?"

Tama: "Anong order ng learning, asking, doing sa unang 4 weeks para hindi ako maging burden sa client?"

Yung order: Week 1 learn + observe, Week 2 small wins + clarifying questions, Week 3 ownership of recurring tasks, Week 4 propose 1 improvement. Reversed order (Week 1 proposing improvements) ay common rookie mistake. Yan ay why-fired-fast pattern.

The 4-week onboarding framework

WeekThemeWhat to actually do
Week 1Learn + ObserveRead every doc/SOP. Watch every Loom they sent. Take notes. Ask only blocking questions. Do not propose changes. Goal: understand their world.
Week 2Small wins + clarifying questionsTake on small clearly-defined tasks. Deliver them on time. Batch your "non-blocking" questions for one weekly check-in, not Slack-trickle.
Week 3Own recurring tasksTake ownership of 1-2 recurring tasks (weekly report, daily Slack triage, content batch). Yung ownership = you do without prompting.
Week 4Propose 1 improvementOne observation, one proposal. Format: "I noticed X. I propose Y. It would save Z." Single bullet, optional pursuit.
The 4 themes mapped to client psychology

Yung client ay binilihan ka ng certainty. Week 1-2, they want certainty na you can show up + understand. Week 3, certainty na you can own recurring work. Week 4 onward, certainty na you can make their work easier. Yung order is psychological, not just operational.

Example: a common solid Week 1 first-day flow

The first day after contract signed

Hour 1 · Welcome ack
Reply to client welcome message within 1 hour. Format: thank you + your work hours + a specific question about access to tools they mentioned. Sets professional tempo.
Hour 2-3 · Tool access audit
List every tool the client mentioned. Email/DM the client: "I have access to A, B, C. Still need invitations to D, E. Will follow up on E by EOD if not received."
Day 1 afternoon · Read everything
SOPs, brand guide, past Loom walkthroughs, internal docs. Notebook open. Write 5 clarifying questions for Friday batch, not 5 separate Slack messages today.
Day 1 EOD · Wrap-up note
Send a 3-line update: "Today I read X, set up access to Y, drafted my first Z. Tomorrow I will Q." Habit established. Client gets visibility without managing.
Day 2-3 · Shadow
Observe their normal communication rhythm. Pace your own Slack messages to match. Pag silent ang team sa morning, hindi ka mag-Slack-bombing sa morning.
Day 5 · First weekly check-in
15-30 min call or async note. Present: what I did this week, what I learned, what I am unclear about (3 questions max). Client feels managed, not burdened.
The architecture insight

Onboarding is impression layering, not skill display. Yung skill mo ay confirmed sa interview. Yung onboarding ay confirming impressions ng client: trustworthy, organized, calm, learning-oriented, low-maintenance. Pareho ng skill, ibang outcome based on onboarding posture.

Yung warnings na hindi pinag-uusapan

Do not propose changes in Week 1

The most common fire-able mistake: new VA sees a "better way" sa Week 1 and Slacks the client about it. Yung client interprets ito as: "you don't trust my judgment, you don't respect our existing system." Save proposals for Week 4. Earn the trust first.

Do not vanish without notice

Personal emergencies happen. Family illness, brownout, internet outage. Always send a 1-line "heads up" message before the missing window, not after. "Internet just went out. Pocket WiFi backup engaging. Will update in 30 min." Yung communication ng disruption is the trust signal, hindi yung absence of disruption.

Practice. 25 minutes, build the Week 1 plan.

  1. Draft your "Day 1 welcome reply template." 3-5 sentences. Thank you + work hours + 1 question about tool access. Save in Google Doc for reuse.
  2. Draft your "Day 1 EOD update template." 3-line structure: today did X, set up Y, tomorrow Q. Reusable across clients.
  3. Build a "first-week question batch" template. Numbered list, max 7 questions, grouped by topic (tools, process, communication). Send Friday after Week 1.
  4. Build a "weekly check-in template." Format: completed (3 bullets), in progress (2 bullets), questions (max 3). Use every Friday for first 4 weeks.
  5. Practice the "Week 4 proposal" structure. Pick an imaginary client. Write 1 observation + 1 proposal + 1 outcome estimate. Single bullet, professional pitch.
  6. Build a personal onboarding folder. Google Drive folder: "Client [Name] Onboarding." Subfolders: Tools Access, SOPs, My Notes, Weekly Updates. Yung structure ay reusable per future client.

Audit checklist:

  • Day 1 welcome reply template drafted
  • Day 1 EOD update template drafted
  • Week 1 question batch template drafted
  • Weekly check-in template drafted
  • Sample Week 4 proposal practiced
  • Onboarding folder structure created in Google Drive

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer ~25% · onboard like a senior

Multiple clients = multiple onboardings. Templatize aggressively. Yung 30 minutes saved per onboarding pays in retention.

Do this week
  1. Build a "Client Onboarding Kit." Templates: welcome reply, tool access tracker, first-week questionnaire, week 1-4 check-ins, week 4 proposal. Send as combined doc on Day 1 to new client.
  2. Add a "compass call" in Week 2. 30-min sync: "Here's what I've learned. Here are the 5 themes I see. Which 2 do you want me to focus on?" Yung framing is senior, not subordinate.
  3. Track your retention rate. Out of last 10 clients, how many stayed past 90 days? Past 6 months? Yung data ay tells you if your onboarding works.
Recommended setup: Onboarding Kit doc · compass call in Week 2 · retention tracker. Senior-VA infrastructure.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner ~30% · BPO onboarding rituals transfer

Yung BPO onboarding muscle memory ay direct match. Yung difference: self-driven sa freelance, not HR-driven.

Do this week
  1. Replicate BPO "training certification" mental model. Week 1: learning. Week 2: shadowing. Week 3: side-by-side. Week 4: solo. Yung structure mo na yan from BPO, port it.
  2. Bring your "metrics-from-day-1" habit. Track your own KPIs even if client doesn't ask. Tasks completed, hours billed, response time. Show in Week 4 check-in.
  3. Communicate "shift end" formally. EOD update sa BPO ay automatic. Replicate as freelance habit. Client appreciates structure.
Recommended setup: BPO-shape onboarding flow · self-tracked KPIs · EOD habit. Familiar discipline, freelance application.
🎨 The Creative Specialist ~15% · brand absorption phase

Creative onboarding takes longer kasi brand absorption. 4 weeks is tight. Plan for Week 5-6 polish.

Do this week
  1. Build a "brand sponge" Week 1. Read every brand guide, every past campaign, every internal Loom. Save links sa a brand bible doc. Internalize voice.
  2. Week 2-3: small deliverables only. 1 IG post, 1 Reels edit, 1 email graphic. Hindi pa mag-pitch ng campaigns. Yung confidence mo ay needs grounding sa their tone first.
  3. Week 4: present 1 mood board or campaign concept. Yung formal creative proposal, mas welcome by then. Earlier, premature.
Recommended setup: Brand bible doc · small-deliverables Week 2-3 · 1 concept Week 4. Creative-VA onboarding shape.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur ~15% · ops parallel

Onboarding habit ay parallel sa shop ops. Treat client as "second shop" with different rhythm.

Do this week
  1. Allocate fixed daily hours to client. Yung 4 hours/day at 9 AM-1 PM (or whatever fits client timezone). Predictable. Yung shop hours ay separate, untouched.
  2. Bring your "first 90 days as a customer" lens. Yung client experience mo ay similar. Apply the same warmth.
  3. Track which client tools overlap with shop tools. Yung overlap ay efficiency gain. Yung non-overlap ay learning investment.
Recommended setup: Fixed daily client window · customer-empathy lens · overlap tracker. Two-business clarity.
📋 The Generalist Admin ~10% · natural fit

Admin profile thrives sa onboarding discipline. Yung 4-week framework ay your home turf.

Do this week
  1. Build a "Week 1 audit report." By end of Week 1, send: tools accessed, SOPs read, observations noted (no proposals yet), questions for Friday call. Yung deliverable ay confidence-builder.
  2. Master the "weekly check-in PDF." 1-page summary per week. Pareho ng executive briefing format. $5-10/hr rate impact over time.
  3. Pair with Time Tracking lesson. Yung tracked time + weekly summary + onboarding milestones = senior-VA polish.
Recommended setup: Week 1 audit report · weekly PDF summary · time tracking from Day 1. Senior-admin infrastructure.
🌱 The Fresh Starter ~5% · first job protection

Yung onboarding ay where most fresh VAs fail. Hindi sa skill. Yung pacing. Yung Week 1-2 protection ay critical.

Do this week
  1. Practice the Day 1 + Day 1 EOD templates 3 times. Send to a friend playing client role. Get feedback. Refine.
  2. Repeat to yourself: "Week 1 = learn, not prove." Yung tendency to over-deliver, suppress. Show up, observe, take notes. Yung "magaling ako" is for Week 4.
  3. Find a SWAHG community accountability partner. Pag onboarding mo ay starting, may kasama ka magpa-check sa Week 1, Week 2, etc. Yung outside pair of eyes catches anxious mistakes.
Recommended setup: Day 1 templates rehearsed · "learn not prove" mantra · community accountability. Anti-rookie-mistake armor.
Universal rule

Sa lahat ng archetype: 30 days is short. Hindi mo kailangang maging legendary sa Day 5. Kailangan mong maging reliable sa Day 30. Yung restraint, observance, calm communication ay the senior moves. Yung over-effort sa unang linggo, junior move. Slow down to retain.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.
