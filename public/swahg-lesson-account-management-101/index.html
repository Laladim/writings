Account Management 101

Yung skill na nag-co-convert ng "na-hire ako" into "kinukuhanin ako every quarter." Cadence + proposal anatomy + scope discipline + retention.

Length: 30 minutes For: VAs with at least 1 active retainer; aiming Account Manager roles Updated: 2026-05-15 (v1) Prerequisite: Onboarding · Choosing My Client

"Yung client ko, parating gusto ng dagdag na trabaho, pero hindi naman tumataas yung bayad. Paano ko sasabihin?"

Common question from new SWAHG learners

This is the most common scenario sa second to fourth month ng new VA retainer. The skill of handling scope creep without losing the client is what separates "got hired" from "kept the client." Account Management is not "salesperson." It is steward of the relationship. Yung lesson covers the 5 disciplines that protect both you and the client.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Paano ko i-upsell yung client ko?"

Tama: "Anong cadence, contracts, at conversations ang gagawing predictable yung relationship namin, para sa both sides?"

Yung account management ay not selling. It is reducing surprise. Weekly updates kill surprise. Clear scope kills surprise. Quarterly review kills surprise. Yung magic ng senior VAs ay hindi sa charm. Yung magic ay sa predictability.

The 5 disciplines of Account Management

DisciplineWhat it doesCadence
1. Weekly check-inStanding call or async update. 3 sections: completed (last week), in progress (this week), questions/decisions needed (max 3). Predictable rhythm.Every Friday or Monday
2. Documented scope1-page scope doc updated quarterly. Lists: deliverables, frequency, exclusions. Single source of truth. Hindi mo na isipin "is this included?" because it's written.Quarterly review
3. Scope creep responseWhen client requests beyond scope: acknowledge → quote → confirm. "Yes, I can do that. It would be X additional hours / Y additional fee. Should I proceed?" Honest, not defensive.As it happens
4. Monthly retainer reportVisual summary: hours billed, top deliverables, key wins. 1-page PDF or Loom video. Yung report ay justifies invoice without client asking.End of each month
5. Quarterly business review30-60 min strategic conversation. Review last 3 months, plan next 3. Discuss expansion or pause. Yung session ay where retention compounds.Every 3 months
The retention math

Average VA churn rate (industry benchmark): ~40% in first 90 days, ~25% per year after. Implementing the 5 disciplines drops yung first-90-day churn to ~10-15%. Yan equivalent ay extending lifetime value 3-4x without finding more clients.

Example: a common solid modern proposal anatomy

1-page proposal structure for a $1,500/month retainer (2026 standard)

Section 1 · Objective (2-3 sentences)
Name the client's outcome, not your tasks. "Help [Client] grow Instagram engagement from 1.5% to 3% over 90 days while reclaiming 15 hours/week of founder time."
Section 2 · Deliverables (5-8 bullets)
Specific outputs: "20 IG posts/month · 8 Reels edits · 12 newsletter drafts · weekly content calendar · monthly analytics report." Quantified, not vague.
Section 3 · Process (3-4 bullets)
How work happens: "Weekly content batch every Monday · Friday async check-in · monthly Zoom review · Trello shared board for visibility."
Section 4 · Investment
"$1,500/month, billed first of each month, net-7 payment terms. 90-day initial commitment, month-to-month after."
Section 5 · Scope boundaries
What's NOT included: ad spend management, paid sponsorships, live-event coverage. Optional add-ons listed separately with their own rates.
Section 6 · Next steps
"Reply 'approved' to start. Onboarding kit will be sent within 1 business day. First deliverable in Week 2."
The architecture insight

A proposal is a one-page contract translation. Yung old "20-page proposal with case studies" ay obsolete 2026. Modern proposals: 1 page, scannable, focused on the client's outcome. Hindi sa convincing ka. Yung outcome ay enough convincing.

Yung warnings na hindi pinag-uusapan

Yung silent scope creep is the killer

Yung "pwede ba dito na lang yung 1 extra task" eventually becomes 5 extra hours/week. Sa month 3, you are billing same rate for 50% more work. Yung resentment builds. Yung client wonders why you're slower. Both wrong outcomes. Surface every scope creep immediately with the acknowledge-quote-confirm framework. Not "after 3 weeks pala."

Do not "discount" your way to retention

Client signals "tight budget," tendency is to lower rate to keep them. Trap. Yung discounting trains them: "you can ask for less and she'll say yes." Pag may budget issue, mas mahusay na reduce scope at same hourly rate, kaysa same scope at lower hourly. Yung dignity ng rate, protect.

Practice. 30 minutes, build your AM toolkit.

  1. Draft a weekly check-in template. 3 sections: Completed (5 bullets), In Progress (3 bullets), Questions/Decisions (max 3). Save in Google Doc.
  2. Draft a 1-page scope doc template. Deliverables, frequency, exclusions. Reusable across clients with name+detail swap.
  3. Practice the scope creep response. Imagine client says "can you also do X?" Write 3 candidate replies. Pick the best one. Save as a Slack/email canned response.
  4. Build a monthly retainer report template. 1-page Google Doc. Sections: hours worked, top 3 wins, deliverables completed, suggested next 30 days. Reusable.
  5. Draft a quarterly business review agenda. 5 questions for client + 5 metrics to show + 1 forward-looking proposal. Aim 30-60 min total.
  6. Build a 1-page proposal template using the 6 sections above. Save as Google Doc + PDF version.

Audit checklist:

  • Weekly check-in template drafted
  • Scope doc template drafted
  • Scope creep response practiced (3 candidates, 1 final)
  • Monthly retainer report template drafted
  • Quarterly business review agenda drafted
  • 1-page proposal template ready for use

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer ~25% · senior account manager

Multiple clients = multiple accounts to manage. Yung difference between $20/hr and $40/hr ay account management discipline.

Do this week
  1. Audit each active client's "discipline coverage." Weekly check-in (yes/no), documented scope (yes/no), monthly report (yes/no), quarterly review (yes/no). Fill gaps starting next week.
  2. Build a "client P&L" view in your tracking. Hours billed, hours actually worked, effective hourly rate per client. Yung data ay tells you who to expand and who to release.
  3. Conduct 1 quarterly review this month with your longest client. Yung session ay where rate raises happen.
Recommended target: Senior Account Manager / Fractional COO · ~$30-50/hr · 2-3 retainers max, deep relationships.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner ~30% · client services natural fit

Yung BPO client services background ay perfect match. Yung difference: smaller client list, deeper relationship.

Do this week
  1. Translate "client service" muscle to freelance. Pareho ng SLA discipline, NPS-style satisfaction check, escalation flow. Apply at smaller scale.
  2. Build a "client health" tracker. Per client: response time, hours used vs scoped, satisfaction signal (proxy: how often client thanks vs corrects). Weekly review.
  3. Practice the "calm during scope creep" response. BPO trained you in this. Bring the muscle to freelance scope creep.
Recommended target: Client Services VA · ~$15-25/hr · 3-5 retainers, medium-touch.
🎨 The Creative Specialist ~15% · creative AM is rare premium

Creative VAs who also handle account management earn $40-60/hr. Yung combination ay rare and valuable.

Do this week
  1. Build a "creative deliverables tracker." Per client: campaigns delivered, engagement metrics, asset reuse rate. Yung data sells expansion.
  2. Practice presenting work visually. Loom walkthroughs of campaigns, before-after engagement screenshots, mood-board pivots. Yung visual presentation, mas selling.
  3. Position as "Creative Director-Lite." Yung framing ay senior + creative + business-aware. Pay band reflects ito.
Recommended target: Fractional Creative Director · ~$30-50/hr · 1-2 deep retainers.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur ~15% · business owner empathy

Yung shop owner background ay your secret weapon. You speak business owner, not freelancer.

Do this week
  1. Frame check-ins around "business outcome," not "tasks done." Yung shop owner client cares about revenue + customer retention + inventory turnover. Map your work to those.
  2. Bring an "owner-to-owner" tone sa proposals. Less corporate, more peer-to-peer.
  3. Specialize sa ecom or local business retainers. Yung niche, mas naturally drawn to your background.
Recommended target: Ecom Account Manager · ~$20-35/hr · 2-3 shop retainers.
📋 The Generalist Admin ~10% · ops + AM hybrid

Yung admin discipline + AM skills = "Operations + Account Management" hybrid role. Premium VA tier.

Do this week
  1. Build a master client tracker. All clients in one Sheet: scope, cadence, retainer fee, expansion potential, renewal date. Yung tracker ay your dashboard.
  2. Build automated weekly report generation. Trello/Asana → Sheet → Doc → PDF. Yung automation, 30 min/week saved per client.
  3. Practice the "calm boundary" voice. Yung scope creep responses, mas hard for admin-natural-helpers to say no. Practice 5 candidate responses para feels natural.
Recommended target: Operations + AM Hybrid VA · ~$20-30/hr · 3-4 retainers with automated infrastructure.
🌱 The Fresh Starter ~5% · build from first client

Wala pang client. AM skills ay best learned WHILE managing your first retainer. Practice the templates now, apply when ready.

Do this week
  1. Draft all 6 templates from the practice section. Yung work sa template ay portable, save until ready.
  2. Apply the weekly check-in cadence sa SWAHG learning. Every Friday, "what I learned this week + what I'm working on next" sa SWAHG group. Habit-build.
  3. Find an accountability partner. Pair with another SWAHG learner. Practice scope conversations using imagined scenarios. Build the muscle before the actual client.
Recommended target: Defer AM specialty until month 4-6 of first retainer. Foundation + onboarding skills first.
Universal rule

Sa lahat ng archetype: relationship over rate. Yung pinaka-mahalaga sa Account Management ay hindi yung Linguistics ng selling. Yung respect ng dalawang panig. Pag walang respect, walang rate raise. Pag may respect, lahat ng negotiation easier. Yung respect ay built sa weekly cadence + clear scope + honest scope creep response.
