Trello Love

Pag walang board mo, "tanga ka ba?" yung sasagot ng client pag nakaligtaan mo yung task.

Length: 20 minutes For: Every freelancer running more than one task at a time Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"Ate, nakaligtaan ko yung deadline. Naitext yata pero nawala sa tig-isang dosena ng chats."

Carla, SWAHG group, lifetime member

Hindi sa stupid ka, friend. Hindi sa kulang yung memory mo. Yung problema, walang external brain mo. Hindi kaya ng ulo natin lahat ng deadline ng 5 clients. The brain is for thinking, not for storing. Yan ang dahilan kung bakit ang every senior freelancer ay may board. PM tool ay 23% coverage lang sa SWAHG community. Yung 77% ay sa text + memory pa lang. Yan ay drop-out funnel.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Mahirap ba mag-Trello?"

Tama: "Anong sistema ang sasabay sa akin sa first client at scales up sa fifth client?"

Trello is the SWAHG default kasi: (1) free tier ay enough for 95% of freelancers, (2) mas mabilis matutunan kaysa Asana o Notion, (3) pareho ng visual sa client briefings. Pag may client na gumagamit ng ClickUp o Monday.com, mag-adapt ka. Same logic lang.

The 4 essentials of a working Trello board

EssentialWhy it mattersHow to do it
1. Standard list flowSame lists across every board = predictable. Inbox → This Week → In Progress → Waiting → Done.5 lists, in this order
2. Due dates on every cardCard without due date = task without urgency. Even "no rush" deserves a date.Press D to add due date
3. Labels for client / typePag may 3 clients ka, color labels = visual scan. Red = ClientA, Blue = ClientB, Green = ClientC.Press L to label
4. Checklists for multi-stepYung "edit + publish + send invoice" ay 3 tasks, not 1. Checklist within the card.Add Checklist button
Power-Up insight

Free Trello = 1 Power-Up per board. Gamitin yang ito sa Calendar Power-Up para makita yung due dates as a calendar view. Pag 2+ Power-Ups gusto mo, upgrade to Standard plan (~₱300/month) or use the calendar built-in to Google Calendar with Trello-import.

My project management stack

Yung mix ng Trello (per project) + Notion (per writing) + plain text BACKLOG (raw brainstorm), yan ay not what I recommend for a freelancer just starting. Yung kayo, start with one tool: Trello. Pag-master mo, dagdagan. Yung sa akin, 4 years of evolving system.

The honest moment

I tried Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, Linear, GitHub Projects, Airtable. Hindi sa wala silang silbi. Sa Trello ako bumalik pala kasi: predictable, fast to set up per new client, at hindi nag-overthink ako kung anong field i-fill. The best tool is the one you actually open every day.

Yung warning na hindi pinag-uusapan sa video

PM tool coverage is 23%

Yung VA Assessments data (641 candidates): only 23% report using a PM tool. Yung 77% ay sa text + email + memory pa lang. Yung skill na ito, dramatic na advantage. Sa interview mo: "I use Trello to track client deliverables across 3 active retainers" beats "I am organized" 10x. Concrete > adjective.

Other warning: do not let the client see your messy Trello. If you keep an internal "brain dump" board, separate from the "client-facing" board. Show them outcomes, hindi yung lahat ng false-start cards.

Practice. Buksan natin ngayon, 18 minutes.

  1. Sign up at trello.com with your professional Gmail (per Lesson 02).
  2. Create your first board. Name: "SWAHG Job Hunt." This will be your application tracker.
  3. Add 5 lists in this exact order:
    • 📥 Inbox (raw job leads)
    • 📅 This Week (applying to)
    • 📤 Sent (applications submitted)
    • ⏳ Waiting (replies pending)
    • ✅ Done (interview / closed)
  4. Add labels: Red (Urgent), Yellow (Promising), Blue (Backup option), Green (Confirmed interview).
  5. Create your first 3 cards. Pick 3 jobs from SWAHG Job List or OnlineJobs.ph. For each: title, company name, due date (apply by when), label, link in description.
  6. Setup Calendar Power-Up. Show menu (right side) → Power-Ups → Calendar → Add. Now you can see due dates in calendar view.
  7. Install Trello mobile app. Yung commute moments, can be used for review.

Audit checklist:

  • Trello account created with professional email
  • "SWAHG Job Hunt" board exists with 5 lists
  • 4 labels created with colors
  • At least 3 cards added with due dates + links
  • Calendar Power-Up installed and showing dates
  • Mobile app installed and synced

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer ~25% · proceed ~80%

Multi-client = multi-board. Trello as your operating system.

Do this week
  1. One board per active client. Naming: "ClientName_Y2026Q2" so you can archive cleanly per quarter.
  2. Add Butler automation. When a card moves to "Done," auto-archive after 7 days. Saves manual cleanup time.
  3. Templates for repeated workflows. Yung "monthly content batch" mo, gawing card template. Click → instantiate → done.
Recommended stack: Trello Standard or Premium plan + Butler + templates + per-client boards.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner ~30% · proceed ~60%

BPO ticketing trained. Trello is the freelance translation.

Do this week
  1. Mirror BPO ticket lifecycle as your lists. New → In Progress → Pending → Resolved → Closed. Same DNA, different surface.
  2. Use checklists for SOPs. Yung "process every ticket the same way" ng BPO, recreate as a Trello checklist template. Concrete + repeatable.
  3. Track SLA-equivalent timing. Add due date + label "Within 24h" / "Within 48h" / "EOM." Yung BPO discipline mo, signal it.
Recommended stack: Trello free + checklists per card + due date discipline + label by urgency.
🎨 The Creative Specialist ~15% · proceed ~70%

Creative work = drafts, revisions, approval cycles. Trello is the review tracker.

Do this week
  1. Lists by review state: Concept → Draft 1 → Client Review → Revisions → Approved → Delivered. Yung approval cycle, visualized.
  2. Attach preview images. Drag-and-drop your latest version directly into the card. Cover image shows up in the board view. Visual scan.
  3. Link to Figma / Canva in card description. Pag click sa card, jumps direct sa source file.
Recommended stack: Trello + Figma Power-Up + drag-drop cover images + per-project boards.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur ~15% · proceed ~55%

Already managing inventory + orders + customer queries. Now needs lighter task surface.

Do this week
  1. Migrate your business tasks to Trello first. Before freelance, fix the existing chaos. Sa next 2 weeks, log ALL business todos sa Trello. Habit-build with familiar context.
  2. Reuse your batch-thinking skill. Yung "Monday morning batch" sa shop, replicate sa freelance: pick 1 day per week as "deep work" day. Yung Trello cards filtered by that day.
  3. Separate boards: Business · Freelance · Family. 3 boards minimum. Hindi mixed.
Recommended stack: Trello free with 3 boards + batch-day filter + checklist templates.
📋 The Generalist Admin ~10% · proceed ~50%

Natural fit. Trello rewards organization-minded people.

Do this week
  1. Build a "Master Index" board. Cards = each client. Description = link to the client's own dedicated board. Single starting surface.
  2. Master Trello shortcuts. N (new card), D (due date), L (label), F (filter), B (open menu). 5 minutes to learn, hours saved.
  3. Pair with Google Calendar + Toggl Track. Trello = what to do. Calendar = when. Toggl = how long. Yung trinity of admin productivity.
Recommended stack: Trello + Calendar + Toggl Track + master index board + shortcut mastery.
🌱 The Fresh Starter ~5% · proceed ~30%

Hindi mo pa alam kung anong PM tool. Start with the simplest.

Do this week
  1. Just one board: SWAHG Job Hunt. Yung practice board mo, gamitin nang seryoso. Track every application sa loob ng 30 days.
  2. Don't customize anything yet. Default colors, default lists. Yung simple, easy to maintain. Pag-add ng kaharian later.
  3. Open Trello every morning, 7 AM. 5 minutes to review board + add new cards from yesterday. Yung morning ritual ay key.
Recommended stack: Trello free + 1 board + daily 5-minute review ritual. Yan na muna.
Universal rule

Lahat ng archetype: open Trello before you open Facebook every morning. Yung 5-minute board review, sets the day. Pag inverted yung order, distracted yung araw mo.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek):

  1. Screenshot ng "SWAHG Job Hunt" board mo. 5 lists, at least 3 cards. Tag: "Archetype: _____. Day 1 of tracking apps."
  2. OR a 1-minute Loom tour. Walk through your board, explain your label system. Practice ka rin sa Loom.
