Create a Resume That Stands Out

Yung 1-2 page document na first impression mo. Hindi 5 pages, hindi 1 line.

Length: 60-90 minutes (resume build) For: Active Freelancing Journey learners Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Ate, hindi ko alam kung pano gumawa ng resume na hindi BPO-style."

Genalyn, SWAHG Freelancing Journey learner

Friend, may dalawang options ka. (1) Ang luma: gumawa sa Word + manual layout. (2) Yung modern: use yung interactive SWAHG Resume Builder. Yung pangalawa, mas mabilis, archetype-aware, at may live job-match. Both shipped. Choice yours.

The 5-step assignment (Canva path)

#Step
1Watch the lesson video.
2Create account at canva.com. Free.
3Search "Resume" templates. Pick ATS-friendly one (single-column, no graphics-heavy).
4Fill in: contact, professional summary (from Lesson 14), skills, experience, education.
5Get shareable link. Submit via ASSIGNMENT TRACKER FORM (Title: Lesson 3).
Alternative: SWAHG Resume Builder

Yung SWAHG Resume Builder ay built. Step-by-step. Archetype quiz Step 1. Auto-fill skills. Live job-match Step 3. Letterpress preview. Export PDF. Hindi mali na gamitin ito instead of Canva. Pareho yung output.

The non-negotiable resume sections

1-page structure

Header
Name + role title + city + email + LinkedIn + portfolio link
Professional Summary
2-3 sentences. Wedge-specific. No clichés.
Skills (4-8)
Work-used, not aspirational. From Resume Builder Step 2.
Experience
Reverse chronological. Bulleted achievements with numbers.
Education
School + degree + year (only if recent or relevant).
Certifications
Any cert from Lesson 17-19 work. Even partial.

Yung warning sa "design over content"

ATS-friendly

Canva templates sometimes have multi-column layouts + graphics. Yung ATS (Applicant Tracking System) ng client cannot parse those. Single column. Standard fonts. No icons sa skill names. Tasteful design ay enough.

Practice. Build first resume. 90 min.

  1. Decide: Canva or SWAHG Resume Builder.
  2. If Canva: pick ATS-friendly template. Single column.
  3. If SWAHG Resume Builder: open it. 5-step wizard.
  4. Fill in all sections honestly. No padding.
  5. Export PDF. Test shareable link in incognito browser.
  6. Submit via ASSIGNMENT TRACKER FORM.
