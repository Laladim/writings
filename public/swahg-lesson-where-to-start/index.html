Where to Start?

"Saan ba talaga magsisimula? Sabay-sabay ba lahat? O isa-isa?"

Length: 15 minutes For: Anyone overwhelmed by the SWAHG Blueprint at first glance Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"Ate Lala, nahihilo ako. Nakita ko yung Blueprint, parang ang dami. Anong unang gagawin ko?"

Catherine, SWAHG group, message last week

Tama yung pakiramdam mo. Yung Blueprint ay 30+ boxes. Pag tinignan mo nang buo, nakakatakot. Pero sasabihin ko sa iyo yung sikretong walang nag-tuturo: hindi lahat ng box ay para sayo this month. Yung first 3 boxes pa lang. Pag yan natapos mo, doon palang sa next 3. Yung Blueprint ay map, hindi to-do list.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong skill ang pinakamabilis kumita?"

Tama: "Anong skill ang pinakamadaling sundan ng commitment ko, depende sa current na situation ko?"

This lesson is the bridge between Preparation (Boxes 1-5) at Launch (Boxes 11-15). Yung warm-up stage ay tungkol sa mindset at planning. Pag sinkip mo ito, lalapit ka sa interviews na hindi clear yung sasabihin mo. So kwento muna ako sa kung paano natin gagawin yung plano nang tama.

The Freelancing Journey reward

Pag tinapos mo lahat ng activities at assignments sa Freelancing Journey lesson series, may free 1-on-1 coaching ka with Shela. Hindi gimmick. Earned. The discipline of finishing IS the screening filter.

The honest moment

Hindi ako overnight success. Yung "Year 1" mo, mukhang slow. Pero yung consistent na 2-3 hours per night, that's the muscle that builds Year 5. Kapag tinanggap mo na, hindi sprint ito, marathon, lalakad ka nang hindi sumusuko.

Yung warning na "matagal-tagal pala"

90-day reality check

Hindi ka magkakaclient sa unang linggo. Hindi sa second linggo. Probably hindi din sa first month. The average SWAHG learner who lands first client lands it between months 3-6. Hindi yan failure. Yan ay normal. Kapag in-expect mo ang fast result at hindi nangyari sa 30 days, susuko ka. Yung kalahati ng SWAHG drop-outs ay nasa month 1 dahil dito.

Practice. Gawin natin ang 90-day plan, 20 minutes.

  1. Open a new Google Doc. Title: "My SWAHG 90-Day Plan."
  2. Answer 4 questions in writing:
    • How many hours per week can I commit? (Realistic.)
    • What skills do I have RIGHT NOW? (No fantasizing.)
    • What career path will I pick? (Remote Job · VA · Specialized Skill: only one.)
    • What's my finish-line date for first client? (90 days from today.)
  3. Break the 90 days into 3 phases: Days 1-30 (foundation), Days 31-60 (skill build + applications), Days 61-90 (interview + first contract).
  4. Add 1 weekly check-in to Google Calendar. "SWAHG Sunday Review · 30 minutes."
  5. Bookmark the Blueprint map. Pag nahihilo ka, balik sa map. Yan ang reset button.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25% · proceed ~80%

Already started. This is the lesson where you formalize your next 90-day growth plan.

Do this week
  1. Set the 90-day stretch goal. Not first client. Next: raise rates by 20%, add a retainer, launch a service line. Write it down.
  2. Audit which skills you've outgrown. Yung tools mo from 2 years ago, may bago na. Pick 2 to upgrade in the next 90 days.
  3. Mentor a Fresh Starter inside SWAHG. Reciprocity builds your reputation in the community. Apply at SWAHG Internship as a mentor.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30% · proceed ~60%

Job na meron. Risk-averse. Plan must protect cash flow.

Do this week
  1. Do NOT quit your job in the first 90 days. Side-hustle muna. Para may safety net.
  2. Block 10 hrs/week on weekday evenings. Use Google Calendar blocks. Treat as second job.
  3. Save 3 months expenses before resignation. Pag dumating yung first freelance client, may runway ka pa para hindi mag-panic.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15% · proceed ~70%

Portfolio is half-built. 90 days is enough to land first paying creative client.

Do this week
  1. Build a portfolio of 10 pieces. Hindi kailangan paid client. Mock projects okay. Quality matters, not source.
  2. Pick a niche specialty. Real estate IG · F&B reels · podcast covers. Vertical = price floor goes up.
  3. Setup Behance + Dribbble profiles. Hindi optional sa creative. Yung portfolio surface, kailangan public.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15% · proceed ~55%

Already running business hours. Plan must integrate freelance into existing schedule.

Do this week
  1. Decide: VA-while-running-business OR sell-and-shift. Yung dalawa pwede. Pero kailangan clarity.
  2. Reuse your business skills as resume bullets. Operations + Customer Service + Marketing: 3 transferable VA skills.
  3. Set 90-day plan to land 1 retainer at minimum ₱30K/month. Yung income mo as VA, dapat replace at least 50% of current shop income.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10% · proceed ~50%

No specialty yet. 90 days is enough to pick + start one.

Do this week
  1. Pick your wedge. Bookkeeping VA · Inbox-Zero VA · Calendar EA · Travel Coordinator. Decide by end of week.
  2. Take ONE free course on your wedge. HubSpot Academy, Google Digital Garage, or LinkedIn Learning (free with PH library card).
  3. Apply to 5 jobs/week starting Day 30. Quantity matters. 20 applications in month 2 = baseline.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5% · proceed ~30%

No experience. No tools. No clients. Honest start.

Do this week
  1. Commit to 90 days of consistency. Hindi sa quick result. Sa habit.
  2. Pick ONE free course this week. Start with OnlineJobs.ph Free Training or Google Digital Garage.
  3. Day 30 goal: 1 finished course + 1 SWAHG group introduction post. Yung introduction post, public commitment device.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek):

  1. Public 90-day commitment. "I am [Archetype]. My finish-line date: [date]. My career path: [Remote Job · VA · Specialized Skill]. My hours: [N]/week."
  2. Tag a study buddy. Find one person from the group to be your accountability partner. Weekly check-in.
