Email Address Expert Secrets

Hindi pwede yung sexyblue_27@yahoo.com. Walang client na nag-rereply doon.

Length: 15 minutes For: Every learner before applying to a single job Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"Ate, nag-aapply na ako pero walang nag-rereply. Tatlong buwan na."

Maricel, SWAHG group, posted twice last month

Pwede kong tingnan yung resume mo. Pero bago natin gawin yan, pwede mo ba ipakita sa akin yung email address na ginagamit mo sa apply? Kasi minsan, hindi sa resume mo nagkukulang. Kundi sa kung kanino siya nanggaling.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Ano yung pinakamadaling email password ko?"

Tama: "Kapag hiring manager binasa yung email address ko, anong unang impression niya?"

Yung first question, magbibigay ng email account. Yung second question, magbibigay ng professional identity. Free pa siya. Hindi mo na kailangan bumili ng domain. Gmail lang, gawin lang natin nang tama.

The 5 rules of a freelancer email address

Sundan natin yung 5-rule framework. Hindi mahirap, pero kailangan strict.

RuleWhy it mattersExample
1. Use your real nameHiring manager wants to know who you are in 1 second. Nicknames at numbers confuse the search.maricel.santos@gmail.com
2. Gmail onlyYahoo + Hotmail signal old-school. Gmail works on every tool a client uses.@gmail.com (not @yahoo.com)
3. Add a middle initial or last name if takenPag kuha na yung maricel.santos@gmail.com, gumamit ng maricel.b.santos o maricelsantosva.maricel.b.santos@gmail.com
4. No numbers like birth year"maricelsantos1985" gives away age. Hindi natin kailangan magbigay ng ammunition sa bias.Avoid: maricel1985@gmail.com
5. One email per identityPersonal email at freelance email, dapat magkahiwalay. Pag may ka-thread ka sa client, hindi pwede may "[Shopee notification]" sa inbox mo.2 separate Gmail accounts
Free, walang catch

Hindi mo kailangan bumili ng domain like yourname.com sa simula. Yan ay Tier 3 upgrade. Pero aim for that within 12 months. Pwede sa Namecheap (~₱500/year) o sa Google Domains. Pag may yourname.com email, may signal ka na "this person treats freelancing as a business."

The professional signature

Sa baba ng bawat email, may signature na may: pangalan, role, link sa LinkedIn profile mo, at link sa portfolio kung meron. Use HubSpot's free generator kung wala kang ginawa pa. Mas magaling pa siya kaysa sa Gmail default.

Yung warning na minsang nakalimutan kong sabihin

Hindi ito drama

Pag nag-apply ka gamit yung cutiepie_maricel_2003@yahoo.com, hindi ka magrarami ng tatlong segundo sa inbox ng hiring manager. Hindi yan kakayanin pati ng resume mo. Yung email address ay first filter. Pagkababa nito, walang resume ang papasok. So bago ka mag-overhaul ng CV mo, fix yung email muna. Two minutes lang.

At isa pa: huwag mong gamitin yung work email ng dating company mo (BPO o office) sa pag-apply ng freelance work. Mawawala yan pagkaalis mo. At kung mabasa siya ng employer mo habang nag-aapply ka pa sa kanila, problema.

Practice. Gawin natin ngayon, 10 minutes lang.

  1. Open Gmail signup. Gumawa ng bagong account: firstname.lastname@gmail.com. Pag kuha na, gamitin yung middle initial.
  2. Setup recovery options. Add a phone number + recovery email. Pag nakalimutan mo password, gusto mo maibalik agad.
  3. Enable 2-step verification. Sa Google Account Security, turn on 2FA. Yung client mo malalaman ito at magugulat sa professionalism mo.
  4. Set your profile photo. Headshot. Clear face. Plain background. Hindi selfie sa beach. Hindi anime.
  5. Create your signature. Use HubSpot's free generator. Save inside Gmail settings: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  6. Test it. Send a sample email to yourself from your old email. Tignan kung gumagana yung signature at kung professional ang dating.

Audit checklist:

  • New Gmail uses real first + last name
  • No birth year, no nicknames, no underscores with random words
  • 2-step verification enabled
  • Profile photo is a clear, plain-background headshot
  • Signature includes: name, "Virtual Assistant" / role title, LinkedIn URL, location (City, Philippines)
  • Personal email separated from this freelance email

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

Iba ang lebel ng email-game per archetype. Take the Resume Builder quiz kung hindi mo pa alam yung archetype mo.

🌟 The Polished Freelancer ~25% · proceed ~80%

Multi-client roster already. Email is a brand surface, not just a contact channel.

Do this week
  1. Upgrade to a custom domain. Buy yourname.com from Namecheap (~₱500/year), forward to your Gmail via Google Workspace (₱360/month). hello@yourname.com signals senior immediately.
  2. Setup canned responses. Gmail Settings → Advanced → Templates. Save 3 templates: project inquiry, rate request, scheduling. Save 5 minutes per email.
  3. Add scheduling link to signature. Use Calendly free tier. Drop your booking URL right in the email signature. Hindi na ping-pong ng "what time?"
Recommended stack: Custom domain (Namecheap) + Google Workspace + HubSpot signature + Calendly. Total ~₱500/year domain + ₱4,300/year Workspace.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner ~30% · proceed ~60%

BPO discipline, may corporate email muna. Now needs a freelance identity.

Do this week
  1. Don't use your BPO/company email for freelance apps. Mawawala yan pag-resign mo. Gumawa ng bagong personal Gmail this week.
  2. Mirror BPO professionalism in your signature. Yung corporate trabaho mo, may signature na may title + dept + contact. Copy that structure for your freelance email. "Customer Support Specialist · 6 years experience · Manila, PH"
  3. Setup folder system day one. Gmail labels: Applications, Active Clients, Admin, Personal. Gmail filters auto-tag incoming. Yung BPO ticketing skill mo, gamitin doon.
Recommended stack: New Gmail + 2FA + HubSpot signature + Gmail filters for auto-labeling. All free.
🎨 The Creative Specialist ~15% · proceed ~70%

Portfolio is your asset. Email is the bridge to it.

Do this week
  1. Lead with portfolio link in signature. Hindi LinkedIn yung pinakaimportante para sa creative. Yung Behance, Dribbble, o personal portfolio. Make it the top link.
  2. Custom email like contact@yourname.com. For creatives, having a branded email matters even more. Get a custom domain ASAP, even before Polished tier. Use Namecheap.
  3. Visual signature, but tasteful. Logo + name + portfolio + IG. Use HubSpot generator or design your own in Canva. Test it renders on Gmail mobile.
Recommended stack: Custom domain + portfolio-first signature + Behance/Dribbble link. Optional: Notion-based portfolio.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur ~15% · proceed ~55%

Already had business emails, but mixed with personal life and storefront.

Do this week
  1. Hard separate: business, freelance, personal. 3 emails minimum. Old shop email stays for closing accounts. New freelance email for VA work. Personal for family.
  2. Migrate vendor contacts before closing old business email. Export contacts via Google Contacts. Don't lose your supplier list pag pinatay mo na yung shop email.
  3. Translate business experience into signature. "Operations · E-commerce · 4 years scaling a [N]-customer business." Frame the entrepreneur background as a transferable skill in the signature itself.
Recommended stack: 3 separate Gmail accounts + Google Contacts export + bold signature that names the business background as an asset.
📋 The Generalist Admin ~10% · proceed ~50%

Calendar-disciplined, email-organized already. Translate that skill into signal.

Do this week
  1. Pick a wedge in the signature. "Bookkeeping VA · QuickBooks + Xero" or "Inbox-Zero VA · Help Scout + Front" or "Executive Assistant · Remote · Calendly + Notion." Mas specific mas mabuti.
  2. Setup Gmail filters mastery-level. Sa Gmail Filters page: auto-archive newsletters, auto-label by sender domain, auto-star from active clients. Yung admin skill mo dito magpapakita.
  3. Pair Gmail with Google Calendar + Tasks. Yung calendar invite, hindi pwedeng makaligtaan. Master Google Calendar + Tasks integration this week.
Recommended stack: Gmail + advanced filters + Calendar + Tasks + wedge-driven signature. All free.
🌱 The Fresh Starter ~5% · proceed ~30%

First Gmail account ever, or only had a school email. Start clean.

Do this week
  1. Just one email lang muna. Hindi mo kailangan ng 3 accounts. Isang professional Gmail with real name. Yan na.
  2. Simple signature. Name + "Aspiring Virtual Assistant · Currently learning [skill]" + Location. Yung "currently learning" line, signal of growth, hindi ng inexperience.
  3. Practice inbox discipline. Check email twice daily, hindi every 5 minutes. Use Gmail mobile with notifications OFF for the freelance account. Focus tool, hindi distraction.
Recommended stack: One Gmail + 2FA + headshot photo + simple HubSpot signature. Bumalik ka after Client #1 para sa next-tier upgrade.
Universal rule

Lahat ng archetype: profile photo is mandatory. Yung email signature, walang epekto kung walang face. Use the same photo across LinkedIn, OnlineJobs.ph, and Gmail. Same face, same brand. Hindi confusing.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek) ang isa sa dalawa:

  1. The before-after screenshot. Litrato ng dati mong email signature vs yung bago. Tag yourself: "Archetype: _____. New signature stack: _____."
  2. The 2-question check. "Tama ba yung email setup ko? maricel.b.santos@gmail.com. Signature: [paste here]. Anong mas magaling pa?"

Yung ka-batch mo, mag-rerefine kasama mo. Yan ang community ladder.
