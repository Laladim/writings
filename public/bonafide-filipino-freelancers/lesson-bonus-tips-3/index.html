E-commerce Classes

Free Amazon, eBay, and dropshipping resources. If e-commerce is your niche, start here.

Length: 35 minutes (overview) For: Solo Entrepreneurs + anyone exploring e-com VA Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"I want to become an e-commerce VA. What should I learn first?"

Common question from new BFF learners

Friend, I already compiled free courses for you. You do not need to pay $497 to begin. These seven free training resources are enough for a 90-day e-commerce VA foundation.

How VAs monetize e-com knowledge

E-commerce Customer Support
Inbox management, returns, refunds. ₱20-40K/month.

The warning about "be your own seller" pitch

Not guaranteed

Some courses sell the dream of becoming your own Amazon millionaire. That is not guaranteed. The more reliable path is to be the VA who helps existing Amazon sellers. Lower risk, faster income.

Practice. Pick 1 + complete. 90 days.

  1. Pick ONE of the 7 resources above. Choose the one that best fits your target service wedge.
  2. Allocate 5 hours/week. Calendar block.
  3. Finish in 60 days. Document key takeaways in Google Doc.
  4. Day 60: pitch to 5 Shopify / Amazon store owners on LinkedIn.
  5. Day 90: first paying e-com VA client.
