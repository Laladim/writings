Before you begin

Before you share information

This workspace uses Eduba, a third-party platform. Files and chats you submit are subject to Eduba's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and are processed using its AI providers. Read both before continuing.

Your resume may include your own personal details. The assistant asks for your explicit agreement once; if you agree, it continues. You do not have to clean the file first or upload a fictional replacement. Share only information you have permission to provide. Your name, phone, email and profile links are optional. The draft omits unnecessary personal attributes and your full home address. Avoid government or licence numbers, passwords, other people's private contacts and confidential employer records; these are excluded from working records and outputs. Eduba stores uploads before AI checking, so details omitted from your draft may remain in the original upload. You are the final reviewer of your draft. Check every claim before using it.

You do not need to read the AI instruction files. Use the chat for guidance and open outputs for your documents. Click Collapse sidebar to put the file tree out of the way; Show files brings it back when needed. This changes the view, not file access.

Have the BFF invitation email and use that same email address to sign in. The account is yours. Start with the welcome in step 3. Access is open for invited BFF members. Follow these steps in order. You review and approve your own exact draft before using it.

4

Give consent and add your resume

Read the notice above. Your own personal details can stay in your resume if you explicitly agree to share them.

  1. The assistant asks: Do you explicitly agree to Eduba and its AI providers processing your resume, including the personal details you choose to share, and your answers to create and improve your VA resume package?
  2. If you agree, reply Yes. If your file was already uploaded, that answer lets the assistant continue. You do not need to clean it or upload it again just because it contains your own personal details.
  3. To add a file, click Inputs above the chat. Under Workflow inputs, click Choose file and select your TXT, DOCX or PDF with selectable text. You can paste your resume instead.
  4. Wait until the selected filename appears. Tell the assistant it is uploaded. After consent it must read the actual text before drafting.
  5. Give your real daily responsibilities, role interests, work setup and available hours when asked. An unknown answer stays unknown. Never copy an example number.
See that it workedThe assistant saves your consent, confirms readable source text and saves your progress. It continues with professional facts and leaves unnecessary personal fields out of the working documents. Upload success alone does not prove the text was read.
No resume, or unreadable file?Say you have no file and provide professional facts, one question at a time. If you do not consent to processing the uploaded resume, say No and use this no-file route instead. TXT and DOCX are the simplest file choices. A scan needs text extraction first; if the assistant cannot read your PDF, paste readable text. Upload success alone is not text-reading success.

What the AI does behind the chat

The workspace follows the converter's six stages. You provide facts and review the result; the files hold the saved work.

  1. 1. IntakeShow the notice, ask once for explicit consent, then read the actual resume and omit unnecessary personal fields from working documents.
  2. 2. ProfilePreserve roles, dates, tools and evidence from the source.
  3. 3. Target roleChoose one supported VA direction and keep other interests for later.
  4. 4. GapsSeparate demonstrated skills, close matches and missing proof.
  5. 5. Draft and reviewWrite, critique and revise an honest draft. You make the final truth check.
  6. 6. PackageSave six Markdown documents and check them. The candidate finishes the working resume.

If you get stuck

I cannot see the BFF workspace.

Check your signed-in email in Account menu and select Bonafide Filipino Freelancing Community. If it is still missing, ask BFF to check the invitation and workspace access for that email.

I cannot see the file list.

Click Show files, then open outputs for your documents. Ask the assistant for your saved progress. You do not have to read its internal records. If the documents are still absent, check that you reopened the same workbench.

The assistant is still marked “working”.

Check the latest files and reply before assuming it finished. Reopen the chat; if it remains stuck, use a new chat in the same workbench and ask it to continue from your saved work. If it cannot confirm saved work, ask BFF for help.

The file uploaded, but the assistant cannot read it.

TXT and DOCX are the simplest choices. A photo or scanned PDF needs text extraction first. If the assistant cannot read the file, paste readable text instead. It should ask for a readable source instead of inventing an empty-source draft. You can also say you have no file and answer from your real professional history, one question at a time.

My resume contains personal details. Must I edit it first?

No. Read the notice and answer the explicit consent question. A clear Yes lets the assistant continue with your own resume. It saves that answer for this workbench so you are not asked again on every visit. Unnecessary personal fields are left out of the working documents; the original upload may still contain them. If you prefer not to consent, say No and use the no-file professional-history route.

Do I need my own Claude subscription or API key?

BFF configured Haiku centrally for this workspace. This walkthrough uses the provided Eduba workspace. If you see a billing or key error, stop and report it to BFF; do not put a key in a chat. Future member allowances and pricing have not been announced here.

Why does a reply label still say Sonnet?

The pilot's reply labels did not match its serving model. BFF checked actual Claude Console requests and confirmed Haiku. Leave the provided model setting as BFF configured it.

Can other members see my resume?

The intended setup gives each member a separate private workbench. Before sharing information, check Private · just you. Leave Add people unchanged: adding someone shares the chat history. Bounded two-account tests denied cross-member chats and selected file links. This is not a guarantee about Eduba administrator access or provider retention; read Eduba's privacy terms.

An older chat requires a fictional or cleaned resume.

That chat uses older instructions. Use the current BFF VA Resume Converter link at the top of this guide. Your own personal resume details are accepted after explicit consent. Do not upload a real source to an old test workbench. Templates do not necessarily update existing files.

How do I put the resume in Google Docs?

Open the current resume under outputs, choose Source, click Copy, and paste into your own Google Doc. Format its headings and bullets. After an Eduba edit, copy the new text again. Your Google Doc does not sync back. PDF delivery is not part of this version.

Can I ask the AI to change a Google Doc I pasted into?

The assistant edits its saved Markdown files in Eduba. Your separate Google Doc does not sync back. Review the revised Eduba file and copy the current text again.

Can I start another task, such as building a portfolio website?

Follow the learning path for your resume and portfolio evidence. A separate automated website workflow has not been qualified in this pilot. BFF will provide the appropriate workspace and instructions when it is available.
