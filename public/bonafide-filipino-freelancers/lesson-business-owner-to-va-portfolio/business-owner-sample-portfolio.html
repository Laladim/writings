Summary

I ran a small online clothing shop for three years. I handled inventory, suppliers, daily sales records, and customer messages myself. I am now bringing that same back-office discipline to remote clients as a virtual assistant. Below are recreated samples that show how I work. The data is fictional; the work behavior is real.

Sample Activities

Google Sheets

Inventory and stock tracker

Item, SKU, stock on hand, reorder point, supplier, lead time, and a low-stock flag, with a summary tab that lists what to reorder. Fictional products. I would update it daily and flag low stock before it runs out.

Google Sheets

Vendor and supplier tracker

Supplier (fictional), product, order date, expected date, status, last follow-up, and next action. Keeps every order moving without a single dropped delivery.

Google Sheets

Income and expense tracker

Date, description, category, money in, money out, and a running total, all with fictional figures. Shows clean recordkeeping a client can rely on. Sample only, not real financials.

Google Docs

Customer reply template library

Templates for order confirmation, shipping update, out-of-stock reply, refund or return, and a polite follow-up. Warm and clear, ready to adapt to a client's brand voice.

Google Forms

Order and client intake form

The exact fields needed to take an order or onboard a client without endless back-and-forth. Shows I gather the right information up front.

How Anne built this

  1. Listed everything she did in her shop: stock, suppliers, sales, customer messages.
  2. Matched each one to a VA sample using the translation map in the lesson.
  3. Built each sample in Google Sheets, Docs, or Forms with fictional data.
  4. Added the recreated-sample label to every file.
  5. Assembled it into this one-page Google Site, set to view-only.
