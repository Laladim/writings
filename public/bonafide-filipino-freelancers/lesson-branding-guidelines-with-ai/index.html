Branding Guidelines With AI

Expert-level brand work, made reachable. Research and draft guidelines fast, and prove you understand the brand.

Length: 16 minutes For: anyone who wants to deliver pro-level brand work Updated: 2026-06-24 (v1) Prerequisite: Understand the Business First

"Branding guidelines sound like something only senior experts make. I would not know where to start."

A belief that AI has quietly made false

On my trial task, I created branding guidelines for the client, and they helped me win the job. I am not a branding agency. What made it possible was using a tool. I asked ChatGPT to help me research and draft the brand persona, the writing guidelines, the voice. Work that used to need years of experience became something a prepared beginner could deliver in an afternoon.

The wrong question vs the right question

Wrong question: "Am I senior enough to make branding guidelines?"

Better question: "Can I research a brand well and let a tool help me write it up clearly?"

The first question stops beginners. The second one is a yes for anyone willing to study the brand and use AI to organize what they find.

What goes in simple branding guidelines

SectionWhat it captures
Brand personaWho the brand is if it were a person: their personality and values.
Voice and toneHow they sound: formal or casual, warm or bold, and what words they use or avoid.
AudienceWho they speak to, and that audience's needs and language.
Key messagesThe few things the brand always wants to communicate.
Visual notesTheir colors and look, taken from their existing materials.
Why AI makes this reachable

AI does the heavy organizing; you do the judgment. You gather what the brand says and shows, paste it to the tool, and ask it to draft a persona and voice guide. Then you check it against the real brand and fix what is off. The tool removes the blank-page problem. You stay the one who decides what is true.

How I delivered brand work as a beginner

The client asked for a newsletter, not branding guidelines. I made the guidelines anyway, because I wanted the newsletter to truly fit, and because I wanted the client to see I understood their business. I asked ChatGPT to help me draft the brand persona and writing guidelines after I had studied the brand. It is very easy once you know how to communicate with the tool, which is why I also studied prompt engineering, so I could ask in a way that produced really good results.

The guidelines were not just decoration. They were proof. They told the client, before I wrote a single line of the newsletter, that I had taken their brand seriously. That is what brand work does for you, and AI is what put it within my reach.

The rule that came out of this

Study the brand yourself, then let AI help you write it up. The understanding has to be real and yours. The drafting and organizing can be fast and assisted. That combination lets a beginner deliver work that looks senior.

The Brand Guide Loop

Five steps. This builds directly on understanding the business first, and it is one of the strongest extras in going the extra mile.

StepWhat you doWhy it works
1. Gather the brandCollect their site copy, posts, and any existing materials.You can only guide a brand you have looked at.
2. Ask AI to draftPaste what you gathered and ask for a persona, voice, and key messages.The tool removes the blank page.
3. Check against the real brandCompare the draft to how they actually sound. Fix anything off.Your judgment makes it true, not just neat.
4. Add visual notesPull their real colors and look from their logo and site.Consistency is what reads as professional.
5. Deliver as proofShare the short guide so the client sees you get them.Brand work is undeniable proof of understanding.

One brand guide, built fast

Setup: a small online shop you are doing a trial for.

Gather
You copy their about page, a few posts, and their product descriptions.
Draft with AI
You ask the tool to propose a persona, voice, and three key messages from what you pasted.
Correct
You compare to the real brand and adjust the tone where the draft missed.
Finish and deliver
You add their colors and share a clean one-page guide with your trial work.
What that cost

An afternoon, and a deliverable that looks like an agency made it. The client sees a beginner who works like a pro, because a tool let you punch above your experience.

Practice. Build one brand guide with AI.

  1. Pick a real or practice brand and gather their materials.
  2. Ask AI to draft a persona, voice, and key messages.
  3. Check it against the real brand and fix what is off.
  4. Add their colors and look.
  5. Deliver it as a clean one-page guide.

Audit checklist:

  • Gathered real brand materials first
  • Used AI to draft persona, voice, and messages
  • Corrected the draft against the real brand
  • Added accurate colors and visual notes
  • Delivered a clean, professional one-pager
