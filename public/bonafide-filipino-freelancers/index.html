How BFF Works

No clickbait. No upsells. Just the next concrete step.

Three rules BFF runs on, set by Lala, who started this community in 2017.

Free, forever.

You should never have to pay to learn how to get started. No ₱4,999 "masterclass." No free webinar that turns into a sales pitch. BFF was free in 2017. It is free today. It will be free tomorrow. If anyone asks you to pay for BFF, that's not us.

Built for Filipino learners.

One day a client abroad will ask you, "When can you start?" You will answer from home, with your kids in the next room. Every lesson here builds toward that day. You earn in dollars. You stay in the Philippines.

Finishable in fragments.

Lessons are 15 to 25 minutes. Made for the mom who gets 20 minutes after the kids fall asleep, and the employee studying after her shift. You don't need a free week. You need 20 minutes, kept.
