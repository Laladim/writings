How BFF Works

No clickbait. No upsells. Just the next concrete step.

Three rules BFF runs on, set by Lala, who started this community in 2017.

📚

Free, forever.

No "and then sell them a $497 course" energy. BFF was free in 2017, free today, free tomorrow. If anyone tells you otherwise, that's not us.

🇵🇭

Built for Filipino learners.

Written for Filipino context, in clear English. Faith-warm, not faith-required. PH job market specifically, with listings that change as the job board refreshes. No US-centric "401k" advice.

⏱️

Finishable in fragments.

Lessons are 15-25 minutes. Designed for the mom who has 20 minutes after the kids are asleep. Sustainability beats speed.
