Creative/Artistic Skills

37 creative paths. You do not study all 37. Choose one. Become an expert there.

Length: 12 minutes (to pick) For: Creative Specialists deepening their craft Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"I want to do creative work. But it looks like I need to study every tool: Photoshop, Premiere, After Effects, Figma…"

Common question from new BFF learners

Friend, you do not need everything. The creative industry is vertical-specialized. A photo retoucher does not need After Effects. A video editor does not need Figma. Choose a vertical, then go deep.

The wrong question vs the right question

Wrong question: "What tool should every creative know?"

Better question: "Which vertical do I want to pursue until I can reach a ₱30K-₱100K/month rate?"

The warning about "I'll learn 5 Adobe apps first"

The Adobe-collector trap

You do not need Photoshop + Illustrator + InDesign + Premiere + After Effects + XD all at once. The 5-tool ambition often leads to 0 clients. Choose 2 tools max. Become an expert in them.

Practice. Pick + start. 10 minutes.

  1. Read 8 paths above. Pick ONE based on archetype + budget.
  2. Click LinkedIn Learning path link. Add to "In Progress."
  3. Set 60-day finish-by. Creative paths shorter than business.
  4. Build portfolio piece per week. Mock projects ok. Quality over quantity.

Action items, based on your archetype

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25%

Specialty + adjacent skill. Stack toward premium tier.

Do this week
  1. UX Designer or Motion Graphics. Highest ceiling.
  2. Stack with business skill from Lesson 17. Creative + strategy = senior signal.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30%

Creative not natural fit. Pick supporting skill, not specialty.

Do this week
  1. Skip this lesson unless creative is your wedge.
  2. If needed: Mobile Photography for basic. Low barrier, useful side-skill.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15%

Primary lesson for your archetype.

Do this week
  1. Pick: Video Editor or Motion Graphics. Highest demand 2026.
  2. OR UX Designer if app-savvy. Premium tier.
  3. Build 5 portfolio pieces in target niche. Real estate, F&B, course creators, podcasters.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15%

Creative + ecom = strong combo.

Do this week
  1. Mobile Photography for ecom. Product shots = monetizable.
  2. OR Graphic Designer for brand kits. SMB ecom needs.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10%

Not primary. But basic creative literacy useful.

Do this week
  1. Skip unless creative is your wedge.
  2. If needed: Canva basics. Free, faster than LinkedIn Learning for admin work.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5%

Creative is high-ceiling but high-barrier. Not the first path.

Do this week
  1. Start with Customer Service (Lesson 17), not creative.
  2. Add Canva basics free. Floor-level creative literacy.
