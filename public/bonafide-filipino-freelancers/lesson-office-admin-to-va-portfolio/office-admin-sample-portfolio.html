Admin-to-VA Summary

My background is in office administration and event coordination. I built this portfolio to show how I translate scheduling, documentation, file organization, follow-up, and project tracking into remote VA support.

This sample portfolio uses fictional details and recreated workflows so the work is inspectable without exposing private employer information.

Scheduled meetings

Becomes a client calendar system with prep time, follow-up blocks, invite copy, and scheduling rules.

Prepared meeting notes

Becomes an agenda and action notes template with decisions, owners, due dates, and follow-up email copy.

Coordinated events

Becomes a project or event tracker for tasks, vendors, registrations, assets, blockers, and post-event follow-up.

Organized office files

Becomes a Drive folder system with naming rules, archive logic, and access boundaries.

Transferable Skills

The portfolio is organized around practical VA behaviors: managing time, documenting decisions, tracking work, organizing files, writing follow-ups, collecting complete requests, and building repeatable workflows.

Calendar control

Protects preparation time, follow-up time, meeting details, and client availability.

Meeting documentation

Turns discussions into decisions, action items, owners, due dates, and status notes.

Project tracking

Keeps tasks, dependencies, blockers, deadlines, and next actions visible.

Drive organization

Uses folders, naming rules, archive logic, and access notes so files stay findable.

Email follow-up

Uses reusable templates and escalation rules for professional communication.

Process discipline

Documents recurring admin workflows so the work can be repeated clearly.
