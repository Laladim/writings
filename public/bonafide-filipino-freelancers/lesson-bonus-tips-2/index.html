Article Curation Training

The quiet skill content VAs need: turn a three-hour article search into a 20-minute system.

Length: 25 minutes For: VAs supporting content creators, bloggers, newsletter writers Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"My client wants a weekly newsletter with five curated articles, but it takes me three hours to find good ones."

Common question from new BFF learners

Friend, the problem is not your ability to search. The problem is the lack of a system. Article curation is tool-driven. When the setup is right, the weekly search can take 20 minutes. Today we will build that system together.

The wrong question vs the right question

Wrong question: "Where should I look for articles?"

Better question: "What feed-based system can bring 50 articles a week into my inbox, and how will I choose the best five?"

The 4-tool article curation stack

ToolWhat it doesFree / paid
FeedlyRSS feed reader. Subscribe to 20-50 sources. Daily digest.Free tier (100 sources)
PocketRead-later. Save articles from anywhere. Tag for client.Free
Google AlertsEmail when keyword appears anywhere on the web.Free
Twitter/X ListsCurated voices in your niche. Real-time discovery.Free

Workflow sample (1 weekly newsletter)

20-minute weekly cycle

Mon (10 min)
Feedly digest scan. Save 10 candidates to Pocket.
Wed (5 min)
Google Alerts review. Add 2-3 candidates to Pocket.
Fri (5 min)
Read all Pocket items. Pick top 5. Write 1-sentence summary each.
Result
20 minutes total. 5 curated articles + summaries.

The warning about "too much research"

Don't read every article

Curation is selection, not consumption. Skim the headline and first paragraph. If it takes too long to judge, it is not a keeper. 20 minutes total per week, not 20 minutes per article.

Practice. Setup curation stack. 30 min.

  1. Pick a niche. (Real or hypothetical client.)
  2. Sign up at Feedly. Subscribe to 20 sources in niche.
  3. Install Pocket Chrome extension.
  4. Setup 5 Google Alerts. Niche keywords.
  5. Run 1 weekly cycle. 20 min. Document the top 5 in Google Doc.
