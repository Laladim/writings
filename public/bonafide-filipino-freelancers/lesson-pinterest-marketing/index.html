Pinterest Marketing

It is not a social network. Pinterest is a search engine. That changes the game.

Length: 16 minutes For: SMM specialists supporting blogs, e-com, lifestyle brands Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"My audience does not use Pinterest. Should I skip it?"

Common question from new BFF learners

Friend, it depends on the client's niche. For blogs, recipe sites, e-com, weddings, and home decor, Pinterest is a discovery goldmine. For B2B SaaS, usually not. But when it is relevant, Pinterest can bring long-tail traffic that FB + IG cannot serve.

The wrong question vs the right question

Wrong question: "Which client should use Pinterest?"

Better question: "Which client niche has buyers searching Pinterest for the solution they sell?"

The 4 niches where Pinterest beats IG

NicheWhy Pinterest wins
Food + recipes"Recipe for ___" searches drive Pinterest 24/7. Bookmark behavior.
Wedding + eventsPlanning behavior = bookmark = Pinterest core.
Home decor + DIYInspiration searches drive purchase intent.
Blog + content sitesLong-tail SEO. Pins live 6+ months vs IG 24 hrs.

The 5-step Pinterest strategy

Setup workflow (one-time)

1. Convert to Business
Free upgrade. Unlocks analytics.
2. Claim website
Pinterest tracks pins linking to client site.
3. Build 10 boards
Niche-specific board titles with keywords.
4. Pin 5-10 daily
Mix client's own + curated. Consistency is what the algorithm rewards.
5. Use Tailwind for scheduling
Free tier. Batch monthly.

The warning about "all clients need Pinterest"

The wrong-niche trap

If your client is B2B SaaS, Pinterest is usually a waste. Their audience is not searching "best CRM" on Pinterest. Pinterest works for visual + planning + lifestyle niches. Tell the client clearly.

Practice. Audit if Pinterest fits. 15 min.

  1. Pick a client (or hypothetical). What's their niche?
  2. Search 5 keywords on Pinterest. Type "___ tips" or "best ___."
  3. Count results. >100 results = active niche. <50 = skip.
  4. If active: setup business account. Build 10 boards. Pin 5 per day for 30 days.
  5. Track traffic in Google Analytics. Pinterest referral after 60 days.
