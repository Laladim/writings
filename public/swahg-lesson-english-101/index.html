English Communications 101

"Just like any other language, English can be learned." Yung refresher na bumibigay ng confidence.

Length: 25 minutes For: Anyone whose English self-rating is 3 or below Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Ate, gusto kong mag-apply pero takot ako mag-call sa client. Hindi ako fluent."

Diana, SWAHG group, recurring fear

Friend, do not fret. Just like any other language, English can be learned. Sa BPO industry, 10 days training muna bago project-specific work. Yan ay achievable level. Ikaw rin kaya. Sa lesson na ito, yung basics. Plus the No-Calls track na strategy if hindi mo pa kayang mag-Zoom.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Kailangan ba ako 100% fluent before applying?"

Tama: "Anong English level ang minimum sa Role Category ko, at paano ko siya iniaabot?"

Yung "100% fluent" ay myth. Marami sa client mo, hindi din 100%. Yung kailangan: clear communication. Yung "learning is 70% practice and 30% reading." Today natin sasamahan yung framework.

The honest moment

Yung Liz, isa sa SWAHG success stories, self-rated English 2/5 nung nag-start. Hindi siya "perfect." Pero nag-apply siya sa text-only roles. Tinatanggap. Now after 3 years, comfortable na mag-Zoom. The only person who can limit you is yourself.

Yung warning sa "I'll wait until I'm perfect"

The fluency paralysis

"I'll learn English first, then apply" ay yung 3-year delay reflex. Apply to No Calls (text-only) roles WHILE learning. Yung paid practice ay mas mabilis bumagal ng fear kaysa free YouTube.

Other warning: do not pay anyone for "English fluency courses" na promising $10K/month income. Yung free resources (basicenglishspeaking.com, 7esl.com, YouTube) ay sapat.

Practice. 30-day English challenge. Start today.

  1. Open basicenglishspeaking.com. Read 1 module.
  2. Self-rate communication skills. Speaking ___/10. Writing ___/10. Reading ___/10.
  3. Identify problem area: Writing? Speaking? Pronunciation? Pick ONE.
  4. Daily practice for 30 days: 20 minutes. YouTube pronunciation videos + 7esl.com exercises.
  5. Book a SWAHG practice session. DM admins via FB group.
  6. Day 30 re-rate. Improvement = data. Even +1 point matters.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 Polished

English likely 4-5/5. Maintenance + business vocabulary.

Do this week
  1. Watch Harvard Business Review videos for business vocab.
  2. Practice writing 1 LinkedIn post per week in English.
💼 Transitioner

BPO English = workplace strong. May gaps sa async written.

Do this week
  1. Use Grammarly on every email.
  2. Watch documentaries with English subtitles for natural cadence.
🎨 Creative

Visual-strong but verbal sometimes weak. Build verbal sa client briefs.

Do this week
  1. Practice writing 3-paragraph client briefs in English.
  2. Watch The Futur for creative-industry vocabulary.
🛒 Solo Entrepreneur

Sales English na trained. Now expand sa business comms.

Do this week
  1. Translate yung shop's IG captions sa English.
  2. Practice writing investor-style emails to mock clients.
📋 Generalist

Reading/writing decent. Speaking often weaker.

Do this week
  1. Book 2 SWAHG practice sessions per month.
  2. Watch TED Talks at 1.25x speed for spoken cadence.
🌱 Fresh Starter

Likely lowest self-rating. Apply to No Calls roles while learning.

Do this week
  1. Start basicenglishspeaking.com daily.
  2. Apply to No Calls tab only this month.
  3. Book monthly SWAHG practice session.
