How to Improve a Crappy Cover Letter

Yung cover letter na nag-go-through filter. Hindi yung "Dear Sir/Madam I am applying" template.

Length: 40 minutes For: Anyone who's sent generic cover letters with zero replies Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Yung cover letter ko, "Dear Hiring Manager, I am applying for the position posted." Sabi ng friend ko, weak daw."

Common Freelancing Journey Lesson 4 question

Friend, friend mo tama. Yung "Dear Hiring Manager" + "I am applying" = death pattern. Hindi binabasa. Today natin sasamahan ang anti-formula cover letter na tama yung client mo nakatingin sa loob ng 5 segundo.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong template ko gagamitin?"

Tama: "Anong 1 specific bagay ko sa client niya hindi pa naming-name ng iba?"

The 3-paragraph cover letter structure

ParagraphPurposeLength
1. Specific openingName THEM. "I saw your LinkedIn post about scaling Q3" or "I noticed your new Shopify launch."2-3 sentences
2. Proof + fitCite 1 specific past result. "Increased CSAT from 78% to 91% at [Company]." Then connect to their need.3-4 sentences
3. Specific CTA"Available for 30-min call next Tuesday. Here's my Calendly: [link]." Hindi "looking forward to hearing from you."1-2 sentences

Anti-formula cover letter sample

Before (weak) vs After (strong)

Weak opening
"Dear Hiring Manager, I am applying for the VA position you posted."
Strong opening
"Hi [First Name], I noticed your IG post last week about needing help with batch content for Q4. I've done exactly that for [other client] last quarter."
Weak proof
"I have 3 years experience as VA."
Strong proof
"In my last role, I batched 60 IG posts/month for a fitness brand. Saved client 8 hours/week. Engagement went from 1.2% to 3.4%."
Weak CTA
"Looking forward to hearing from you."
Strong CTA
"I have a 30-min slot open Tuesday 10am Manila time. Calendly: [link]. Or reply with your preferred time."

Yung warning sa over-customization

Don't write a novel

Yung 5-paragraph essay ang opposite trap. Hiring manager has 200 applications. 3 short paragraphs. 150-200 words max. Yung depth, sa interview na.

Practice. Build template + 1 customized. 40 min.

  1. Open Google Docs.
  2. Build 3-paragraph template. Placeholders for client name, specific reference, proof, CTA.
  3. Find 1 real job advert. From SWAHG Job List or OnlineJobs.ph.
  4. Customize template to that 1 client. Specific reference, specific proof, specific CTA.
  5. Submit via ASSIGNMENT TRACKER FORM (Title: Lesson 4).

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 Polished

Senior tier. Lead with social proof + retention stats.

Do this week

  2. Add Loom intro link in CTA.
💼 Transitioner

Translate BPO metrics specifically.

Do this week
  1. Use CSAT %, ticket volume, SLA % as proof.
  2. Use Paddy prompt 23 framing.
🎨 Creative

Lead with portfolio + 1 specific work example.

Do this week
  1. Embed portfolio link in paragraph 1.
  2. Reference 1 specific brand piece in paragraph 2.
🛒 Solo

Cite shop metrics. Revenue, customer count, SKUs.

Do this week
  1. "Operated ₱X/month business" as proof.
  2. Show transferable skills.
📋 Generalist

Wedge-anchored proof.

Do this week
  1. Open with wedge-specific tool name.
  2. Cite 1 specific deliverable type.
🌱 Fresh Starter

No experience. Lead with enthusiasm + learning evidence.

Do this week
  1. Cite 1 free course completed (HubSpot, Google Digital).
  2. "Committed 20 hrs/week to learning [skill]" as proof of activity.
