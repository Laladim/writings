Professional Online Presence

Yung 4 platforms na binibilang ng client bago sila mag-reply sa application mo.

Length: 20 minutes For: Every freelancer past first applications Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"Na-Google ako ng client. Nakita yung lumang blog ko mula college. Nakakahiya."

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, Google search yourself today. Anong nakikita ng client? Yung first 5 results, yan ang reputasyon mo online. Being lazy will not get you anywhere but doing hard work doesn't necessarily mean you will succeed unless you have the right foundation and you have good strategy. Today natin sasamahan yung 4 platforms na shapeable.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong platform ang pinaka-trending?"

Tama: "Anong 4 platforms ang pinag-cclickan ng client bago sila mag-reply?"

Trending is fleeting. Yung 4 na "must-haves" ay 5+ years stable. LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, plus a portfolio surface. Yan ang foundation. Yung TikTok, Threads, BlueSky, optional.

The 4 platforms every Filipino freelancer needs

PlatformWhat it signalsMinimum setup
LinkedIn"Professional presence." Yung first stop ng international clients.Photo + Headline + Summary + 1 work entry
Facebook"Authentic person." PH clients especially check FB. Verify you're real.Clean profile + photo + city + occupation
Instagram"Visual personality." Especially critical for creatives. Optional but valuable.Professional handle + bio + 9-12 posts representing brand

The pro tip

Yung video lesson sa SWAHG ay 2X-able. Yung tip ay: speed up to 2X to finish faster. Still understandable. Yung tip na yan, generalizes: don't watch videos at 1X kung kaya mo 2X.

Example: a healthy 4-platform online presence

Example 4-platform setup

LinkedIn
Professional profile · clear role title · connections built deliberately over years
Facebook
Personal page locked down · public surface limited to work-related content · admin of a community group if you run one
Instagram
Personal-brand handle · selective posting · Stories for ongoing community
Portfolio
Your own URL (writings site / portfolio / case studies). Owned surface, not rented.
The honest moment

A common setup: Facebook personal posts locked to "Friends Only." Public surface is limited to work-related content. You don't have to share everything publicly. Privacy settings yung tools natin.

Yung warning na minsang nag-iiyak ang applicant

The old-photo trap

Yung old photos sa Facebook (anything from your college / early-twenties era), visible pa rin sila even if you don't remember. Most clients FB-stalk before hire. Yung pre-onboarding background check, real. Spend 2 hours cleanup before first major application.

Practice. Audit + cleanup. 60 minutes (one-time).

  1. Google your name + city in incognito browser. What appears in first 10 results?
  2. LinkedIn setup or refresh (15 min):
    • Professional photo (per Lesson 02)
    • Headline matches your archetype + wedge
    • About section: 3-paragraph story from Lesson 14
    • Current role + 1-2 past roles
  3. Facebook cleanup (20 min):
    • Profile photo + cover photo professional or neutral
    • About → Work and Education updated
    • Privacy → set old posts to "Friends Only"
    • Untag yourself from cringe photos (Activity Log)
  4. Instagram (15 min):
    • Decide: personal IG or new professional IG?
    • If professional: handle = your-name-VA or your-name-creator
    • Bio = wedge + location + portfolio link
  5. Portfolio (10 min):
    • Notion public page is fastest. Create at notion.so
    • 3-5 work samples minimum (mock projects okay)
    • Share link in LinkedIn + IG + email signature

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25% · proceed ~80%

All 4 platforms exist. Optimization mode.

Do this week
  1. LinkedIn weekly post. Thought-leadership in your wedge. Build inbound.
  2. Portfolio upgrade. Notion → custom site (Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, or Webflow). Premium signal.
  3. Cross-link all 4 surfaces. Same bio, same link, same brand. Consistency.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30% · proceed ~60%

LinkedIn likely best-set already (BPO industry uses it). Audit IG + portfolio.

Do this week
  1. LinkedIn translate corporate jargon out. "AHT" → "average handle time." "FCR" → "first-call resolution."
  2. Build portfolio of "translated work." Anonymized examples of past BPO work, reframed for remote VA.
  3. Hide BPO-only IG posts if personal account is mixed. Or create new VA-focused IG.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15% · proceed ~70%

Instagram + Portfolio are your primary channels. LinkedIn supporting.

Do this week
  1. IG must be flawless. Grid coherence, bio with portfolio link, Highlights organized.
  2. Portfolio on Behance or Dribbble. Industry-standard for creatives.
  3. LinkedIn = secondary but present. US clients still check LinkedIn even for creatives.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15% · proceed ~55%

Probably has shop IG + personal FB. Add LinkedIn + VA portfolio.

Do this week
  1. Setup LinkedIn from scratch. "Operations + Marketing for E-commerce SMEs." Wedge-driven.
  2. Reuse shop IG as portfolio surface. "I grew this account from 0 to N. I can do the same for you."
  3. Separate personal FB from business FB. Cleanup, don't blend.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10% · proceed ~50%

LinkedIn most important. Portfolio is harder for admin work but doable.

Do this week
  1. LinkedIn polished + wedge-positioned. Match the wedge from Lesson 14.
  2. Portfolio = process templates. Yung SOP, workflow, calendar, etc. that you've built. Visualize.
  3. Instagram optional. Skip if not natural. LinkedIn + Notion portfolio is enough.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5% · proceed ~30%

All 4 needed but lightweight. Don't over-engineer.

Do this week
  1. LinkedIn basic setup. Photo + Headline + Summary + Education + 1 internship/project.
  2. FB privacy lockdown. Old photos = friends only or archived.
  3. Notion portfolio with 3 mock projects. No paid client needed.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek):

  1. 4 links. "Archetype: ___. LinkedIn: [link]. FB cleanup: ✅. IG: [link or N/A]. Portfolio: [link]."
  2. Ask for peer audit. Tag 2 SWAHG members. Yung extra pair of eyes ay catches what you miss.
