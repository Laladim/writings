How to Become a Virtual Professional

The first assignment. Not just watching. You will submit a Google Doc.

Length: 35 minutes (video + assignment) For: Active Freelancing Journey learners Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Why do you want to work from home? And what challenges do you have as you become a successful freelancer?"

Common Freelancing Journey Lesson 1 question

Friend, the question looks simple. But your honest answer gives you clarity for the next 6 months. Not the Pinterest answer. Not the Instagram caption answer. The real answer inside you, the one you write when no one is watching.

The 4-step assignment

#Step
1Watch the lesson video (linked on SWAHG live).
2Create a Google Doc. Answer: "Why do you want to work at home? What are your challenges to become a successful freelancer?"
3Share the document. Permissions = "Anyone with the link."
4Submit via the ASSIGNMENT TRACKER FORM (Title: Lesson 1). See sample answer doc.

The sample answer template

What strong answers include

Why work from home
Specific. Not "freedom." Write something like: "be home with kids ages 5 and 7, save 2 hrs daily commute, restart career after 4-year gap."
Top 3 challenges
Honest. "Confidence in English. No portfolio. Family thinks freelance = unstable."
Strategies to overcome
Specific. "Take SWAHG English 101 in next 30 days. Build mock portfolio in 60 days. Show parents first paycheck."
Length
1-2 pages. Not 10 paragraphs. Not 1 sentence.

The warning about pretending

Be honest

If the answer is only aspirational ("I want freedom and abundance"), it can get screened out. An honest answer with rough edges ("I'm scared but desperate") makes a stronger candidate. Not judgmental. Real.

Practice. Submit Lesson 1 today.

  1. Open Google Docs.
  2. Title: "Lesson 1 - [Your Name] - Why Work From Home + Challenges"
  3. 3 sections: Why + Challenges + Strategies. 1-2 pages.
  4. Share permissions: "Anyone with the link can view."
  5. Submit via ASSIGNMENT TRACKER FORM. Find link sa local Freelancing Journey lesson.

Action items, based on your archetype

🌟 Polished

Reframe: write about why scaling matters now.

Do this week
  1. Why work from home (next stage)? What scaling challenges?
💼 Transitioner

Be honest about BPO burn-out + family time motives.

Do this week
  1. "Why I left BPO." Genuine, not corporate PR.
🎨 Creative

Write about creative freedom + monetization gap.

Do this week
  1. Why creative + freelance combo specifically.
🛒 Solo

Honest about pandemic / business challenges.

Do this week
  1. Frame the shop transition as positive pivot, not loss.
📋 Generalist

Specific about wedge.

Do this week
  1. Mention chosen wedge. Not "I do everything."
🌱 Fresh Starter

Honest about being new. That is a strength.

Do this week
  1. Write about wanting growth from zero.
  2. Be specific about challenges at the beginning.
