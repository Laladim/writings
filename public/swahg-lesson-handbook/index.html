Open University Guidelines

"Follow someone ahead of you. Help someone behind you. Never forget that God is always above you."

Length: 22 minutes For: Every SWAHG member, new or veteran Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"May unwritten rules ba sa SWAHG group? Anong dapat ko alam bago mag-post?"

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, oo. Hindi unwritten kasi nakasulat siya dito. Yung SWAHG ay not just a course. It's a family. May mga values. May 7 things you should not do. May process. Yung lesson na ito, yung guidebook bago ka maglakad sa loob.

Who are we?

Bonafide Filipino Freelancers believe that it is important to study and become an expert in our niche. We do not believe in get rich quick, scammy money making online schemes.

  • We are talented, intelligent and loving people who want to create a meaningful life.
  • We believe EVERYONE HAS A CHANCE. We are equal. We are all learners of life.
  • We aim to be a blessing and be that helpful person who will inspire another person who needs help.
  • We are a family. We promote kindness and generosity.
  • Every day is a training day. We pursue our passion and we don't stop chasing our dreams.
  • We don't fake positivity. Positive power comes from faith and personal inspirations.

Why Become a Bonafide Filipino Freelancer?

We like to see ourselves like a tree. We are meant to grow and to bear fruit.

We are meant to grow in God and be that person God wants us to be (Heavenly purpose). Also, we are meant to bear fruit for the benefit of others, whether a client or someone who needs us (Earthly purpose).

When we live in sync with what God wants and the responsibilities we have here on earth, we believe that is an "unwasted life." Our success or happiness is not directly proportional to our income. Money will always come as we genuinely serve people.

7 things you SHOULD NOT do in this group

Violation = warning, then potential removal.

#Rule
1No one should collect money from you. The Open University is free. Some external tools (LinkedIn Learning, SkillShare) may charge, but no person should collect except Lala / SWAHG Team for the MasterClasses and BFF Blueprint.
2No selling of anything inside the group without Lala's permission. Quality check first.
3No sending of self-promo links to group chats unless approved by admins.
4No rude speeches and no swearing.
5No imposing of faith or religion. Express what you believe but never condemn another's belief.
6No borrowing of money inside the group (except when offered willingly as financial help).
7No handling of special cases. For professional, medical, legal counsel: those follow at your own risk. SWAHG team is not responsible for loss or damage.

What is Open University?

The FREE vehicle to learn how to earn money online as a Freelancer. Yung 12 features:

  • Open Accessibility. All aspiring Bonafide Filipino Freelancers welcome, regardless of background.
  • Freedom of Time. Study whenever you want (except MasterClasses which require attendance).
  • Freedom of Pace. Learners study at their own pace, make their own decisions about learning path.
  • Open Program. Create your own curriculum using the Curriculum Tracker or the interactive SWAHG Curriculum builder.
  • Comprehensive Course Resources. Free + paid (LinkedIn Learning etc.), all surfaced in one BFF Blueprint PDF.
  • Weekly Interactive Learning Sessions. Every Monday 2PM on Work At Home Geek FB page.
  • Peer Practice + Self-paced Lessons. Use the SWAHG FB group for peer mock interviews and Freelancing Journey assignment swaps.
  • Tools Familiarization Platform. Software tutorials.
  • Bonafide Freelancer Success Stories. Interviews with members who landed clients via the community.
  • Personality + Relationships Breakthroughs. Celebrate wins of all sizes.
  • Spiritual Breakthroughs. Faith-warm. Skip or embrace.
  • Virtual Meet & Greet Party. Optional video calls with members.
Paid surface

Yung Open University ay free. Yung SMM Master Class ay paid-special class na ginawa ni Lala for specialized SMM learning.

Practice. New-member onboarding. 30 minutes.

  1. Join the SWAHG FB group (Work At Home Geek) if hindi pa.
  2. Like the Work At Home Geek FB page for Monday 2PM sessions.
  3. Read 7 rules above twice. Yung "no selling without permission" especially.
  4. Open Curriculum Tracker OR the interactive SWAHG Curriculum builder.
  5. Build your 90-day curriculum. Pick 5 lessons from this site to complete.
  6. Post an introduction in the FB group: name, archetype, 90-day goal. Yung community welcome.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 Polished

Veteran. Mentor newer members. Reciprocity.

Do this week
  1. Comment on 3 new-member intros per week.
  2. Apply for SWAHG Internship as mentor.
💼 Transitioner

Read rules carefully. Avoid corporate language in posts.

Do this week
  1. Read rule #4 (no rude speeches). BPO sarcasm doesn't translate.
  2. Post intro mentioning BPO years + transition goal.
🎨 Creative

Share work selectively. Rule #2 about selling.

Do this week
  1. Share creative work as "feedback request," not "buy mine."
  2. Pair with personal brand from Lesson 14.
🛒 Solo Entrepreneur

Rule #2 critical for you. No promoting shop or self-services.

Do this week
  1. Re-read rule #2.
  2. Frame past business as "learning resource," not "buy from me."
📋 Generalist

Methodical. Build the curriculum properly.

Do this week
  1. Use Curriculum Tracker.
  2. Plan 90 days. Block calendar.
🌱 Fresh Starter

Welcome home. Take time to read everything.

Do this week
  1. Join FB group + page.
  2. Post intro. Mention "Fresh Starter, here to learn."
  3. Attend next Monday 2PM session.
