Online Jobs Account

Yung profile na hindi makakaaway ng client. Step-by-step. Tapos lang sa 30 minutes.

Length: 20 minutes For: Everyone who needs to setup OnlineJobs.ph properly Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"May OnlineJobs.ph profile na ako. 6 months. Walang reply pa rin."

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Tignan natin yung profile mo. The first step to get a home-based job is to create a profile that clients will never reject. Hindi sapat na may account ka. Yung profile mo, dapat tumama sa filters ng employer. Yung kung ano yung mga details na pinag-cclickan ng client, doon ka mag-focus.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong category dapat ko pumili?"

Tama: "Anong impression ang ibibigay ng profile ko sa unang 5 segundo ng client view?"

Yung OnlineJobs.ph ay PH-native job platform. Yung "OnlineJobs" tab sa SWAHG Job List ay may 5+ live listings sa anumang araw. Pero kung yung profile mo ay incomplete, hindi ka makikita sa employer search. Today natin tatapusin yung 9-section profile build.

The 9 sections of a strong OnlineJobs.ph profile

#SectionWhat to put
1Profile photoClear headshot. Plain background. Smile or neutral. Hindi selfie sa beach.
2Full nameReal name. First + Last. Hindi nickname.
3Skill summary (3 lines)"Customer Support VA with 4 years BPO experience. Bilingual EN/TL. Available night shift PST/EST." Specific.
4Skills (4-8 selected)Hindi 30 skills. Yung 4-8 best, work-used. Use the auto-fill from your Resume Builder Step 2.
5Cover letter (uploadable)1-page PDF. Templated but customizable. Per-application customization expected.
6Resume (PDF, ATS-friendly)Export from SWAHG Resume Builder. No tables, no columns, single-column format.
7Salary expectationHindi "negotiable." Range like "₱25K - ₱35K/month" or hourly "$5-$8/hour." Filter for serious leads.
8Availability"Full Time, 40 hrs/week" OR "Part Time, 20 hrs/week, PST overlap." Specific.
9Verified ID + ID badgeUpload PH government ID for verification. Yung badge that comes after, signal of trust.
The verification advantage

ID-verified profiles get 3-5x more views than unverified. Hindi expensive. Hindi takdang oras. Submit it Day 1.

Yung mga winning profile signals (from real hiring data)

What hiring managers filter by

English proficiency rating
Self-rated. Honest about it. 3 stars > 5 stars na hindi totoo.
Last login date
Active in last 7 days = answered quickly. Inactive = passed over.
Test scores
Yung OnlineJobs.ph aptitude tests are free. Take 3-5 in your target niche.
Hours overlap
"Will work 9pm-5am Manila for US client" is signal. Surface explicitly.
The honest moment

Common pattern: a beginner OnlineJobs.ph profile with no photo, no specific skill summary, generic resume. Zero replies for 2 months. Once all 9 sections get fixed, views climb and a first interview lands within ~3 weeks.

Yung warning na ginagawang lalo na ng Fresh Starters

The "premium profile" pressure

OnlineJobs.ph may free at paid plans. Free plan ay enough para makapag-apply. Yung paid plans ay para sa employer-side, hindi candidates. Pag may "upgrade to premium" prompt ka, ignore for v1. Pag may unang client na, doon mo isipin upgrade.

Other warning: do not pay anyone to setup your OnlineJobs.ph profile. May mga scammer na nagsasabi "I'll get you a job for ₱5,000." That's not how this works. SWAHG ay free. Yung OnlineJobs setup ay free. Walang dapat babayaran.

Practice. Setup the whole profile, 30 minutes.

  1. Sign up at OnlineJobs.ph using the professional Gmail from Lesson 02.
  2. Upload profile photo. Plain background. Recent (within 1 year). Cropped to face + shoulders.
  3. Write 3-line skill summary. Use format: "[Specialty] with [N] years [context]. [Tools]. [Availability]."
  4. Pick 4-8 skills. From SWAHG Resume Builder Step 2. Skills rated Confident+ qualify.
  5. Upload resume PDF. Export from Resume Builder. Single column, ATS-friendly.
  6. Set salary expectation. Range. Specific. Per Lesson 07, your path determines floor.
  7. Take 3 aptitude tests. English + your target specialty + 1 transferable skill.
  8. Upload ID for verification. Government-issued PH ID. Wait 24-48 hrs for badge.
  9. Mark availability: "Full Time" or "Part Time." Add time-zone overlap explicit.
  10. Apply to first 3 jobs TODAY. Hindi bukas. Yung application muscle, build muna habang fresh ang profile.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25% · proceed ~80%

Already on OnlineJobs.ph. This lesson is about optimization.

Do this week
  1. Update profile photo if >1 year old. Yung "fresh look" matters.
  2. Refresh skill summary to current specialty. 3-year-old summary may not reflect current wedge.
  3. Add salary expectation in USD if targeting international. Signals senior + clear.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30% · proceed ~60%

OnlineJobs.ph ay PRIMARY platform. BPO experience translates directly.

Do this week
  1. Lead summary with BPO years + CSAT %. "Customer Support · 6 years BPO · 92% CSAT · Bilingual."
  2. Highlight night-shift availability. US-based clients filter heavy on this. PST/EST overlap = goldmine.
  3. Take Customer Service test on OnlineJobs.ph. Score should be 80%+ given your background.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15% · proceed ~70%

OnlineJobs.ph is secondary. Upwork + portfolio direct outreach are primary.

Do this week
  1. Still setup OnlineJobs.ph as secondary. Some Filipino agencies hire creatives there. Don't skip.
  2. Link portfolio in summary. "See work at [URL]" should be the most clickable item.
  3. Set higher salary expectation. Creatives undervalue on OnlineJobs.ph. Don't be the floor.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15% · proceed ~55%

"E-commerce VA" or "Operations VA for SMEs" is the niche.

Do this week
  1. Lead with business experience. "Operations + Marketing · ran a [type] business for [N] years · now VA-ing for similar SMEs."
  2. Apply to e-commerce / Shopify / Amazon FBA listings. Familiar territory = faster ramp.
  3. Mention tax + BIR knowledge. PH clients love VAs who understand local compliance.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10% · proceed ~50%

Primary platform. Pick a wedge, apply systematically.

Do this week
  1. Wedge in title. "Bookkeeping VA · QuickBooks + Xero." Specific.
  2. Apply 5 jobs/day in your wedge. Volume + targeting.
  3. Track applications in Trello (Lesson 05). 30-day target: 150 applications.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5% · proceed ~30%

First job platform. Important to do it right.

Do this week
  1. Complete ALL 9 sections. Don't skip. Incomplete profile = 0 views.
  2. Apply only to Newbie Friendly + No Calls filters. Don't reach for senior roles yet.
  3. Set realistic salary: ₱15K-₱20K/month start. Yung "₱50K" expectation as Fresh Starter, hindi makakapasa filter.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek):

  1. OnlineJobs.ph profile screenshot. Hide email but show photo, summary, skills. Tag: "Archetype: ____. Verified: yes/pending."
  2. Day 0 stats. "Profile completeness: ___%. Tests taken: ____. Applications sent today: ____."
