10 Secret Skills

Hindi Photoshop. Hindi Excel. Yung 10 skills na hinahanap ng client at hindi kayang i-Google.

Length: 20 minutes For: Anyone wondering "anong skill ang dapat ko i-build first?" Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 lesson

"Anong skills ang pinakahanap-hanap ng client? Yung Canva ba? Or Photoshop?"

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, hindi yung tool ang scarcity. Lahat naman pwede mag-Canva. Yung scarcity ay yung skill sa likod ng tool: yung pag-iisip, pag-organize, pag-communicate. Yung 10 secret skills na 'to, hindi nakasulat sa resume mo as bullet points. Pero pag andiyan, ang client mo, hihindi mawalang sa iyo.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong software ang dapat ko aralin?"

Tama: "Anong meta-skill ang makakapag-promote sa akin sa kahit anong client, kahit anong industry?"

Here are the 10 important skills that will help you achieve success sa Freelancing Industry. Hindi pareho sa skill list ng resume. Yung 10 na ito ay yung foundation. Pag wala ito, kahit pinakamagaling kang Canva user, magbabalik ka sa applications.

The 10 Secret Skills

#SkillWhy it's secret (most candidates skip it)
1Reading instructions carefullyYung 30-40% ng SWAHG candidates ay nare-reject kasi hindi nasunod yung application instructions. Free, but rare.
2Async written communicationHindi text speak. Clear, full sentences, in either English o malinaw na Taglish. Pang-email at pang-Slack.
3Time tracking discipline2% ng community gumagamit. Yung discipline ng "mag-Toggl start before any task" ay competitive advantage.
4Self-managementWalang nag-bantay. Ikaw mismo magse-set ng deadlines, magse-self-check, mag-prioritize. Skill yan.
5Asking smart questionsHindi "ano gagawin ko?" Pero "based sa brief, may dalawang interpretation ako: A or B. Which path?"
6Receiving feedback gracefully"Yung Filipino pride" can become a career-blocker. Practice mo yung "thank you, I'll revise" muscle.
7Documentation habitEvery task, may notes. Every meeting, may summary. Yung "I'll remember" doesn't scale to 3 clients.
8ResourcefulnessGoogle before asking. YouTube tutorial before quitting. Yung "ginalaw ko muna, tinanong ko later" yan ang signal.
9Confidentiality disciplineHindi pwedeng "kuya, anong project mo?" Yung client info, walang kasama sa SWAHG group chat.
10Long-game thinkingHindi "biglaang yaman." Yung 3-year client retention, 5-year skill stack. Patience as a skill.
The kicker

Lahat ng 10 ay free to learn. Walang course needed. Pero kailangan ng deliberate practice. Yung daily reflection: "Did I do these today?" yan ang difference between aspirant at professional.

Example: a 10-skill audit (Year 1 vs experienced freelancer)

Sa Year 1, expect low scores. Yung tinatawag nating "secret skill" ngayon, kadalasan hindi alam ng baguhan dati. The point isn't to compare yourself to anyone, the point is to see which skills move with deliberate practice.

Example scorecard pattern (Year 1 vs experienced freelancer)

1. Reading instructions
Year 1: ~4/10 (skim mode) · Experienced: 9/10 (re-read 2x before reply)
2. Async writing
Year 1: ~5/10 · Experienced: 9/10 (after years of email + Slack practice)
3. Time tracking
Year 1: ~0/10 · Experienced: 8/10 (a tool like Toggl, consistent for years)
5. Smart questions
Year 1: ~3/10 (asked everything) · Experienced: 8/10 (think first)
7. Documentation
Year 1: ~2/10 (memory based) · Experienced: 9/10 (Notion + Sheets)
10. Long-game
Year 1: ~5/10 (wanted fast results) · Experienced: 9/10 (multi-year plans)
The honest moment

Walang skill na inborn. Lahat ay built over time. Yung Year 1 mo, kahit mababa pa ang scores, ok lang. Yung lesson ay yung ALAM mo na kailangan i-build sila. Awareness ay 50% ng progress.

Yung warning na ginagawang mistake ng karamihan

The "I'll learn tools first" trap

Mas masakit pag magaling ka sa Adobe Premiere pero hindi mo masagot ng tama yung "anong delivery date?" email. Yung 10 secret skills ay prerequisite, hindi nice-to-have. Pag wala ito, yung tool skills mo, irrelevant.

Practice. Self-audit. 25 minutes.

  1. Score each of the 10 skills 1-10 honestly. Be brutal. Sa Year 1 ko, yung average score ko ay 4.5.
  2. Identify your 3 lowest. Yan ang priority for next 30 days.
  3. For each of the 3 low scores, write ONE habit you'll build:
    • Low Reading Instructions → "Read every job post twice before applying"
    • Low Documentation → "End every work session with 2-bullet summary"
    • Low Time Tracking → "Start Toggl before every task for 30 days"
  4. Setup a habit tracker. Use a simple Google Sheet: daily checkmarks for 30 days. Or paper checklist taped on wall.
  5. Re-score in 30 days. Calendar a recurring monthly self-audit. Yung muscle ay grows or atrophies, hindi static.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25% · proceed ~80%

High scores on most. Lesson is about leveling up the remaining 2.

Do this week
  1. Score honestly. Probably 8+ on most, but 1-2 weaker. Identify those.
  2. Coach a Fresh Starter on YOUR weakest skill. Teaching is the fastest way to level up.
  3. Lock in "Receiving feedback gracefully" practice. Yung pride na nasa senior level, biggest risk. Stay coachable.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30% · proceed ~60%

BPO trained on most. Focus on async + self-management.

Do this week
  1. Async writing > phone discipline. Yung muscle of "explaining via email" ay different. Practice 1 long email per day.
  2. Self-management without supervisor. Yung daily standup ng BPO, gone. Recreate via Trello + Calendar self-checks.
  3. Smart questions > escalation. Hindi escalate sa supervisor. Solve first, ask later.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15% · proceed ~70%

Tool-deep, often process-shallow. Documentation + feedback are common gaps.

Do this week
  1. Build documentation for every project. Brief + revisions notes + final version log. Hindi just memory.
  2. Receive feedback without defending. Yung "but the artistic vision" reflex, suppress. Hear first, defend later (or not).
  3. Confidentiality discipline. Hindi mo i-share sa IG yung WIP ng client project. NDAs are real.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15% · proceed ~55%

Self-management strong. Async writing + smart questions are typical gaps.

Do this week
  1. Async writing. Yung "I'll just call" reflex ng business owner, hindi works sa async clients. Practice 3-paragraph updates.
  2. Smart questions, not boss-style directives. Yung "ito gawin mo" tone na ginamit mo sa staff, hindi sa client.
  3. Long-game thinking applied to client retention. Hindi just sales-focused. Yung "client retention 18 months" thinking.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10% · proceed ~50%

Strong on Documentation + Self-management. Weak on Resourcefulness.

Do this week
  1. Resourcefulness training. Before asking client "how do I do X?", spend 15 minutes Googling + YouTube. Most answers are public.
  2. Smart questions over comprehensive questions. Hindi 5 questions sa email. Pick THE one that unblocks you.
  3. Long-game positioning. Yung wedge mo, dapat consistent across 3 years. Resist niche-hopping.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5% · proceed ~30%

Scores probably low across the board. That's normal. Foundation time.

Do this week
  1. Pick TOP 3 skills only. Reading Instructions, Async Writing, Self-Management. Yan muna.
  2. Use SWAHG application process as practice. Read every SWAHG instruction carefully. Reply async-style.
  3. Daily journaling habit. 5 minutes EOD writing: "Did I read instructions today? Did I document? Did I track time?" Yung audit muscle.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek):

  1. Your honest 10-skill scorecard. Lowest 3 highlighted. Tag: "Archetype: ____. Building habits for: [skill A, B, C]."
  2. Re-post sa 30 days. Before vs after scores. Public accountability.
