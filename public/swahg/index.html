How SWAHG Works

No clickbait. No upsells. Just the next concrete step.

Three rules SWAHG runs on — set by Shela, the SWAHG founder, in 2019 and unchanged since.

📚

Free, forever.

No "and then sell them a $497 course" energy. SWAHG was free in 2019, free today, free tomorrow. If anyone tells you otherwise, that's not us.

🇵🇭

Built for Pinoys.

Tagalog and Taglish welcome. Faith-warm, not faith-required. PH job market specifically: 26 current listings from the last 30 days. No US-centric "401k" advice.

⏱️

Finishable in fragments.

Lessons are 15–25 minutes. Designed for the mom who has 20 minutes after the kids are asleep. Sustainability beats speed.
