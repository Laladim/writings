Employee to Freelancer

Hindi mo kailangan mag-resign tomorrow. Yung "jump" ay isang plano, hindi isang gabi-gabing decision.

Length: 18 minutes For: Anyone currently employed considering full freelance Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"Ate, ayaw ko na sa office. Nag-resign na ako. Anong next?"

Ronel, SWAHG group, second time asking

Friend, isama natin yung pag-bilis at pag-stabilize. Although we are Freelancers and it is the best job in our own opinions, hindi ko irrecommend na jump straight away. Yung case ko ay different. Yung situation mo ay different. Yung pag-resign muna bago may client, parang tumalon sa pool na hindi alam kung may tubig.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Kailan ako pwede mag-resign?"

Tama: "Anong income level at risk profile ang dapat ko kayang i-shoulder bago ako mag-resign?"

Yung tanong ng "kailan" ay anxiety. Yung tanong ng "anong baseline" ay strategy. Yung Freelancing Journey is a marathon, hindi sprint. Pag-prepare ka ng tama, mas matagal ka tatakbo nang hindi nasusugatan.

The 5 conditions before you resign

ConditionWhy it mattersTarget
1. First freelance incomeProof of concept. Hindi ka magrresign dahil "I think I can do it." Show me yung first ₱5,000 sa bank.First paying client landed
2. Emergency fund3-6 months ng expenses sa savings. Pag dumating yung dry spell, hindi ka magpa-panic.3-6× monthly expenses
3. Retainer client minimumHindi project lang. May matatag na monthly income. ₱20K-₱30K minimum monthly retainer.1 retainer @ ₱20K+/month
4. Health insurance planEmployer-provided HMO maglalaho. Mag-bili ka ng personal HMO o ipon-pondo for emergency.Personal HMO arranged
5. Family alignmentPareho kayo ng spouse / parents. Pag may panic, may support system.Family conversation completed
The bridge phase

For 3-6 months, magka-tabi ang office job mo at first freelance client. Yes, masakit. Yes, pagod. Pero yung bridge phase na yan, ay yung kalokohan ng pinaka-secured transition. Hindi mo kakailangan ng "first month freelance panic" kasi may stable income pa.

The honest moment

Nag-resign ako pagkatapos ng 13 months ng dual-life. Akala ko mabilis na 'yon. Para sa iba, 18-24 months pa. Walang "perfect" timeline. Yung perfect timeline ay yung sayong kayang i-shoulder nang hindi mo isinasapanganib ang pamilya mo.

Yung warning na ginagawa ng SWAHG group every quarter

The "resigned, now what?" panic

Every 3 months, may bagong member sa SWAHG na nag-post ng "I resigned today, anong next?" Yan ay yung worst position. Walang client, walang savings, may bills pa rin. Yung pressure mag-bagsak ng rates, magsign sa exploitative contracts, mag-rush ng portfolio. Resign with a plan, hindi with a feeling.

Practice. Build your resignation calculator. 20 minutes.

  1. Open a Google Sheets. Title: "My Resignation Math."
  2. Calculate monthly expenses: rent + utilities + food + transport + family support + savings target. Sum.
  3. Calculate 6-month emergency fund target. Monthly expenses × 6 = your safety floor.
  4. Calculate current savings. Bank balance + emergency stash. Gap = how much more to save.
  5. Calculate freelance-income breakeven. Monthly expenses + tax allowance (30% buffer) = minimum freelance income before resignation.
  6. Set your "Resign Decision Day." When freelance income ≥ breakeven for 3 consecutive months AND emergency fund met = green light. Mark calendar.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25% · proceed ~80%

Probably already resigned. This lesson is about scaling protection.

Do this week
  1. Setup 12-month emergency fund. Freelancers need bigger buffer than employees. Aim for 12× monthly expenses long-term.
  2. Get HMO + life insurance. Cobra / employer-provided HMO is gone. Personal coverage critical. Check Maxicare or Medicard individual plans.
  3. Diversify client base. Hindi pwede 80% income from 1 client. Aim for no client > 30% of revenue.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30% · proceed ~60%

Highest stakes. Job stability versus freelance dream.

Do this week
  1. DO NOT resign yet. Side-hustle for minimum 6 months while employed. Yung "I want to quit" feeling, hindi enough.
  2. Document your work for portfolio. Before resignation, screenshot everything (anonymized). Yung achievements, ihanap mo ng paraan i-mention sa portfolio.
  3. Inform 1 trusted friend at work. Hindi sa HR. Sa kaibigan. Yung emotional support sa transition, valuable.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15% · proceed ~70%

Portfolio is the safety net. Build it strong before jumping.

Do this week
  1. 10-piece portfolio before resignation. Mock projects okay, but published / shareable.
  2. Secure 1 retainer creative client first. Hindi project-only freelance. May monthly base.
  3. Setup tax tracking from Day 1. Use Taxumo for PH freelancer tax filing. Pag may regular income na, mag-register sa BIR.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15% · proceed ~55%

Already self-employed. "Resignation" means closing/scaling shop.

Do this week
  1. Decide: close shop or run both? Pareho pwede. Pero kailangan clarity.
  2. If closing: orderly wind-down. Sell inventory, close vendor accounts, file final BIR returns. Hindi sudden.
  3. If running both: time-block aggressively. Mondays + Wednesdays = shop. Tuesdays + Thursdays = freelance. Fridays = both / catch-up.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10% · proceed ~50%

Steady employee. Transition needs methodical plan.

Do this week
  1. Pick a wedge BEFORE resigning. Hindi "I'll figure it out." Decide: Bookkeeping VA, Calendar EA, etc.
  2. Apply to 5 part-time VA roles this month. Test market interest before fully committing.
  3. Setup emergency fund equal to 6 months of expenses. Generalist Admins lose to specialists on speed-to-first-client. Buffer longer.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5% · proceed ~30%

No employment to resign from. This lesson is preview for later.

Do this week
  1. Skip this lesson's action items. Bumalik ulit kapag may employment ka.
  2. Use the bridge time well. Walang office job? Yung 8 hrs/day, ipasok mo sa SWAHG learning + applications.
  3. First client first. Resign-and-quit drama, hindi yet. Focus on the basics.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group (Work At Home Geek):

  1. Your resignation math. "Monthly expenses: ₱___. Target emergency fund: ₱___. Current savings: ₱___. Gap: ₱___. Estimated resign date: [month/year]."
  2. OR if not employed: skip and tag your past-self in 12 months. "I will return to this lesson when I have an office job to leave."
