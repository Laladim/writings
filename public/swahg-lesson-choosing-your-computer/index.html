Choosing Your Computer

"Kwento muna ako." Paano pumili ng kompyuter na hindi mabibitin sa unang client mo.

Length: 18 minutes For: Every SWAHG learner before Box 2 Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2) Replaces: 2019 video lesson

"Ate Lala, sale daw sa Lazada. Eight thousand lang yung laptop. Pang-VA na ba 'yon?"

Annie, SWAHG Facebook group, last week

Ganyan din ako noon. Bibili ng pinakamura kasi takot ako masayang yung pera. Pero may natutunan ako: a bad computer may hurt your career growth in freelancing. Hindi sa kasi mahal yung magandang computer. Kasi yung mabagal na computer, naghihintay ka habang naghihintay ang client mo. At yung client, may option na hindi ka na pasahuran ulit next month.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Magkano yung pinakamurang laptop?"

Tama: "Anong computer ang sasabay sa akin sa unang tatlong client, hindi mabibitin sa loob ng tatlong taon, at hindi gigipitin ang badyet ng pamilya ko ngayon?"

Yung unang tanong, magbibigay ng laptop. Yung pangalawang tanong, magbibigay ng career foundation. Lahat ng box sa Blueprint nakasalalay dito.

The 6 criteria of a good freelancer computer

This is the checklist I use kapag may humihingi ng advice sa SWAHG group. Hindi specs ang sinasabi ko, kung anong job dapat kayang gawin ng machine mo.

WhatWhy it mattersMinimum to ship
RAM (memory)Kung kaunti, mag-fr-freeze pag nag-zoom ka habang nakabukas Chrome at Canva8 GB minimum, 16 GB target

Battery lifePag brown-out, dapat may oras ka pa para sabihan yung client6+ hours real use

Practical truth

You don't need the most expensive one. You need one that can run for a long period of time doing what your future client will ask. The cheapest laptop sa Lazada usually fails on RAM + SSD. A refurbished 2-year-old machine na may 16 GB RAM almost always beats a brand-new ₱20K na may 4 GB.

Ano ang gamit ko ngayon

Honestly, gusto kong ipakita sa inyo na hindi ako nagpapakitang "perfect setup." May limitations din yung sa akin, at gagawin ko yung honest math kasama kayo.

The honest moment

Pumili ako ng 16 GB sa MBP kasi badyet noon. Pero pag tumatakbo na yung Claude Code + Chrome + 30 browser tabs, naha-hit ko yung 16 GB ceiling. Nag-swap na siya, bumagal. Kung kayang abutin yung 32 GB, abutin. Especially for Apple Silicon kasi hindi mo na ma-uupgrade later.

Yung warning na minsang nakalimutan kong sabihin

Hindi ito drama

Pag bumili ka ng walang-RAM na laptop kasi mura, ang mangyayari: tatanggap ka ng interview. Mag-zoom. Mag-freeze yung kompyuter. Mag-cut yung audio. Tatawagin ka mamayang gabi ng client to say "let's go with someone else, mas stable yung setup nila." Hindi yung skill mo ang umurong. Yung tools mo. That's the consequence we don't talk about enough.

Hindi ko sinasabing pera ang pinakaimportante. Sinasabi ko: kung makakatipid ka ng ₱5,000 ngayon at mawawalan ka ng ₱30,000/month na client dahil dito, hindi tipid 'yon. Yon ay expensive economy.

Practice. Gawin natin ngayon, hindi bukas.

Buksan mo yung kompyuter mo na ngayong ginagamit. Sundan natin yung 5-step audit. 12 minuto lang.

  1. Check RAM. Mac: Apple menu → About This Mac. Windows: Settings → System → About. Tandaan: anong dami ng RAM mo?
  2. Check storage. Mac: About This Mac → Storage. Windows: Settings → Storage. Magkano ang libre?
  3. Check chip / processor. Same About screen. May "Intel Core i3" ba? "Apple M1"? "Ryzen 5"?
  4. Plug out the charger. Time your battery. Habang nag-bbrowse ka ng OnlineJobs.ph. Pag bumaba ang battery ng 50% in less than 2 hours, mahina na siya.
  5. Open a video call to yourself. Buksan mo yung Google Meet. Tignan: gumagana ba mic? Webcam? Mukha ka bang dilim sa silhouette ng madilim na ilaw?

The audit form: Isulat sa notebook (oo, papel):

  • My RAM: ____ GB
  • My storage: ____ GB free of ____ GB total
  • My processor: ____
  • My battery: lasted ____ minutes out of unplugged
  • My webcam: ☐ works ☐ blurry ☐ broken
  • My mic: ☐ clear ☐ hissy ☐ broken

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

Hindi pareho ang journey natin. Iba ang baseline ng Polished Freelancer kaysa sa Fresh Starter. Sa Resume Builder, may quiz na nag-rroute sayo sa archetype mo. Kung hindi mo pa alam, kunin yung Resume Builder quiz muna, balik ka dito.

Heto ang action items per archetype para sa lesson na 'to:

🌟 The Polished Freelancer ~25% · proceed ~80%

Already running your own client roster. Multi-platform. Articulates pricing without flinching.

Do this week
  1. Audit your current machine against client-load math. Kung 3+ active clients ka na, dapat 16 GB RAM minimum. Pag 8 GB pa, mag-upgrade na within 6 months.
  2. Add a backup machine. Mag-bili ng refurb na ChromeBook o lumang iMac mula sa Swappa as failover. Pag nasira yung primary, hindi ka mawawalan ng kita.
  3. Pre-claim the upgrade. Ilagay sa Google Sheets budget mo: "Q3 2026, 32 GB MacBook upgrade, paid from retainer #3." Make it a business expense, hindi gastos.
Recommended pick: Refurbished Apple Certified Refurbished 14" MacBook Pro M3 16 GB · 1 TB · ~₱90-110K. Pay-from-client-money, ipinanunood sa books mo.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner ~30% · proceed ~60%

BPO English plus office discipline. Strong from years of phone work. Needs the freelancing bridge.

Do this week
  1. Don't quit your job to buy a computer. Tapusin mo muna yung notice period. Yung 13th month + leave conversion mo, doon ka magbili.
  2. Mid-range Windows or refurbished MacBook Air. Hindi na kailangan top-tier. 16 GB Windows laptop sa Lenovo ThinkPad refurbished line, ~₱35-45K, will last 4 years.
  3. Verify your home internet first. Run Speedtest. If under 25 Mbps, get a backup mobile hotspot before you buy the laptop. The internet failure rate is 45% in the SWAHG candidate pool.
Recommended pick: Lenovo ThinkPad T-series refurb 16 GB · 512 GB SSD · i5, OR MacBook Air M2 16 GB · 256 GB if budget allows.
🎨 The Creative Specialist ~15% · proceed ~70%

Photoshop, Premiere, video editing. Portfolio is your wedge. Admin tooling thin.

Do this week
  1. Your machine must run Adobe Creative Cloud + CapCut without choking. 16 GB RAM ay floor, hindi target. 32 GB if you do 4K video. 8 GB is not enough, period.
  2. External SSD non-negotiable. Add a SanDisk Extreme Portable SSD 1 TB (~₱5,500). Mag-iiwan ka ng project files doon, hindi sa internal drive.
  3. Color-accurate display matters. If you do photo or video work, mag-calibrate via DisplayCAL at babasahin sa macOS Display settings kung anong color profile. Sirang colors = unprofessional render.
Recommended pick: MacBook Pro 14" M3 16 GB · 512 GB (₱115-130K) OR refurbished M1 Max 32 GB · 1 TB from OWC.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur ~15% · proceed ~55%

Ran your own ecom or store. Self-taught Canva + Shopify + Mailchimp. Often a mom with scheduling considerations.

Do this week
  1. Treat it as a business expense. Itago mo yung resibo, isama sa income tax filing. The laptop is depreciable equipment, hindi personal spending.
  2. Pick something the kids cannot break. Hindi MacBook Air sa coffee table. Itago sa locker o sa loob ng cabinet pag hindi ginagamit. ChromeBook is the budget-friendly bullet-proof option.
  3. Add scheduling tools to it day one. Install Google Calendar, Trello, Toggl Track sa unang araw. Yung business-running skill mo, gamitin para sa client work.
Recommended pick: Acer ChromeBook Spin 8 GB · 128 GB (~₱20-25K) for kid-proof daily, OR ASUS VivoBook 16 GB if budget supports.
📋 The Generalist Admin ~10% · proceed ~50%

Excel-fluent, calendar-disciplined. No specialty wedge yet.

Do this week
  1. Match the machine to a wedge. Bookkeeping VA needs QuickBooks compatibility (Windows easier). Inbox-Zero VA needs reliable webcam + mic. Pick the wedge BEFORE the laptop.
  2. Mid-range Windows is fine. Hindi mo kailangan ng MacBook. Dell Inspiron 16 GB · 512 GB ay enough for 95% of admin VA work.
  3. Add a second monitor. ₱8K monitor mula Shopee ay productivity multiplier. Pwede side-by-side: client email left, your work right.
Recommended pick: Dell Inspiron 15 16 GB · 512 GB i5 (~₱40-50K) OR refurbished ThinkPad E-series.
🌱 The Fresh Starter ~5% · proceed ~30%

Eager but unprepared. 0–2 tools. Weak English self-rating. Confidence gap is the bottleneck.

Do this week
  1. Hindi mo kailangan magbili kaagad. Bago ka maglabas ng ₱30K, kumpletuhin mo muna yung Blueprint stages 1-2. Pag committed ka na sa skill, doon ka magbili.
  2. Borrow first if you can. Hiramin ang laptop ng sister o cousin for the first 30 days. Test mo kung talagang gusto mo yung freelance work. Pag matanggap ka sa OnlineJobs.ph at first client, gamitin yung first paycheck para sa upgrade.
  3. If you must buy: 0% installment lang. Hindi cash-out. Check Home Credit o Lazada Wallet 0% sa 6 months. Yung first 3 client payments mo, doon mag-iincome para sa amortization.
Recommended pick: Refurbished Acer Aspire 8 GB · 256 GB (~₱18-25K) for first 12 months. Upgrade to 16 GB machine after Client #2.
Universal rule

Lahat ng archetype: internet speed + backup ay equally important as the laptop. 45% ng SWAHG candidate pool ay sumosobra sa 10 Mbps internet. Pag may 16 GB MacBook ka pero 5 Mbps internet, hindi pa rin tatanggapin yung Zoom. Test via Speedtest.net at i-include sa resume mo.

Checkpoint. Proof na nagamit mo yung lesson.

Postable artifact

I-post mo sa SWAHG Facebook Group ang isa sa dalawa:

  1. The audit photo. Litrato ng papel mo na may filled-out checklist. "Eto yung audit ko. RAM: 8 GB. Susunod na step ko: ____."
  2. The decision post. "Archetype ko: _____. Pick na pinili ko: _____. Bibili ako sa _____ next month, after natapos ko ang Blueprint Box 2."

Yung mga ka-batch mo, mag-iiwan ng feedback. Yan ang community ladder, hindi solo flight.
