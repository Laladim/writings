Freelancing Journey: Introduction

Yung 6-lesson fast-track series. Tapusin lahat. Earn free 1-on-1 coaching with Lala.

Length: 12 minutes For: Anyone committing to the structured fast-track Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Sabi mo may free 1-on-1 coaching. Paano makukuha?"

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, hindi gimmick. Tapusin mo yung Freelancing Journey 6 lessons + assignments, dadalhan kita ng 1-on-1 coaching free. Hindi sa "registration." Hindi sa pay. Sa completion. Yung kayang tumapos, kayang i-coach.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong promise sa training?"

Tama: "Anong 6 specific deliverables ko sa loob ng 6-8 weeks?"

The reward

Tapusin all 6 + submit assignments via the ASSIGNMENT TRACKER FORM. Earn 30-minute 1-on-1 coaching with Lala. Free. First-come-first-serve.

Practice. Start now.

  1. Watch the introduction video. Live link via SWAHG site.
  2. List 3 challenges you currently experience. Honest.
  3. List 3 strategies you'll use to overcome them.
  4. Commit to 6-8 week completion timeline. Calendar.
  5. Open Lesson 1 today. Hindi tomorrow. Today.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 Polished

Already past most of this. Skip unless coaching is the goal.

Do this week
  1. Skip if not coaching-seeking.
  2. If yes: complete for free 1-on-1.
💼 Transitioner

Structured roadmap = familiar territory.

Do this week
  1. Run all 6 lessons. Treat as 8-week training.
🎨 Creative

Lesson 3 (resume) ay critical. Lesson 5/6 = portfolio gold.

Do this week
  1. Start Lesson 1 today.
🛒 Solo

Translates business skills into VA frame.

Do this week
  1. Complete all 6. Free coaching is high-value.
📋 Generalist

Forces you to pick wedge (Lesson 2). Highest leverage.

Do this week
  1. Start today.
  2. Treat Lesson 2 (personality + career path) as wedge decision.
🌱 Fresh Starter

PERFECT for you. Yung 6-lesson scaffold = exactly what you need.

Do this week
  1. Drop everything. Start Lesson 1 today.
  2. Aim for 6-week completion.
