SMM Handbook

"A product of love, a gift from God." Free SMM resource guide ng SWAHG.

Length: 20 minutes (overview) For: Anyone in SMM path or considering it Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"Ate, may sinabi yung handbook na may templates. Saan ko ma-access?"

Mariel, SWAHG group, common question

Friend, yung SMM Handbook ay product of love. A gift from God. Full of knowledge that will guide you to successful SMM campaigns. Yan ang sabi ko sa intro. Hindi ako exaggerating. Iniluto ko siya sa loob ng 2 years of running my own SMM career. Yung lahat ng nasa loob, alam mo na bago ka makipag-meet sa first client.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Mahal ba yung Handbook?"

Tama: "Anong section sa Handbook ang pinaka-relevant sa current stage ko?"

Free yung Handbook. Pero hindi ito spam download. Yung password, kunin mo via DM sa Work At Home Geek FB page. Yung intentional access ay built-in commitment device.

Yung warning sa "I'll read it later"

Download = read = apply

Marami sa SWAHG ay download lang. Hindi binabasa. Yung Handbook ay action-oriented. Bawat section may exercise. Pag walang exercise output, walang learning happened.

Practice. Get + apply 3 sections. 60 minutes total.

  1. DM Work At Home Geek page for password.
  2. Download the SMM Handbook.
  3. Open Section 4 (6-Step Process of Creating Content). Apply to ONE post.
  4. Open Section 7 (10 Ways of Finding Your Client). Pick 3 ways. Try them this week.
  5. Open Section 16 (Branding Templates). Apply ONE template to your own brand.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 Polished

Use as SOP source. Build agency processes.

Do this week
  1. Build client onboarding SOP using Sections 9 + 14 + 16.
  2. Use Section 12 as deliverable quality gate.
💼 Transitioner

Familiar with SOPs from BPO. Apply same discipline.

Do this week
  1. Read Sections 3, 4, 9 in order.
  2. Apply Section 4 to your own LinkedIn.
🎨 Creative

Visual mastery + strategy = premium tier.

Do this week
  1. Read Sections 5, 14, 16.
  2. Apply Section 16 templates to your brand portfolio.
🛒 Solo Entrepreneur

Reuse your business sense + handbook framework.

Do this week
  1. Read Section 7 (10 Ways of Finding Client). Pick 3.
  2. Apply Section 4 to shop social media first.
📋 Generalist

Convert Handbook into operational templates.

Do this week
  1. Build Notion templates of Sections 4, 12, 16.
  2. Use as client onboarding system.
🌱 Fresh Starter

Start with Sections 3, 4, 7. Foundation.

Do this week
  1. Read Section 3 (Am I a Newbie?). Honest answers.
  2. Apply Section 4 to your own IG/FB. Practice first.
