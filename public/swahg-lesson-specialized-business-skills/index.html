Specialized Business Skills

Yung 38 specialty tracks ng LinkedIn Learning. Pick ONE. Master it sa loob ng 90 days.

Length: 15 minutes (to pick) For: Past first client, ready to specialize Updated: 2026-05-15 (v2)

"May free LinkedIn Learning ako thru PH public library. Anong path kunin?"

Common question from new SWAHG learners

Friend, may 38 specialized business skill paths sa Career Path Training. Marami silang options, pero yan ay overwhelm trap. Hindi 38 ang aralin mo. Isa lang. Yung tamang isa, fit sa archetype mo at sa target Role Category. Today natin nila-narrow ang choice.

The wrong question vs the right question

Mali: "Anong path ang pinaka-trending?"

Tama: "Anong path ang nagpapasok sa target Role Category ko sa SWAHG Job List?"

Yung Career Path Training sa SWAHG ay free na resource library. Yung mga LinkedIn Learning paths ay PH library access via LinkedIn Learning or paid (~$30/month). Yung 90-day specialization, beats yung 3-year "I'm exploring" loop.

The honest moment

Almost no one is a one-and-done specialist. Most start as generalist, narrow into one lane, then deepen. Yung specialization ay evolves. Sayang ang oras na "What if I picked wrong?" stress. Pumili, gawin 90 days, tignan ano nagustuhan. Iterate.

Yung warning sa course collectors

The "I'm still studying" trap

Marami sa SWAHG ay 4-5 unfinished LinkedIn Learning paths. Yung 5 half-done > 1 finished, hindi totoo. Pick one. Finish it. Add to resume. Apply to jobs with that path's role. Tapos pwede next.

Practice. Pick ONE path. 12 minutes.

  1. Filter the 7 highest-leverage paths above by archetype block below.
  2. Click ONE LinkedIn Learning path link. Read course descriptions.
  3. Set 90-day finish-by date. Add to Google Calendar.
  4. Block 5 hrs/week for course. Same time every day.
  5. Apply to 5 jobs in that Role Category this week. Study + apply parallel.

Action items, depende sa archetype mo

🌟 The Polished Freelancer~25%

Vertical-deep already. Build adjacent skill OR senior-level cert.

Do this week
  1. Become a Senior Manager. Yung step up.
  2. OR Program Manager. Cross-project orchestration.
  3. OR Six Sigma Black Belt. Operations specialty.
💼 The Corporate Transitioner~30%

BPO foundation. Build remote-specific specialty.

Do this week
  1. Customer Service Specialist. Direct translation.
  2. OR Customer Service Manager. Leadership path.
  3. OR Administrative Professional. EA route.
🎨 The Creative Specialist~15%

See Creative/Artistic Skills (Lesson 18). But add a business skill too.

Do this week
  1. Social Media Marketer. Marries creative + strategy.
  2. OR Content Strategist. Premium tier.
  3. OR Digital Advertising Specialist. Monetizable.
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur~15%

Operations + marketing fluency. Pick a wedge to deepen.

Do this week
  1. Business Operations Associate. Familiar terrain.
  2. OR Online Marketing Manager. E-com adjacent.
  3. OR Bookkeeper. If you ran BIR books already.
📋 The Generalist Admin~10%

Critical: pick a wedge. End the "everything VA" loop.

Do this week
  1. Bookkeeper. Highest monetization.
  2. OR Project Coordinator. Generalist → PM.
  3. OR Excel Skills. Multiplier for everything.
🌱 The Fresh Starter~5%

Pick the highest-job-count path. Maximize match probability.

Do this week
  1. Customer Service Specialist. 24% of jobs.
  2. OR Soft Skills. If anxious about communication.
  3. Don't pick more than one path simultaneously. Yan ang trap.
