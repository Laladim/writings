# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Filipino freelancers and aspiring remote workers who need to scan current job
opportunities quickly, judge fit, and move from discovery to a direct
application. Many are beginners, career transitioners, or people comparing
roles on a laptop or phone with limited time and attention.

## Product Purpose

The BFF job board turns verified public job postings into a dependable,
beginner-friendly decision tool. Success means a visitor can understand the
role, eligibility, schedule, requirements, and next action without decoding a
wall of source text.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, and beginner-friendly. The interface should feel supportive
without becoming childish, and professional without becoming corporate or
intimidating.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense job-board layouts, tiny text, long unbroken descriptions, unclear
application paths, exaggerated urgency, and decorative UI that competes with
the job information.

## Design Principles

1. Make every job easy to scan before asking the reader to commit attention.
2. Use the same information order for every role so comparisons feel natural.
3. Separate verified posting facts from BFF interpretation and fit guidance.
4. Keep direct application actions obvious and secondary tools supportive.
5. Preserve the calm Writings by Lala visual language across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text sizes, comfortable line lengths, clear heading hierarchy,
keyboard-visible controls, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and plain
language suitable for readers with different levels of remote-work experience.
