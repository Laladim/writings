I remember a time when I thought love was earned.

If I performed well, I would be loved. If I did badly, the love would be withdrawn. I carried that idea for a long time. It made me anxious every day. It made me exhausted around the people I most wanted to be near.

The day I let go of that idea, my life changed.

The love you receive from the people around you is not a direct reflection of how great or bad you are. It is a reflection of who they are.

When we give real love and real compassion, it is like a small flame that can light up hundreds of candles.

When the inspiration runs out because work is heavy, because life is heavy, because the body is heavy, look up. There are bright smiles. There is love. From the people who carry the light you once showed them.

Many are looking for what is real. Many are fooled.

But when you find them, life will never be the same.

Because you know that whatever happens, even if they are not perfect, they are always there.

You have hands to hold when you are in pain.

If the love you have been receiving has not felt deserved, you are not the only one who has felt this. None of us deserve it. That is what makes it love.

The love of the Lord is the same. We do not receive grace and love because we are worthy of it. We receive it because the Lord is merciful.

We have a great and loving God.

Like a mama and papa who do not get tired of loving you, even when you are difficult, even when they are worn out, that is what He is.

Love is an infinite power God gave us.

The more we share it, the more we see the meaning of life God has given us.

It is not perfect, but it is our best.

Tonight, before you sleep, look at the love you have received this week. Do not measure whether you earned it. Just receive it.

— Lala
