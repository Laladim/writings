This is personal.

It is scary to go public and tell people that I am a rare-disease survivor.

I had hereditary spherocytosis since I was a child.
I no longer have a spleen and a gall bladder.
My spine is weak because of degenerative disc disease.
My brain is prone to bleed because of cavernoma.
My immune system is always at risk.
I get sick easily.

There are days when the fear is loud.

"This will be your whole story."

"Your body will always limit you."

"Maybe you cannot build anything meaningful."

If your body has ever made you feel small, I know that feeling.

I know what it is like to look strong outside and still feel fragile inside.

But these sicknesses do not define me.

They are part of my story.
They are not my Savior.

Christ is.

I am a survivor.
I have been doing freelancing since 2015.
I teach people how to start working at home for free.
I teach the steps to grow into a Social Media Manager.

There is still life even if you are sick.

There is joy even in your pain.

There are ways to earn outside the corporate world.

God never makes a mistake.

Our pain has a purpose.

Our suffering is meant to show who God is, not how strong we are.

When everyone has left you.

When everything has fallen apart.

God is the only rescue you need.

The beauty of this vast universe shows that human beings are nothing but dust.
A helpless creature apart from God.

That is not meant to crush us.
It is meant to correct the posture of our hearts.

We are small.
God is not.

We are weak.
Christ is enough.

My mission is to bring hope.
Not the hope that says, "Look within yourself."

The hope that comes from God alone.

If you are carrying sickness today, do not let pain write your whole name.
Bring your weak body to Christ.
Receive the grace for today.
Then take the next small step.

Let us keep moving on.

For the glory of God.

\- Lala
