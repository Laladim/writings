This is personal.

It is scary to go public and tell people that I am a rare-disease survivor.

I had hereditary spherocytosis since I was a child. I no longer have a spleen and a gall bladder. My spine is weak because of degenerative disc disease. My brain is prone to bleed because of cavernoma. My immune system is always at risk. I get sick easily.

These sicknesses do not define me. I am a survivor and doing freelancing since 2015. I teach people how to start working at home for free, and the steps to grow into a Social Media Manager.

There is still life even if you are sick.

There is joy even in your pain.

There are ways to earn outside the corporate world.

God never makes a mistake.

Our pain has a purpose.

Our suffering is meant to show who God is.

When everyone has left you.

When everything has fallen apart.

God is the only rescue you need.

The beauty of this vast universe shows that human beings are nothing but a dust. A helpless creature apart from God.

My mission is to bring hope. The hope that lies not within ourselves, but from God alone.

Let us keep moving on. We can do this.

— Lala
