A reader asked me, "How will I ever get experience if no one hires beginners?"

It is one of the most common questions inside the community. And I was exactly like this when I was starting out.

Here is how I built my own portfolio.

I started with what I knew. Chat support. I was promoted to Customer Support Manager. But I did not grow my skills there, so when I was let go, I was back to zero.

I applied as a VA. My role was admin tasks. Research. Sending emails. Power point. Replying to messages. Whatever the client needed.

The client told me to learn new things that would help his business. So I learned video editing in Adobe Premiere. I studied social media marketing. I studied Amazon. I studied WordPress.

I watched my client grow his half-million followers.

He bought me training materials so I could learn and apply them in his business.

We had good results.

We are now in our seventh year together.

A lot of people think buying a course will get them a high-paying job.

No.

If you did not learn the actual thing, you will always be lacking.

There are many ways to build a portfolio in freelancing. Two proven ones.

1. Start low. Climb to the harder roles.

2. Study. Apply what you studied through internship. Build the portfolio from real work.

A lot of people miss the most important element. Actual training.

That is why you have no confidence. You know you have not done the thing you only studied.

A certificate is not enough.

Reading about how to swim is not the same as being ready for big waves.

You cannot fake clients. Maybe for a little while. But sooner or later, you will be found out if you do not know what you are doing.

The best freelancers are givers. They give value to the business. They are not takers.

Takers cannot deliver what they say they can deliver.

Freelancers are hired because they are expected to solve problems. They are expected to add value. Not to add problems.

I always say, pick a battle you can win.

There is no shortcut.

Do the right and difficult things today. Then tomorrow will get easier.

Do the wrong and easy things today. Then tomorrow will get harder.

So I say, do things right.

I pass the love on today. If a beginner is reading this and waiting for someone to hire them, send them this. They are not behind. They are still being built.

People with a warm love always win.

— Lala
