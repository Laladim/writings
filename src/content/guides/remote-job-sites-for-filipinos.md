When I started, I used OnlineJobs.ph and nothing else.

Most clients I met there were low ballers. I almost believed that was the ceiling. It was just the view from one window.

The voice in my head kept asking, *"Maybe this is just my rate."*

If you have been opening the same job site every day, I have been there.

The answer is not to abandon one useful site. The answer is to add more windows.

This guide brings those windows together.

## The count

We researched **107 unique remote-work sites and platform identities**.

Here is how that number was reached:

- The BFF source registry contained 105 research rows.
- Eight of our eleven newest reviews were already in that registry.
- Career Vault, Jobgether, and Remote Rocketship added three new sites.
- Support Shepherd and Somewhere are the same company, so they count once.
- The result is 107 unique platforms, not 108 duplicated rows.

Of those 107:

- **79 are current places people can browse for jobs, clients, projects, teaching work, or remote-work opportunities.**
- **28 are closed, duplicated, not really job boards, or unsuitable as dependable job-search sources.** They remain in the guide so you know why we do not count them as active job sites.

The original registry was audited on July 24, 2026. Every link used below was probed again on August 27, 2026. Some sites block automated checks but still open in a normal browser. A working website is not proof that every listing is safe, current, free, remote, or open to Filipinos.

## Read this before you apply

A remote label is not enough.

For every job, check five things:

1. The exact listing says applicants in the Philippines may apply.
2. The role is remote, not hybrid or onsite.
3. The original employer or applicant-tracking page is still open.
4. The employer does not ask candidates to pay an application or placement fee.
5. The publication date belongs to the employer listing, not only to an aggregator that refreshed it.

Our permission labels also need one explanation. A site can be useful for a person to browse while still being unavailable for BFF to copy, store, or republish automatically. **Manual only** does not mean the site is bad. It means we use the site as a lead, then verify the original opening ourselves.

## A simple way to use this resource

Do not open 79 tabs.

Choose three places from different groups:

1. One Philippines-focused platform or staffing company.
2. One broad remote-job board.
3. One specialist site for your strongest skill.

Search the same job title on all three. Save only roles that clearly accept applicants from the Philippines. Then open the employer's original page before applying.

You do not dig the well when you are thirsty. Build your search system before you feel desperate.

And remember this. A job board is a tool. It is not your provider. Christ does not ask you to pretend or panic. Search faithfully. Tell the truth about your skills. Apply where you can truly serve. Let the result rest with God.

Tomorrow, choose your three sites. Search one role. Verify one real opening. Send one honest application.

It is not about perfection. It is about direction.
