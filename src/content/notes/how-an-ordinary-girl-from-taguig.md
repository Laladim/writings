How did an ordinary girl from a humble place in Taguig build a platform with thousands of followers in the noisy world of social media?

There are thousands of pages and groups in the hot market of "work at home" and "freelancing" that have huge followers and are run by expensive content marketing.

The answer.

Genuine content and kindness.

Everyone can get knowledge in just a few clicks on Google.

But sharing genuine life experience and sincerely caring for your audience can make a huge difference.

There is no secret recipe.

But nothing beats the authenticity of her character and her mission.

— Lala
