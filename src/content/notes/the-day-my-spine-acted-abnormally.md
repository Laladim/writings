For two days, my spine decided to act abnormally.

Severe nerve pain in my left leg. I could not stand. I could not walk.

Today the pain is not as bad. I am thankful and happy.

It is okay if you are in bed today, and there is nothing to do but wait to get better. Receive the love of the people around you while you are helpless. Tomorrow, the day after, you will be out there again. Then you will have even more genuine love for them.

Some days you feel like you can rule the world. Some days you feel like a loser.

That is normal.

I want to share this.

If our world has different seasons, it is okay if sometimes you cannot get up. Because of the rain, the trees bear more fruit. Your tears carry a deep meaning.

Thank you for the prayers.

I will see you when I can.

— Lala
