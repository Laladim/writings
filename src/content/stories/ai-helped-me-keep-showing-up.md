Last year my mom was diagnosed with cancer.

She refused chemo. She chose palliative care. She is in a small house far from me. I cannot move her. I cannot heal her. I cannot keep showing up at the hospital. My body is already a battlefield.

In the same season, I had a job I was not qualified for. A B2B SaaS company in alternative lending. A field I knew nothing about when I started. My body would not let me work forty hours a week. My back would not hold up. My pain would not negotiate.

The voice in my head said:

"You cannot heal your mother. You cannot heal your body. You cannot keep this job. You are about to lose three things at once."

If your hands are full of things you cannot fix, I have been there.

I want to tell you something honest about this season. AI helped me. I am going to say that with no apology and no embarrassment. AI helped me get a job I was unqualified for. AI helped me build a Top 10 newsletter. AI helped me manage my health. AI helped me understand medical documents written in a language I did not speak. AI helped me automate my work from forty hours to fifteen.

That margin is what let me call my mom. That margin is what let me lie down when my pain decided. That margin is what let me sit in a doctor's room with AJ. On a Friday in February. Without losing my job. The tool was not the answer. The tool was the provision.

God's kindness is tangible. It comes in the form of tools. It comes in the form of provision. It comes in the form of time margin when your body and life circumstances offer none.

There are two versions of this story I have learned to tell. One is for believers. It is about God's goodness in a season of suffering. The other is for people who do not believe. It is about how an AI tool let me keep my job. They are the same story told from two angles. God provides through the tools of His age.

If you are carrying things you cannot fix, name them. Then look at what God has put in your hands today. Use it. Not because the tool is the answer. Because today the tool is the provision. The next provision comes when it comes.

For the glory of God.

— Lala
