Last Valentine's Day, I sat in a doctor's office.

AJ was beside me. The chart was on the screen. The doctor said the words Stage 4 deep infiltrating endometriosis. He said adenomyosis. He said no cure. He said pain management only.

I have been sick since I was a child. Hereditary Spherocytosis. Yellow skin and yellow eyes for twenty years. A spleen removed. A gallbladder removed. A tumor in a vein in my brain. A spine sixty years old inside a thirty-year-old body. I thought my body had given me every diagnosis it had.

The voice in my head said:

"Is it not enough that You made my skin yellow since I was a child? Is it not enough that my side has always hurt? Is it not enough that every year I get blood transfusions? Why this too?"

If you have heard a diagnosis you cannot fix, I have been there.

My body is a battlefield. That is the language I have for it now. Some bodies are gardens. Mine is a battlefield. Pain wakes me up. Pain decides what hours I can work. Pain decides if I can stand long enough to cook rice.

I went home that night and I cried. AJ held me. He did not say anything. He has been beside me since the cardboard. The charity ward. 2010. He knows there are seasons when there are no words.

God did not take away my endometriosis. I want to say that out loud. He did not. I prayed. AJ prayed. The community prayed. The diagnosis is still on the chart.

But here is what God did do. He has been stunningly faithful in providing what we needed to survive. The job I was not qualified for. The AI tools. Fifteen hours of work instead of forty. The community that has not left. The husband who has not left. The Word that has not left. The body that is a battlefield is also a stage for divine glory. God is most glorified in me when I am most satisfied in Him. Not when I am healed. When I am satisfied.

If you are reading this and the chart in your hand has a diagnosis you cannot fix, I want you to know this. The chart does not have the final word. God is not done writing.

Tonight, before you read the chart again, put your hand on it. Tell God what is true. Then tell Him what you trust. Then sleep. The next provision comes when it comes.

For the glory of God.

— Lala
