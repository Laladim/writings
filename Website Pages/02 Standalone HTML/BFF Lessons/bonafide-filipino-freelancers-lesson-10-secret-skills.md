The new rule

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
The BFF tools are a sequence, not a menu. At a restaurant you pick one dish and skip the rest. These tools are steps in a line: the map points to the quiz, the quiz points to the curriculum, the curriculum points to the resume builder, the resume builder points to the job board. You run them in order, once, and then the line loops as you grow.

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
"Which tool is the best one to use?" This treats the tools like a menu and keeps you frozen.

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
"What order do I use them in?" The tools are a loop. Run them in sequence and each one hands you to the next.

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Below is the whole loop. Each tool has a job and one small proof to build. Make the proof. That is how the tool becomes useful instead of just open in a tab.

Three ways people fall back into the freeze

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
Avoid these

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Hoarding tabs.

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
Open one tool, finish its small proof, then move on. Six open tabs is the freeze wearing a disguise.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
Skipping the quiz.

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
If you skip the Archetype Finder, the Curriculum and Resume Builder lose your path and you are guessing again.

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
Applying before you have proof.

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Saving one matched job is calm. Spraying applications with an empty resume is the panic that burns people out.

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Practice. Run the loop once, today, with your real situation.
