<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
"I post 3x/week on the client's IG. But it has been stuck at 800 followers for 6 months."

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Common question from new BFF learners

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
Friend, the posting cadence is not the real problem.

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
There is no strategy.

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
IG is an ecosystem now: Feed + Reels + Stories + DMs + Hashtags + Engagement. If you only work in one lane, growth freezes. Today we will walk through the 5-pillar approach.

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
"How many posts per week should I publish?"

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"What content mix across the 5 IG pillars builds both community and sales for my client?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
The 5 IG content pillars (2026)

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
Pillar

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
What it does

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
Cadence (per week)

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
1. Reels

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
Discovery + reach. Algorithm favors them in 2026.

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
3-5 Reels/week

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
2. Carousels

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Education + saves. High engagement, info-dense.

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
2-3 carousels/week

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
3. Stories

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Daily engagement. Behind-scenes. Polls + Q

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
5-7 stories/week

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
4. DMs + Replies

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Community + sales. Algorithm boosts engaged accounts.

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
15 min/day minimum

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
5. Single image posts

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Brand consistency. Quote / product highlight.

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
1-2/week max

The warning about "viral chase"

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Stop chasing viral

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
One viral Reel without infrastructure = 0 conversion.

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Steady consistency (5 Reels/week for 6 months) > random viral attempt.

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
The algorithm rewards reliability.
