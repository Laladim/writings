The 30-Day Hired Plan

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
One AI service. One proof video. One aimed pitch a day. One hour a day, thirty days.

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
30 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
Beginners with one free hour a day

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
2026-06-08 (v1)

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
Pairs with:

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
How to Master Tools Using AI

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
"I want to be a VA. I have no experience. No proof. I have one free hour a day. Where do I start?"

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
The question we hear most from Fresh Starters

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
You do not need to wait until you feel like a complete VA. If you have one free hour a day, you need a small proof project, a simple service, and a daily pitch rhythm. This plan turns the question "where do I start?" into 30 days of visible work.

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
Stop asking the wrong question

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
"Which course should I finish before I can apply?"

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"What proof can I build in one week, and which twenty businesses will see it?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Tutorials produce nothing a client can see. This plan is different.

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
Proof first, not study first.

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
One service. One recorded work sample. One aimed pitch a day for thirty days.

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
What the data says

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
The number

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
What it means

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
641 applications

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
Inexperience did not kill them. Empty claims did. The candidates who moved on had proof.

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
249 job postings

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
One week in May 2026. The boring basics win. Spreadsheets were in 64.7% of them. Inboxes. Calendars. Follow-ups. The market buys operations.

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
~3x pay spread

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
A specialist makes three times more than a generalist. The market pays for focus.

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
77% and 96% gaps

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
77% of the community cannot prove project management. 96% cannot prove email marketing. These gaps are doors.

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
~30% advance

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
About 5% of applicants are Fresh Starters. About 30% of them proceed. An honest beginner with evidence does well.

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
The bet: inbox rescue

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
AI-assisted inbox and calendar management.

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Every business owner is behind on email. This is an entry-level task, and they consider beginners for it. AI makes a careful beginner credible, fast. Focus on one service for thirty days, not ten tools.

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Provable depth beats a shallow claim.

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
The flood

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
A relative of mine was job hunting. Qualified. Hardworking. Shy. He got nowhere. Job boards are a flood. Hundreds of postings, most of them junk: wrong fits, stale listings, scams. His generic applications vanished into it. They got silence.

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
So I built a system. It pulls postings from four public sources into one sheet and filters them. One day in May 2026, it found

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
264 postings

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
. After filtering,

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
58 survived

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
. Most postings were noise before a human even looked.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
The rule

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
What survives the filter is proof.

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Both sides filter. A spray-and-pray application is invisible, because it lands in a flood. You do not need my system. You need its lesson:

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
one targeted pitch a day, with proof attached, beats fifty templates a week.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
The Loop: how you work

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
This is the skill under the service. Four steps. Do them in order.

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Step

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
What you do

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Why it works

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
1. Context

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Know the client. Read the whole thread. Find the outcome needed. Get two or three of the client's old emails for voice.

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
Good context makes good drafts. Bad context makes robots.

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
2. Prompt

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Write one clear ask. Delegate it to the AI like you would to an assistant. Set a word limit.

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
You are not chatting. You are commanding.

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
3. Edit

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
Cut filler words. Kill phrases no human says. Read it aloud. It should sound like the client on a good day.

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
Two minutes of editing is the difference. AI-obvious writing gets you fired.

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
4. Verify

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
Check every name. Every date. Every number. Every link. Check them yourself.

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
AI lies, and it lies with confidence. Done is not what the AI says. Done is what you can see working.

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
What clients buy

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
Clients do not pay for prompts. They pay for a clean inbox and zero mistakes.

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
AI makes the draft. You own every word.

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
Put that line in your pitch.

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
Week one: build the proof

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
You will build a simulation. Label it honestly as one. Seven days, one hour a day.

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
The Spec Inbox Rescue

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
Day 1 · The fake client

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
Use AI to invent a business owner: their profile, stress points, and voice. Pick a business you understand. Work one hour, then stop.

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
Day 2 · The messy inbox

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
Generate 15 realistic emails. Scheduling, anger, invoices, a double-booking, a vendor, two spam, and one trap email a VA must never answer alone. Email the batch to yourself so the inbox feels real, or keep it in one doc.

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
Day 3 · The dry run

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
Process five emails through the Loop. No recording. Set your labels: Reply Today, Waiting On, Owner Decision, Archive. Slow and ugly is the assignment today.

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
Day 4 · The rescue

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
Set a 60-minute timer. Turn on your screen recorder. Triage the inbox. Draft the replies. Fix the calendar. Escalate the trap email, do not answer it. Stop when the timer rings. No laptop? Your phone has a built-in screen recorder. If day four goes badly, redo it tomorrow. One redo is normal.

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
Day 5 · The video

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
Cut it to three minutes. Thirty seconds of chaos, two minutes of you working, thirty seconds of a clean inbox. Trim it in your phone's gallery editor or CapCut if you have no laptop. Talk over it plainly. Clarity wins.

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
Day 6 · The one-pager

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
Write one page: "How I Run Your Inbox." Explain your labels, your process, your escalation rule. Add one line: "I use AI to draft, but I stay accountable for every word." This is your leave-behind and your interview script.

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Day 7 · The pitch

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
Two sentences and a link. "Hi [name], I noticed [a problem I can see from outside]. I am a VA who manages inboxes with an AI-assisted system. Here is a 3-minute video of how I work." Let the video do the talking.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
The rule of honesty

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
Label your video a demonstration. Do not pretend it is real client work. That lie sends you straight to the rejected pile, and 641 applications taught us exactly what happens there.

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
Honesty is the asset.

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Days 8 to 30: the daily pitch

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
The proof is built. Now you spend it. One hour a day, the same shape.

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
The block

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
What happens in it

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
40 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
Find one real business. Name a problem you can see. Send the pitch. Attach the video.

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
20 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
Practice. Have AI generate three messy emails. Run them through the Loop. Stay sharp.

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Track your work

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
in one spreadsheet: Date, Business, Problem, Response. It proves you did the work on the days you feel like quitting.

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
The rules of engagement

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
Speed:

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
reply the same day. Your response time is your audition.

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
The trial:

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
if they ask for a test, charge a flat rate for a trial week, never free. Not sure your number is sane? Post it in the group first.

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
The adjustment:

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
if ten pitches fail, change the business type or the problem. Never change the service.

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
Day 30

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
You will have one work sample, more than 20 tracked pitches, and one real conversation. If you have this and no client yet:

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
the system is working. Continue. Do not rebuild.

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
Where to look

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
Start close. Pitch the places you buy from. Pitch Facebook pages with reviews complaining nobody answers their messages. They are bleeding. Point at the blood. You are not inventing a problem; you are naming one they already have.

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
When you use job boards, use both lanes. The

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
BFF Job Board

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
pulls from live sources and removes stale postings after 30 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
OnlineJobs.ph

<!-- wbl-text:0130 -->
covers PH-native listings. This service ladders into the Admin / VA, Customer Service, and Account Management roles, so filter with those in mind.

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
Avoid the traps

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
Verify identity.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
Make sure the person works where they say they work.

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
No hidden costs.

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Never pay to work. No real client charges you up front.

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
Watch the guarantees.

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
Real jobs describe work. Scams promise guaranteed money.

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
Protect data.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
Never paste a real client's emails into a public AI without permission.

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
Stay calm.

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
The first reply is dangerous because you are eager. Run your checks. If it feels wrong, walk away.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
Practice. Week one starts today, not Monday.
