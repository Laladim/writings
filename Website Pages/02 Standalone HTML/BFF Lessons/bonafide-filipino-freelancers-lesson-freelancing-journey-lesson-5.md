How to Prepare for Job Interviews

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
"Tell me about yourself." This is the question where 60 to 90 seconds can set the direction of the interview.

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
45 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
Anyone with upcoming interviews or interview anxiety

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
2026-05-15 (v2)

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
"The client asked me to tell them about myself. I blanked and stayed silent for 5 seconds."

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Common Freelancing Journey Lesson 5 question

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
There is no shame in this. Many candidates freeze on this question.

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
"Tell me about yourself" is a practice question, not a natural question.

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
You need a prepared structure. Record yourself, watch the playback, and iterate.

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
"What should I say about myself?"

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"What 60-second story answers (1) where I am from, (2) what I have done, and (3) why I am here?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
The 60-second "Tell Me About Yourself" formula

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
Section

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
What to say

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
Time

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
1. Where I'm from

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
"I'm a [role] based in [city]. Background in [industry]."

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
15 seconds

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
2. What I've done

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
2-3 specific achievements. Numbers where possible.

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
25 seconds

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
3. Why I'm here

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Connect to their company / role specifically.

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
15 seconds

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
4. CTA / question

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
End with a question. Sustains conversation.

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
5 seconds

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Sample answer (real)

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Maria's answer (60 seconds)

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Where

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
"I'm a Customer Support Specialist based in Manila with 5 years of BPO experience at [Company]."

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
What

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
"In my last role I handled 60+ tickets daily across phone and email, maintained 92% CSAT, and trained 4 new hires on our SOPs."

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Why

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
"I'm transitioning to remote VA work because I want to use my customer empathy + SLA discipline for an international team. I see your company is scaling, and I notice you mentioned needing help with the help desk system."

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Question

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
"How does your team handle response time targets?"

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
The warning about over-rehearsed answers

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Don't sound like a robot

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Recording yourself 10 times can turn your answer into robotic playback. The target is

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
natural, not memorized.

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Practice once. Record. Watch. Adjust. Practice once more. That's it.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Practice. Draft + record + submit. 45 min.
