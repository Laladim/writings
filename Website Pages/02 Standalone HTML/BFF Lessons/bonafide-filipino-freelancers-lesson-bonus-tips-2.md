Article Curation Training

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
The quiet skill content VAs need: turn a three-hour article search into a 20-minute system.

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
25 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
VAs supporting content creators, bloggers, newsletter writers

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
2026-05-15 (v2)

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
"My client wants a weekly newsletter with five curated articles, but it takes me three hours to find good ones."

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Common question from new BFF learners

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
Friend, the problem is not your ability to search. The problem is the lack of a system. Article curation is tool-driven. When the setup is right, the weekly search can take 20 minutes. Today we will build that system together.

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
"Where should I look for articles?"

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
"What feed-based system can bring 50 articles a week into my inbox, and how will I choose the best five?"

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
The 4-tool article curation stack

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
Tool

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
What it does

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
Free / paid

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
Feedly

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
RSS feed reader. Subscribe to 20-50 sources. Daily digest.

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
Free tier (100 sources)

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
Pocket

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
Read-later. Save articles from anywhere. Tag for client.

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
Free

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Google Alerts

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
Email when keyword appears anywhere on the web.

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
Free

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Twitter/X Lists

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
Curated voices in your niche. Real-time discovery.

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Free

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Workflow sample (1 weekly newsletter)

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
20-minute weekly cycle

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Mon (10 min)

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Feedly digest scan. Save 10 candidates to Pocket.

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Wed (5 min)

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Google Alerts review. Add 2-3 candidates to Pocket.

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Fri (5 min)

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Read all Pocket items. Pick top 5. Write 1-sentence summary each.

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Result

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
20 minutes total. 5 curated articles + summaries.

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
The warning about "too much research"

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
Don't read every article

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Curation is selection, not consumption. Skim the headline and first paragraph. If it takes too long to judge, it is not a keeper.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
20 minutes total per week, not 20 minutes per article.

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Practice. Setup curation stack. 30 min.
