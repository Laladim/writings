Pinterest Marketing

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
It is not a social network. Pinterest is a

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
search engine

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
. That changes the game.

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
16 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
SMM specialists supporting blogs, e-com, lifestyle brands

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
2026-05-15 (v2)

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
"My audience does not use Pinterest. Should I skip it?"

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
Common question from new BFF learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
Friend, it depends on the client's niche. For blogs, recipe sites, e-com, weddings, and home decor, Pinterest is a

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
discovery goldmine.

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
For B2B SaaS, usually not. But when it is relevant, Pinterest can bring long-tail traffic that FB + IG cannot serve.

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"Which client should use Pinterest?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
"Which client niche has buyers searching Pinterest for the solution they sell?"

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
The 4 niches where Pinterest beats IG

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
Niche

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
Why Pinterest wins

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
Food + recipes

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
"Recipe for ___" searches drive Pinterest 24/7. Bookmark behavior.

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
Wedding + events

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Planning behavior = bookmark = Pinterest core.

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
Home decor + DIY

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
Inspiration searches drive purchase intent.

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Blog + content sites

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
Long-tail SEO. Pins live 6+ months vs IG 24 hrs.

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Resource

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
The original BFF

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Pinterest Marketing slide deck

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
is accessible. Skim before starting.

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
The 5-step Pinterest strategy

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Setup workflow (one-time)

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
1. Convert to Business

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Free upgrade. Unlocks analytics.

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
2. Claim website

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Pinterest tracks pins linking to client site.

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
3. Build 10 boards

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Niche-specific board titles with keywords.

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
4. Pin 5-10 daily

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Mix client's own + curated. Consistency is what the algorithm rewards.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
5. Use

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Tailwind

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
for scheduling

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Free tier. Batch monthly.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
The warning about "all clients need Pinterest"

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
The wrong-niche trap

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
If your client is B2B SaaS, Pinterest is usually a waste. Their audience is not searching "best CRM" on Pinterest.

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Pinterest works for visual + planning + lifestyle niches.

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Tell the client clearly.

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Practice. Audit if Pinterest fits. 15 min.

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Pick a client (or hypothetical).

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
What's their niche?

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Search 5 keywords on Pinterest.

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Type "___ tips" or "best ___."

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Count results.

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
>100 results = active niche.

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
<

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
50 = skip.

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
If active: setup business account.

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
Build 10 boards. Pin 5 per day for 30 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
Track traffic in Google Analytics.

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
Pinterest referral after 60 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->

Optional skill. Pick if wedge fits.
