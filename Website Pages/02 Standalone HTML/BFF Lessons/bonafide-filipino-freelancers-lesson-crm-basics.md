Common question from new BFF learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
CRM is not scary. It is a glorified contact list with a pipeline view. The dashboard looks complex, but the core skill is small:

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
add contacts cleanly, move deals through stages, log activities, and pull reports

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
. Learn that pattern once, and a client can trust you with the system instead of only giving you simple admin tasks.

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"Which CRM should I study?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->

"What are the 4 entities inside every CRM, and how are they connected?"

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
(1) Contacts, (2) Companies, (3) Deals (or Opportunities), (4) Activities (calls, emails, tasks)

. They are the same in

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
HubSpot

,

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->

,

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->

. Master the 4 in one CRM, then transfer to any CRM in 2 hours.

The 4 entities of every CRM

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Entity

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
What it stores

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
VA job daily tasks

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
1. Contact

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
A person. Name, email, phone, title, LinkedIn. Tagged by source, stage, owner.

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Add · merge · enrich · tag

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
2. Company

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
The org. Name, domain, industry, size. One company has many contacts.

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Match contacts · update revenue/size

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
3. Deal / Opportunity

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Potential sale. Linked to a company + 1+ contacts. Has stage, amount, expected close date.

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
Create · move · update amount

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
4. Activity

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Emails sent, calls logged, meetings booked, tasks. Activities prove pipeline movement.

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Log · schedule · complete

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
The pipeline view in 1 sentence

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
A pipeline is a horizontal flow of deal stages (Lead → Qualified → Demo → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won / Closed Lost). The VA's job is to keep that flow moving with clean data and timely activities. You do not need to close the deal. You need to keep the pipeline from having stuck cards.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Why this skill is worth learning

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
In the VA Assessments dataset (641 candidates),

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
only 14% had CRM experience

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
. That 86% gap is the opportunity. You do not need to master every CRM today. You need to understand the four entities well enough to practice in one tool.

Example: a common solid CRM hygiene day

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
How a competent CRM-assistant VA's day looks

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
Morning · Inbox triage

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
New leads from website forms, LinkedIn DMs, referral emails. Add to CRM with source tag. 15-20 contacts/day typical.

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
Contact enrichment

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
Find missing LinkedIn URLs, company size, title. Use

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
LinkedIn Sales Nav

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
or

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
Apollo

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
if client has access. 30 min/day.

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Pipeline review

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
Look at every deal in "Qualified" or later stages. Has activity been logged in the last 7 days? If not, flag it for the manager.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
Deal stage moves

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
Sales rep sends Slack "moved DealA to Demo." VA confirms in CRM, updates close date, schedules next task.

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
Activity logging

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Listen to recorded sales calls (if shared). Log call outcome + next steps in deal record. ~10 calls/day.

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Friday · Weekly report

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
Pull pipeline by stage, value, owner. Send to sales manager. 1-page summary, screenshot of dashboard.

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
The architecture insight

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
CRM work is not glamorous. It is

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
data hygiene + activity logging + reminders

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
. But it directly affects the sales team's quota and the founder's revenue forecast. That "behind the scenes" feel is also why pay scales fast: $5/hr starting, $15-25/hr in 12 months if you specialize.

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
The warnings people usually skip

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Duplicates are the silent killer

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
The most common fire-able mistake: creating duplicate contacts. Marc dela Cruz and marc@company.com are the same person. Merge, do not create a new entry. Every CRM has a "find duplicates" tool. Run it weekly. A "200 duplicates merged this week" report is impressive to managers.

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
PII / data privacy is real

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
Contact data is personal information. GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and the Philippine Data Privacy Act can all apply. Do not export the database to your personal Gmail. Do not share screenshots with private contact details in a Facebook group. That is fire-able and legally risky.

Practice. 25 minutes, free HubSpot, fake pipeline.

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
Sign up at

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
hubspot.com/products/crm

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
with professional Gmail.

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Add 5 fake contacts

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
with names, emails, company, title. Use realistic-looking but obviously fake (e.g., "Jane Test · jane@testcompany.com").

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
Create 3 fake companies

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
for those contacts. Set industry, size, location.

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
Set up a 5-stage deal pipeline.

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
Settings → Objects → Deals → Pipelines. Stages: Lead → Qualified → Demo → Proposal → Closed Won. Or use HubSpot's default.

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
Create 3 deals

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
linked to your contacts + companies. Set amount + expected close date.

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
Log 5 activities.

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
2 emails sent, 2 calls scheduled, 1 task assigned to yourself. See activity history.

<!-- wbl-text:0130 -->
Run the pipeline report.

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
Reports → Dashboards → Sales Dashboard. Screenshot.
