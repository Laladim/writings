Browser Expert Secrets

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
Do not work from a messy browser. If your browser is disorganized, even your skills can look amateur.

18 minutes

"What browser am I using? Just the one that was already there when I bought the computer."

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
Common question from new BFF learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
That is the computer's default browser. It was not a choice you made. Using it is like still driving the practice car from driving school. Nothing is wrong with starting there, but it will slow you down. Today we will set up your browser so it stops stealing your attention.

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->

Better question:

"How do I set up my browser so it becomes a productivity tool, not a distraction?"

The 6 disciplines of a freelancer browser

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
Discipline

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Why it matters

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
Tool / setting

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
1. Multiple profiles

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Your work browser and personal browser should be separate. No Lazada notifications while you are on Zoom with a client.

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Chrome Profiles or Arc Spaces

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
2. Bookmark hierarchy

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
When you have client dashboards, project boards, and communication tools, they should be one click away. You should not have to hunt for them.

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Bookmarks bar + folders

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
3. Tab groups

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Twenty tabs can be normal. Fifty tabs is messy. Group tabs by project. When the work is done, close the whole group.

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Chrome Tab Groups (right-click → Add to group)

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
4. Password manager

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
You cannot rely on "I will remember everything." Use a password manager. The free

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Bitwarden

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
plan is enough to start.

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Bitwarden / 1Password

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
5. Ad-blocker + tracker blocker

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
If your client sees a pop-up ad during screen share, it does not look professional. Block them.

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
uBlock Origin

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
6. Screenshot + recording tools

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
When you are explaining a bug or a process, a screenshot or short video is ten times clearer than text.

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Loom

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
+

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Awesome Screenshot

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Lesson 01

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Memory Saver

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
feature, or consider

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Arc

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
if tab management is your biggest problem.

The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
Sometimes my browser still reaches 40 tabs. But now there is a system: tab groups by project, color-coded, and every Friday afternoon I close every tab I did not touch that week. The "bookmark this and close it" muscle takes about two weeks to build. After that, it becomes automatic.

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
The warning people usually skip

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
Privacy + security

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
When you share your screen with a client, they see everything your browser shows. The text notification. The old Lazada cart. The bookmark you did not mean to show.

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Open a clean work-only profile before any screen share.

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
"Sorry" will not erase that impression.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
One more thing: do not save client passwords in the default Chrome password manager if you share your computer. Use a real password manager with master password protection.

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
Practice. Open it now and give this 15 minutes.

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
Create a new Chrome profile.

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Click your photo (top-right) → Add → "Work" / "BFF VA". Set a distinct color so you do not confuse it with your personal profile.

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Move work bookmarks to the work profile.

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
Inside the work profile, create folders for Active Clients, Tools (OnlineJobs, Upwork, Calendly, etc.), and Reference (BFF, this page).

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
Install

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
these 4 extensions

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
(ad blocker),

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Bitwarden

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
(password mgr),

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
Loom

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
(screen recorder),

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
Grammarly

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
(writing helper).

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
Turn on Memory Saver.

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
Chrome → Settings → Performance → Memory Saver ON. Your computer may stay slow if this is off.

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Setup tab groups.

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
Right-click any tab → "Add tab to new group" → name it "Job hunting" or "Client X." Practice closing the group when done.

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
Bookmark this page.

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
Cmd+D (Mac) or Ctrl+D (Windows). When you come back tomorrow, it will be easy to find.

🌟 The Polished Freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
~25% · proceed ~80%

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
Multiple clients = multiple workflows. Browser is your operating system.

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
One profile per active client.

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
If you have three retainers, create three work profiles. It keeps context switching cleaner and faster.

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
Master keyboard shortcuts.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
Cmd+Shift+T (reopen closed tab), Cmd+Option+Arrow (switch tabs), Cmd+L (jump to URL). That 10-minute investment can return five hours per month.

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
Setup a search-engine-as-shortcut.

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
Chrome → Settings → Search → Manage. Add custom shortcuts: "tr" → Trello search, "n" → Notion search. Typing "tr clientname" in the URL bar should take you straight to the client board.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0143 -->
Chrome with multi-profile + 1Password Business + Loom Pro + Grammarly Premium. Total ~₱2,500/month, business expense.

<!-- wbl-text:0145 -->
~30% · proceed ~60%

<!-- wbl-text:0146 -->
BPO trained on locked-down corporate browsers. Now needs to setup their own.

<!-- wbl-text:0147 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0148 -->
You're allowed to install extensions now.

<!-- wbl-text:0149 -->
Corporate IT is no longer your gatekeeper. Install the four starter extensions today.

<!-- wbl-text:0150 -->
Translate your BPO ticket discipline to bookmarks.

<!-- wbl-text:0151 -->
Take the Active, Pending, and Closed discipline from ticketing tools and replicate it as bookmark folders.

<!-- wbl-text:0152 -->
Free Loom for client recordings.

<!-- wbl-text:0154 -->
Loom

<!-- wbl-text:0156 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0157 -->
Chrome + Bitwarden (free) + Loom free tier + Grammarly free. All ₱0.

🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur

<!-- wbl-text:0180 -->
~15% · proceed ~55%

<!-- wbl-text:0181 -->
Used to managing browser tabs across Shopify, Mailchimp, IG, FB Ads, supplier portals.

<!-- wbl-text:0182 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0183 -->
Separate "business old" vs "freelance new" profiles.

<!-- wbl-text:0184 -->
Put shop credentials in Bitwarden or 1Password under a "Shop" tag. Keep freelance credentials in a separate folder.

<!-- wbl-text:0185 -->
Bookmark your supplier + tax portals.

<!-- wbl-text:0186 -->
Bookmark BIR eFPS, Bureau of Customs, and supplier portals. Set it up once so tax season is faster.

<!-- wbl-text:0187 -->
Use

<!-- wbl-text:0188 -->
Google Sheets

<!-- wbl-text:0189 -->
as your master tab tracker.

<!-- wbl-text:0190 -->
Use the spreadsheet skill you built from e-commerce inventory work for client task tracking. Familiar workflow means less learning curve.

<!-- wbl-text:0191 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0192 -->
Chrome + Bitwarden + Loom + Google Sheets-based dashboard. Reuse existing spreadsheet skills.

📋 The Generalist Admin

<!-- wbl-text:0194 -->
~10% · proceed ~50%

<!-- wbl-text:0195 -->
Already organized. Now scale that discipline up.

<!-- wbl-text:0196 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0197 -->
Build a "command palette" bookmark bar.

<!-- wbl-text:0198 -->
Top 8 most-used tools, single-character labels: G (Gmail), C (Calendar), T (Trello), N (Notion), D (Drive), S (Sheets), L (LinkedIn), J (Jobs). Fast scan, fast launch.

<!-- wbl-text:0199 -->
Master Chrome's tab search.

<!-- wbl-text:0200 -->
Cmd+Shift+A opens tab search. When you have more than 30 tabs open, tab search becomes instant rescue.

<!-- wbl-text:0201 -->
Install

<!-- wbl-text:0202 -->
Momentum

<!-- wbl-text:0203 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0204 -->
Replaces new tab page with focus prompt + clean dashboard. Removes the temptation to check news on every new tab.

<!-- wbl-text:0205 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0206 -->
Chrome + Momentum + Bitwarden + Loom + master keyboard shortcuts. Free tier across the board.

🌱 The Fresh Starter

<!-- wbl-text:0208 -->
~5% · proceed ~30%

<!-- wbl-text:0209 -->
You already have Chrome. You may already have 50 tabs. What you need now is a reset and a system.

<!-- wbl-text:0210 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0211 -->
Bookmark everything important, then close all tabs.

<!-- wbl-text:0212 -->
Fresh start. Reopen only what you need today.

<!-- wbl-text:0213 -->
Just one profile to start.

<!-- wbl-text:0214 -->
You do not need three profiles yet. Add more when you get your first client.

<!-- wbl-text:0215 -->
Install ONLY 2 extensions:

<!-- wbl-text:0216 -->
uBlock Origin

<!-- wbl-text:0217 -->
+

<!-- wbl-text:0218 -->
Grammarly

<!-- wbl-text:0219 -->
. Add the others only when a client need appears.

<!-- wbl-text:0220 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0221 -->
Chrome + uBlock Origin + Grammarly. That is enough for now. Come back after Client #1.
