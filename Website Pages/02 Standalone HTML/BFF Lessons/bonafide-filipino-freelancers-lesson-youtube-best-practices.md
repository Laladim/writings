The warning about Shorts-only strategy

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Shorts create reach. Long-form creates revenue.

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Shorts get views but ad rev is low. Long-form drives subscription, course sales, sponsorship.

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Balanced strategy: Shorts for reach, long-form for monetization.
