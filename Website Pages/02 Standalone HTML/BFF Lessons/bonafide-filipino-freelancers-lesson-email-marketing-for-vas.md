The 4 pieces of every email marketing system

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Piece

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
What it is

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
What a VA does with it

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
1. List

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
The subscribers. Names + emails + tags. Tags are how you segment: "VIP," "Newsletter Subscriber," "Course Buyer," "Cold Lead."

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Import · clean · tag · suppress

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
2. Sequence

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
Automated emails that fire on trigger. A welcome series can be 5 emails over 7 days. Cart abandonment can be 3 emails over 48 hours.

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Build · trigger · timing

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
3. Broadcast

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
One-time send to part or all of the list. Newsletter is a broadcast. Sale announcement is a broadcast.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Draft · schedule · segment

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
4. Metrics

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Open rate, click rate, unsubscribe rate, conversion rate. This is what the VA reports back to the client weekly.

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Pull · format · explain

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Benchmark numbers (industry average, 2026)

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Open rate:

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
20-30% healthy.

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Click rate:

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
2-4% healthy.

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Unsubscribe rate:

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
under 0.5% per send. If open rate is below 15%, there may be a deliverability issue. If it is above 50%, it may be suspicious, or the list may be a very small VIP list.

Example: a common solid 5-email welcome sequence

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
Welcome sequence skeleton (used across thousands of brands)

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
Email 1 · Day 0 · Welcome + delivery

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Sent immediately. Confirms signup, delivers the freebie/lead magnet, sets expectations: "You'll hear from me on [days]." 60-100 words.

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
Email 2 · Day 2 · Origin story

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
Brand or founder story. Builds connection. Soft CTA: "Hit reply, tell me what you're working on." Replies = high deliverability boost.

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
Email 3 · Day 4 · The biggest mistake

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
Common mistake the audience makes + how to avoid it. Pure value, no pitch. Builds authority.

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Email 4 · Day 6 · Social proof

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Customer story or case study. "Here's what [past customer] did with this approach." Soft pitch: "Want this for yourself?"

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
Email 5 · Day 7 · Direct offer

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
Clear pitch: product/service, price, deadline if any, CTA button. After this sequence, they graduate to the broadcast list.

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
Daily output target (VA building these)

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
Draft 5 emails in 4 hours. Client review + revisions adds 2-3 hours. Full sequence built and tested in 1-2 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
The architecture insight

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
Email marketing is

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
NOT writing emails one by one

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
. The skill is system thinking: you ask, "When this subscriber responds, what is the next email automatically?" The if-then logic becomes the skill. Writing is 30%. Architecture is 70%.

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
The warnings people usually skip

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
Deliverability is the silent killer

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
The biggest trap: technically perfect campaigns that never reach the inbox because they land in Spam. Reasons: cold list, no authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), high bounce rate, low engagement.

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
Before any big send, run a test:

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
send to 100 engaged subscribers first. If open rate is under 15%, there is a delivery issue, not a content issue.

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL = real legal risk

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Every marketing email needs: (1) physical postal address in the footer, (2) clear unsubscribe link, (3) accurate "from" name. For EU subscribers: explicit opt-in (no pre-checked boxes). For Canada: express consent + record-keeping. Your client is accountable. As the VA, you protect them by knowing these.
