Freelancer or VA?

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
"Are those the same thing? Why do the labels matter?"

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
14 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
Anyone choosing between project-based and retainer-based work

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
2026-05-15 (v2)

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
Replaces:

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
"Are Freelancer and Virtual Assistant the same thing? The LinkedIn labels confuse me."

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
They are not the same. Both can be remote, both can involve Filipino workers and international clients, but

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
the business model is different.

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Understanding the difference gives you clarity on rates, contract structure, and which jobs to apply for. Today we will compare the two paths.

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"Which pays more, freelancer or VA?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
"Which business model fits my current lifestyle, financial situation, and career goal?"

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
One goal of

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
SWAHG Open University

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
is to help you understand your career path clearly. You do not need to choose one label for life. But you do need to choose one direction for the next 90 days so your applications are focused.

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
Freelancer vs Virtual Assistant: the 5-dimension comparison

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
Dimension

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
Freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Virtual Assistant

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
Engagement

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
Project-based: 1 deliverable, fixed scope, then done.

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Retainer-based: ongoing, hours/week or monthly contract.

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
Income predictability

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Variable. It can be feast or famine.

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Stable. Same client paying same amount per month.

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Skill ceiling

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Higher. Specialist rates: ₱2K-₱10K/hour for senior creative.

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Lower floor and ceiling. ₱200-₱1K/hour typical.

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Examples of work

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Logo design · 1 video edit · 1 ads campaign · 1 strategy doc

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Inbox management · scheduling · social media posting · CRM updates

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Platform

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Upwork

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
·

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Fiverr

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
· direct outreach

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
OnlineJobs.ph

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
· Upwork hourly contracts · direct hire

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
The third option

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Beyond Freelancer and VA, there is also a

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Specialized Skill

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
option: SDR, Account Manager, Customer Success, Content Marketer, and similar roles. The rate can be higher, the skill takes longer to build, and the work can be stable. If you already have transferable corporate skills, this may be your lane. See

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
SWAHG Jobs

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
for current listings.

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Example: a layered income mix for a mid-career freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Example income mix (Specialized-Skill freelancer)

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Primary income

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
A long-term retainer with one main client · ~70% of monthly income

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
Secondary income

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Project-based coaching / training / consulting · ~20%

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Tertiary income

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Personal-brand work (long-term play, e.g. writing site, newsletter) · ~10%

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Label

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
A Specialized Skill title (e.g. "Content Marketing Specialist") that is more specific than "VA" or "Freelancer"

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
It's common to start as a Generalist VA, drift into Freelancer projects, then evolve into a Specialized Skill (Content Marketing, SDR, Bookkeeping, etc.).

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
You are not stuck with your first label.

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
But you need to choose one direction for the next 90 days so your learning and job applications stay focused.

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
The warning the old video did not say

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
The "Generalist VA" trap

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
Saying "I can do everything: SMM, admin, design, video editing" feels like offering a buffet to the hiring manager. It does not make one clear reason to hire you.

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
Choose a wedge. Pick the one thing you are best at. Let the other five skills become bonuses.

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
That is what a polished freelancer does from day one.

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
Practice. Decide today. 12 minutes.

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
Score yourself on 5 questions (1-10 each):

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
I need stable monthly income (1 = no, 10 = absolutely)

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
I prefer ongoing client relationships over project bursts

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
I have a specific specialty I can charge premium for

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
I'm willing to bid on platforms vs direct hire

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
I value predictability over upside potential

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
Sum the score.

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
30-40 = VA path · 20-29 = Freelancer · 10-19 = Specialized Skill

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
Write your label.

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
"[Name] · [Wedge] · [Path]" for example: "Maricel Santos · Customer Support VA · Retainer-based."

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
Update your

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
LinkedIn

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
headline.

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Match the label. Your headline is the first filter.

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
Check current job listings for that path.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
Open

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
SWAHG Job List

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
filtered to your path's Role Category. See what's available NOW.

Action items, based on your archetype

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
🌟 The Polished Freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
~25% · proceed ~80%

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
Already have a label. Lesson is about sharpening it.

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
Decide: stay project-based or shift toward retainer?

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
Retainer = stable. Project = higher per-hour.

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
If shifting to retainer:

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Propose monthly engagement to 2 best clients this week.

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
Update profile across all surfaces:

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
LinkedIn, Upwork, OnlineJobs.ph. Consistent positioning.

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
💼 The Corporate Transitioner

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
~30% · proceed ~60%

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
VA path is the bridge. Replicate BPO discipline in remote retainer.

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
Default to VA path.

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Customer Service, Admin, Inbox VA. Familiar muscle, retainer-stable income.

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
Target

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
OnlineJobs.ph

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
PH-eligible, retainer-friendly, BPO-trained-favored.

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
Highlight shift availability in profile.

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
293 of 641 candidates had night-shift OK but didn't mention it. You should.

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
🎨 The Creative Specialist

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
~15% · proceed ~70%

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
Freelancer path natural. Project-based per creative deliverable.

<!-- wbl-text:0130 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
Default to Freelancer path.

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
Per-project pricing. Set minimum project rate now: ₱5K floor, ₱20K target.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
Target

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
Upwork

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
+ direct outreach via portfolio.

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
OnlineJobs.ph too low-rate for creative.

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
Consider hybrid: 1 retainer + project work.

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
The retainer gives you base income. Projects give you upside.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
~15% · proceed ~55%

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
VA or Specialized Skill, depends on hours.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0143 -->
Part-time VA path if running business simultaneously.

<!-- wbl-text:0144 -->
20 hrs/week max. Don't overcommit.

<!-- wbl-text:0145 -->
Full-time Specialized Skill if exiting business.

<!-- wbl-text:0146 -->
E-commerce Manager, Operations Specialist, Marketing Lead.

<!-- wbl-text:0147 -->
Frame as "Operations VA for E-commerce SMEs."

<!-- wbl-text:0148 -->
Your shop background is the differentiator.

<!-- wbl-text:0149 -->
📋 The Generalist Admin

<!-- wbl-text:0150 -->
~10% · proceed ~50%

<!-- wbl-text:0151 -->
VA path is the natural fit. Pick a wedge inside.

<!-- wbl-text:0152 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0153 -->
Default to VA path with a wedge.

<!-- wbl-text:0154 -->
Executive Assistant, Bookkeeping VA, Calendar Coordinator. Pick one.

<!-- wbl-text:0155 -->
Apply to retainer-only listings.

<!-- wbl-text:0156 -->
Sa OnlineJobs.ph, filter "Full Time" or "Part Time" (not "One-Time Job").

<!-- wbl-text:0157 -->
Quote monthly, not hourly.

<!-- wbl-text:0158 -->
₱25K/month flat reads more senior than ₱150/hour.

<!-- wbl-text:0159 -->
🌱 The Fresh Starter

<!-- wbl-text:0160 -->
~5% · proceed ~30%

<!-- wbl-text:0161 -->
No experience to lean on. VA path is the lowest-friction entry.

<!-- wbl-text:0162 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0163 -->
Default to VA path.

<!-- wbl-text:0164 -->
Customer Support, Data Entry, Admin. Lowest barrier to first client.

<!-- wbl-text:0165 -->
Don't claim Freelancer / Specialized Skill yet.

<!-- wbl-text:0166 -->
Earn the label after 6 months of VA work.

<!-- wbl-text:0167 -->
Apply only to

<!-- wbl-text:0168 -->
Newbie Friendly + No Calls

<!-- wbl-text:0169 -->
tabs.

<!-- wbl-text:0170 -->
Filter explicitly to entry roles.
