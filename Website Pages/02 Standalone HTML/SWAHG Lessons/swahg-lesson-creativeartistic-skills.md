The warning about "I'll learn 5 Adobe apps first"

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
The Adobe-collector trap

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
You do not need Photoshop + Illustrator + InDesign + Premiere + After Effects + XD all at once. The 5-tool ambition often leads to 0 clients.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Choose 2 tools max. Become an expert in them.

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Practice. Pick + start. 10 minutes.

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Read 8 paths above.

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Pick ONE based on archetype + budget.

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Click LinkedIn Learning path link.

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Add to "In Progress."

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Set 60-day finish-by.

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
Creative paths shorter than business.

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Build portfolio piece per week.

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
