The 5-step edit flow

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Step

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
What to do

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Time budget

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
1. Import + organize

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Drop footage into the bin. Rename clips. Star or color-tag the best takes. Make a separate folder for "selects." 1 minute of saved scrubbing pays 10x later.

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
5-10 min per project

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
2. Rough cut (story trim)

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Drag selects to timeline in story order. Cut the dead air, the "umms," the failed takes. Goal: get to story spine first, polish never.

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
15-30 min for 60-sec output

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
3. Captions + music

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Auto-caption (CapCut + Premiere + Resolve all have it). Style the text. Add music from licensed library. Around 80% of TikTok/Reels viewers watch on mute.

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
10-15 min

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
4. Color + transitions

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Match exposure across clips. Add 2-3 transition types max (cut, fade, whip). Do not over-decorate. Restraint is a sign of a pro.

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
10-15 min

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
5. Export to platform specs

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
9:16 vertical for TikTok/Reels/Shorts. 16:9 for YouTube long-form. H.264 codec, MP4, 1080p target. Check file size on the target platform.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
2-5 min export

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->

The toolkit

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Tool

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Strong for

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Price reality

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
CapCut

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
SWAHG default. Free on mobile + desktop. Built for short-form vertical. Auto-captions, trending sounds, easy effects. 70% of TikTok content is edited here.

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
Free with optional Pro

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
Premiere Pro

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
Industry-standard for agencies + brands. Heavier learning curve. Higher pay band for "Premiere skilled."

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
~₱1,200/month subscription

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
Final Cut Pro

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
Mac-only. One-time purchase. Fast renders. Common among solo creators + Apple-shop YouTubers.

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
$300 one-time (Mac only)

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
DaVinci Resolve

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
Free version is fully professional. Best color grading in the industry. Steeper UI but rewards investment.

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
Free (Studio version $300)

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
Descript

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
Edit video by editing text (like a doc). Best for podcast-clip workflow, talking-head content.

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
Free tier with limits

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
SWAHG default for first 2-3 months:

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
CapCut Desktop

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
. Free, fast, polished output. Master it, then upgrade to Premiere or Final Cut when client work demands.

Example: a common solid 60-second Reels build

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
Anatomy of a 60-second branded Reels edit

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
0:00-0:03 · Hook

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
Strongest visual or claim. Big text caption: "3 things I wish I knew before X." Cut at hard 3-second mark.

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
0:03-0:20 · Setup

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
Context for the claim. B-roll over voiceover. Caption on screen continuously. Keep visual change every 2-3 seconds (cut to new shot, zoom, text overlay).

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
0:20-0:50 · Payload

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
The 3 points (or however many). Numbered captions. New visual per point. Music tempo matches scene change.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
0:50-0:60 · CTA

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
"Follow for more / save this / comment X" pattern. Big visual end card with branded logo + handle. Caption double-size.

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
Music

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Use trending sound (from CapCut library or platform's sound library) for organic-reach algorithm boost. Or licensed library track for safer brand work.

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Captions style

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
White text, black stroke, sans-serif (Inter or Montserrat). Centered, lower-third position. Animated word-by-word reveal for short attention spans.

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
The architecture insight

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
Video editing is not "make it look pretty."

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
It is shaping attention

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
. The viewer decides in the first 3 seconds whether to stay. The skill is engineering that decision. Same tool, same software, but the editor with an attention-architecture mindset earns 3x more.

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
The warnings people usually skip

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Music licensing is real

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
The "free trending sound" in the CapCut library may be licensed for personal use on TikTok. If your client uses your edit in a YouTube ad or branded campaign, there may be legal exposure. Use the platform's own commercial library or licensed services:

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
Artlist

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
,

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
Epidemic Sound

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
,

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
Musicbed

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
. Client pays subscription, VA uses with permission.

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Aspect ratio = revenue

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
A 16:9 horizontal video exported with vertical letterbox black bars on TikTok is almost guaranteed to lower engagement. ALWAYS check the target platform specs. 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts. 1:1 for IG feed. 16:9 for YouTube long-form. Wrong aspect = wasted work.

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
Practice. 30 minutes, free CapCut, one Reels.

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
Download

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
CapCut Desktop

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
(Mac or Windows) or mobile app.

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
Record (or find) 60 seconds of footage.

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
Phone selfie video of yourself explaining "3 things I learned in SWAHG this week" works perfectly.

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
Build the 4-block structure

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
(Hook 3s, Setup 17s, Payload 30s, CTA 10s) as described above. Use placeholder if your footage is shorter.

<!-- wbl-text:0130 -->
Auto-caption.

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
CapCut: Text → Auto Captions → Generate. Style it: white text, bold, centered, slight stroke.

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
Add music from CapCut library.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
Trending sound or chill background. Match audio level so caption-only viewers still understand.

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
Color correct.

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Filters → choose a subtle warm or cool filter. Avoid heavy. Adjust brightness if your footage is dark.

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
Export.

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, MP4, 30fps. Save the file.

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
Watch your final video on your phone.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
Does it grab attention by second 3? Are captions readable? Music balanced?
