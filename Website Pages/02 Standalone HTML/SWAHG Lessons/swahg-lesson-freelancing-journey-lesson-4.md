How to Improve a Crappy Cover Letter

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
The cover letter that gets through the filter. Not the "Dear Sir/Madam I am applying" template.

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
40 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
Anyone who's sent generic cover letters with zero replies

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
2026-05-15 (v2)

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
"My cover letter says, "Dear Hiring Manager, I am applying for the position posted." My friend said it is weak."

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Common Freelancing Journey Lesson 4 question

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
Friend, your friend is right. "Dear Hiring Manager" + "I am applying" is a death pattern. It does not get read. Today we will walk through an anti-formula cover letter that shows the client you understood what matters within 5 seconds.

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
"What template should I use?"

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
"What is 1 specific thing about this client that other applicants have not named yet?"

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
The 3-paragraph cover letter structure

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
Paragraph

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Purpose

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
Length

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
1. Specific opening

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
Name THEM. "I saw your LinkedIn post about scaling Q3" or "I noticed your new Shopify launch."

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
2-3 sentences

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
2. Proof + fit

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
Cite 1 specific past result. "Increased CSAT from 78% to 91% at [Company]." Then connect to their need.

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
3-4 sentences

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
3. Specific CTA

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
"Available for 30-min call next Tuesday. Here's my Calendly: [link]." Not "looking forward to hearing from you."

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
1-2 sentences

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Anti-formula cover letter sample

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
Before (weak) vs After (strong)

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Weak opening

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
"Dear Hiring Manager, I am applying for the VA position you posted."

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Strong opening

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
"Hi [First Name], I noticed your IG post last week about needing help with batch content for Q4. I've done exactly that for [other client] last quarter."

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Weak proof

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
"I have 3 years experience as VA."

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Strong proof

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
"In my last role, I batched 60 IG posts/month for a fitness brand. Saved client 8 hours/week. Engagement went from 1.2% to 3.4%."

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Weak CTA

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
"Looking forward to hearing from you."

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
Strong CTA

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
"I have a 30-min slot open Tuesday 10am Manila time. Calendly: [link]. Or reply with your preferred time."

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
The warning about over-customization

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Don't write a novel

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
The 5-paragraph essay is the opposite trap. The hiring manager may have 200 applications.

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
3 short paragraphs. 150-200 words max.

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Save the depth for the interview.

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Practice. Build template + 1 customized. 40 min.
