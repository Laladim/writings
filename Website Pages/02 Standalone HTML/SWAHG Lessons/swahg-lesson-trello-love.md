Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
You are not stupid, friend. Your memory is not the problem. The problem is that you do not have an external brain. Our heads cannot hold every deadline across 5 clients.

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
The brain is for thinking, not for storing.

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
That is why every senior freelancer has a board. PM tool usage has only

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
23% coverage

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
in the SWAHG community. The other 77% is still relying on text + memory. That is a drop-out funnel.

Asana

Notion

The 4 essentials of a working Trello board

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Essential

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Why it matters

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
How to do it

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
1. Standard list flow

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Same lists across every board = predictable. Inbox → This Week → In Progress → Waiting → Done.

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
5 lists, in this order

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
2. Due dates on every card

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Card without due date = task without urgency. Even "no rush" deserves a date.

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
Press D to add due date

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
3. Labels for client / type

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
If you have 3 clients, color labels create a visual scan. Red = ClientA, Blue = ClientB, Green = ClientC.

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Press L to label

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
4. Checklists for multi-step

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
"Edit + publish + send invoice" is 3 tasks, not 1. Use a checklist within the card.

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Add Checklist button

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Power-Up insight

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Free Trello = 1 Power-Up per board. Use it for the

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Calendar Power-Up

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
so you can see due dates as a calendar view. If you want 2+ Power-Ups, upgrade to the

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Standard plan

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
(~₱300/month) or use the calendar built into

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Google Calendar

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
with Trello import.

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
I tried Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, Linear, GitHub Projects, Airtable. They are not useless. I returned to Trello because it is predictable, fast to set up per new client, and does not make me overthink which field to fill.

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
The best tool is the one you actually open every day.

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
The warning people usually skip in the video

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
PM tool coverage is 23%

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
The VA Assessments data (641 candidates):

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
only 23% report using a PM tool.

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
The other 77% is still on text + email + memory. This skill is a dramatic advantage. In your interview: "I use Trello to track client deliverables across 3 active retainers" beats "I am organized" 10x. Concrete > adjective.

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
Other warning: do not let the client see your messy Trello. If you keep an internal "brain dump" board, separate it from the "client-facing" board.

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
Show them outcomes, not every false-start card.

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
Practice. Open it now, 18 minutes.

🌟 The Polished Freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
~25% · proceed ~80%

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
Multi-client = multi-board. Trello as your operating system.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
One board per active client.

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Naming: "ClientName_Y2026Q2" so you can archive cleanly per quarter.

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
Add

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
Butler automation

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
When a card moves to "Done," auto-archive after 7 days. Saves manual cleanup time.

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
Templates for repeated workflows.

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
Turn your "monthly content batch" into a card template. Click → instantiate → done.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0143 -->
Trello Standard or Premium plan + Butler + templates + per-client boards.

<!-- wbl-text:0144 -->
💼 The Corporate Transitioner

<!-- wbl-text:0145 -->
~30% · proceed ~60%

<!-- wbl-text:0146 -->
BPO ticketing trained. Trello is the freelance translation.

<!-- wbl-text:0147 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0148 -->
Mirror BPO ticket lifecycle as your lists.

<!-- wbl-text:0149 -->
New → In Progress → Pending → Resolved → Closed. Same DNA, different surface.

<!-- wbl-text:0150 -->
Use checklists for SOPs.

<!-- wbl-text:0151 -->
Recreate the BPO habit of "process every ticket the same way" as a Trello checklist template. Concrete + repeatable.

<!-- wbl-text:0152 -->
Track SLA-equivalent timing.

<!-- wbl-text:0153 -->
Add due date + label "Within 24h" / "Within 48h" / "EOM." Signal your BPO discipline.

<!-- wbl-text:0154 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0155 -->
Trello free + checklists per card + due date discipline + label by urgency.

🎨 The Creative Specialist

<!-- wbl-text:0157 -->
~15% · proceed ~70%

<!-- wbl-text:0158 -->
Creative work = drafts, revisions, approval cycles. Trello is the review tracker.

<!-- wbl-text:0159 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0160 -->
Lists by review state:

<!-- wbl-text:0161 -->
Concept → Draft 1 → Client Review → Revisions → Approved → Delivered. The approval cycle, visualized.

<!-- wbl-text:0162 -->
Attach preview images.

<!-- wbl-text:0163 -->
Drag-and-drop your latest version directly into the card. Cover image shows up in the board view. Visual scan.

<!-- wbl-text:0164 -->
Link to

<!-- wbl-text:0165 -->
Figma

<!-- wbl-text:0166 -->
/

<!-- wbl-text:0167 -->
Canva

<!-- wbl-text:0168 -->
in card description.

<!-- wbl-text:0169 -->
When you click the card, it jumps directly to the source file.

<!-- wbl-text:0170 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0171 -->
Trello + Figma Power-Up + drag-drop cover images + per-project boards.

<!-- wbl-text:0172 -->
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur

<!-- wbl-text:0173 -->
~15% · proceed ~55%

<!-- wbl-text:0174 -->
Already managing inventory + orders + customer queries. Now needs lighter task surface.

<!-- wbl-text:0175 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0176 -->
Migrate your business tasks to Trello first.

<!-- wbl-text:0177 -->
Before freelance, fix the existing chaos. For the next 2 weeks, log ALL business todos in Trello. Build the habit with familiar context.

<!-- wbl-text:0178 -->
Reuse your batch-thinking skill.

<!-- wbl-text:0179 -->
Replicate the "Monday morning batch" from your shop into freelance: pick 1 day per week as "deep work" day, with Trello cards filtered by that day.

<!-- wbl-text:0180 -->
Separate boards: Business · Freelance · Family.

<!-- wbl-text:0181 -->
3 boards minimum. Do not mix them.

<!-- wbl-text:0182 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0183 -->
Trello free with 3 boards + batch-day filter + checklist templates.

<!-- wbl-text:0184 -->
📋 The Generalist Admin

<!-- wbl-text:0185 -->
~10% · proceed ~50%

<!-- wbl-text:0186 -->
Natural fit. Trello rewards organization-minded people.

<!-- wbl-text:0187 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0188 -->
Build a "Master Index" board.

<!-- wbl-text:0189 -->
Cards = each client. Description = link to the client's own dedicated board. Single starting surface.

<!-- wbl-text:0190 -->
Master Trello shortcuts.

<!-- wbl-text:0191 -->
N (new card), D (due date), L (label), F (filter), B (open menu). 5 minutes to learn, hours saved.

<!-- wbl-text:0192 -->
Pair with

<!-- wbl-text:0193 -->
Google Calendar

<!-- wbl-text:0194 -->
+

<!-- wbl-text:0195 -->
Toggl Track

<!-- wbl-text:0196 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0197 -->
Trello = what to do. Calendar = when. Toggl = how long. The trinity of admin productivity.

<!-- wbl-text:0198 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0199 -->
Trello + Calendar + Toggl Track + master index board + shortcut mastery.

🌱 The Fresh Starter

<!-- wbl-text:0201 -->
~5% · proceed ~30%

<!-- wbl-text:0202 -->
You do not know which PM tool yet. Start with the simplest.

<!-- wbl-text:0203 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0204 -->
Just one board: SWAHG Job Hunt.

<!-- wbl-text:0205 -->
Use your practice board seriously. Track every application for 30 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0206 -->
Don't customize anything yet.

<!-- wbl-text:0207 -->
Default colors, default lists. Simple is easy to maintain. Add the kingdom later.

<!-- wbl-text:0208 -->
Open Trello every morning, 7 AM.

<!-- wbl-text:0209 -->
5 minutes to review board + add new cards from yesterday. The morning ritual is key.

<!-- wbl-text:0210 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0211 -->
Trello free + 1 board + daily 5-minute review ritual. That is enough for now.
