"I have a client as an SMM. But I am not sure what I should post. I am just winging it."

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
Friend, the post itself is not the problem. The problem is no foundation. No strategy. The 5 SMM Core Strategies are the foundation. Without them, even your most polished Reel will miss the audience.

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
You know WHO you are now and you know WHO you want to become.

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
That is the beginning.

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
"Which viral trend should I copy?"

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"WHO am I, WHO is my client, WHO is my audience, WHY are we doing this, WHERE are they, WHAT do they need, HOW do I deliver?"

1

WHO (you)

2

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->

3

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
4

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
WHAT (need)

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
5

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
HOW (execution)

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
You know HOW you can achieve those business needs and achieve success.

The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Most beginners do this audit AFTER 6 months of fumbling. That wastes time.

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
Run the 5-strategy audit BEFORE applying to SMM clients.

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
Save yourself 6 months of "post and pray."

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
The warning about "I'm just posting"

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
The "post-and-pray" trap

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
Without the 5 strategies in place, your SMM work becomes "post-and-pray." Often no engagement. No growth. No impact.

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
Your client may disengage after 90 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
The 5 strategies are required infrastructure.

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
Practice. Audit yourself OR your next client. 25 minutes.
