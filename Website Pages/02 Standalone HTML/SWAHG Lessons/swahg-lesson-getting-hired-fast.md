"I have been studying for 6 months. I still do not have a client. What should I do?"

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
Friend, the issue is not always lack of study. The missing piece is

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
application

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
. ACTION TAKER is the phrase that should describe a great SWAHG student.

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
It is good to read and watch video lessons but the success happens when we make our ideas and learning into action.

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Today we will walk through the jump from "learner" to "applicant."

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
"What skill do I still need to study before applying?"

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
"Do I already have enough skill to sell to a first client, even if it is not perfect yet?"

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
The answer is almost always "yes." A 60-70% skill level is already enough for entry-level VA work. 100% perfection is not attainable.

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
There is no magic trick. YOU NEED TO DO THE WORK.

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
But the "work" is not more studying. The "work" is application + interview + first month.

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
The 5 action-taker behaviors that get hired fast

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Behavior

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
What it looks like in practice

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
1. Volume + targeting

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
5-10 quality applications per day, not 1 perfect application per week. Apply consistently.

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
2. Build proof while applying

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Don't wait for paid client to start portfolio. Mock projects, volunteer work, free pilots all count.

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
3. Custom every application

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Each cover letter 60% template + 40% client-specific. Read their site, mention 1 specific thing.

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
4. Reply within 12 hours

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
When client replies, you reply same day. The fast-replier wins, not necessarily the deepest CV.

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
5. Send

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
Loom videos

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
for outreach

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
2-minute personalized video > 2-page cover letter. Stands out in inbox of 200 applicants.

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
The one platform at a time rule

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
Study and explore on one platform at a time!

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
If SMM is your path, focus on Instagram for 30 days. Then Facebook for 30 days. Then LinkedIn for 30 days. Not 5 platforms in parallel. Depth beats breadth.

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
My fastest hire, a mentee case study

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Janelle, SWAHG mentee, 2025

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Archetype

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Corporate Transitioner (5 years BPO Customer Support)

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Day 1 commit

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Setup OnlineJobs.ph + Upwork. Took aptitude tests.

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Days 2-7

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Applied to 30 customer support roles. 24 hrs reply discipline.

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Day 12

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
First interview. US-based startup. Sent Loom intro before call.

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Day 15

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Hired. ₱45K/month retainer. Start date Day 20.

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Why she won

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
Replied within 4 hours. Sent custom Loom. Matched timezone. Verified tools knowledge.

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Janelle was not the most experienced applicant. Some applicants had 10 years of BPO experience. But

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
she was the action taker.

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Others replied after three days. Others did not send a portfolio link. Janelle showed up with the complete package in her first reply.

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
The warning about perfection paralysis

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
The "I'll apply when I'm ready" trap

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
"I need three more courses before I apply" can become a

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
protective story.

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
It feels comfortable because it keeps you away from rejection. But six months later, you may still be in the same place.

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
Apply with what you have today. Learn from the rejections. Iterate.

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
Practice. 30-day Action Taker challenge. Setup now.

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
Open your

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
Trello board

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
Title: "30-Day Action Taker Challenge."

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
Daily commitment: 5 applications per weekday.

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
25/week. 100 in 30 days.

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
For every application:

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
Read job description 2x

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
Customize cover letter (3 sentences specific to them)

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
Attach shareable resume link

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
If high-value role: send

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
Loom

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
intro

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
Mark "Sent" in Trello + log datetime

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
Daily 12-hour reply rule.

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
When client replies, you reply same day.

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
Track funnel weekly:

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
Apps Sent → Replies → Interviews → Offers.

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
30-day expected outcome:

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
100 apps → 10-20 replies → 3-5 interviews → 1-2 offers.

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
Action items, based on your archetype

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
🌟 The Polished Freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
~25% · proceed ~80%

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Already an action-taker. Focus on quality bidding + retainer conversion.

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
3 high-quality applications per day, not 10.

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
Senior level = more depth per app.

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
Send Loom intro for every $1000+/month opportunity.

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
Differentiate yourself.

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
Follow up after 5 days if no reply.

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Senior freelancers follow up. Junior ones don't.

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
💼 The Corporate Transitioner

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
~30% · proceed ~60%

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
Disciplined. Easiest archetype to execute the 30-day challenge.

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
5 customer support / admin applications per day.

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
Familiar territory.

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
Highlight night-shift availability + BPO tenure.

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Specific differentiators.

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
Send Loom for any role > ₱30K/month.

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
Sound + look professional via video.

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
🎨 The Creative Specialist

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
~15% · proceed ~70%

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
Portfolio is your action-taking proof.

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
5 applications per day for creative roles.

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
Each with portfolio link prominent.

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
Send Loom walkthrough of your portfolio.

<!-- wbl-text:0130 -->
90-second tour of best 3 pieces.

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
Pitch unsolicited spec work.

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
"I redesigned your IG profile, here's the mockup" stands out.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
~15% · proceed ~55%

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Action-orientation is natural. Channel toward applications.

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
Cold-pitch to e-commerce store owners on LinkedIn.

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
Direct outreach > platform bidding.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
Send Loom showing your shop's IG growth as proof.

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
"I did this for my own business, I can do this for yours."

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
5 platform applications + 3 direct outreaches daily.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
Hybrid funnel.

<!-- wbl-text:0143 -->
📋 The Generalist Admin

<!-- wbl-text:0144 -->
~10% · proceed ~50%

<!-- wbl-text:0145 -->
Methodical. Will execute the 30-day plan reliably.

<!-- wbl-text:0146 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0147 -->
5 wedge-aligned applications per day.

<!-- wbl-text:0148 -->
Bookkeeping → bookkeeping. Inbox → inbox.

<!-- wbl-text:0149 -->
Quote monthly retainer rates.

<!-- wbl-text:0150 -->
Stand out from hourly bidders.

<!-- wbl-text:0151 -->
Track funnel diligently in

<!-- wbl-text:0152 -->
Trello

<!-- wbl-text:0153 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0154 -->
Use your admin skill for the job hunt.

<!-- wbl-text:0155 -->
🌱 The Fresh Starter

<!-- wbl-text:0156 -->
~5% · proceed ~30%

<!-- wbl-text:0157 -->
Hardest archetype to execute volume. Quality first.

<!-- wbl-text:0158 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0159 -->
3 applications per day, not 5.

<!-- wbl-text:0160 -->
Per app, more time on customization. Avoid overwhelming yourself.

<!-- wbl-text:0161 -->
Apply only to Newbie Friendly tab.

<!-- wbl-text:0162 -->
Don't waste energy on senior roles.

<!-- wbl-text:0163 -->
Complete 1 free course in parallel with applications.

<!-- wbl-text:0164 -->
Skill + applying simultaneously.
