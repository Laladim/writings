"I already have an OnlineJobs.ph profile. It has been 6 months. Still no replies."

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
Let's look at your profile.

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
The first step to get a home-based job is to create a profile that clients will never reject.

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
Having an account is not enough. Your profile needs to match the employer's filters. Focus on the details clients actually click and evaluate.

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"Which category should I choose?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
"What impression will my profile give in the first 5 seconds of a client's view?"

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
OnlineJobs.ph

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
is a PH-native job platform. The "OnlineJobs" tab in the SWAHG

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
Job List

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
usually has 5+ live listings on any given day. But if your profile is incomplete, employers will not find you in search. Today we will finish the 9-section profile build.

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
The 9 sections of a strong OnlineJobs.ph profile

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
#

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Section

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
What to put

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
1

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Profile photo

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
Clear headshot. Plain background. Smile or neutral. No beach selfie.

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
2

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
Full name

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
Real name. First + Last. No nickname.

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
3

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Skill summary (3 lines)

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
"Customer Support VA with 4 years BPO experience. Bilingual EN/TL. Available night shift PST/EST." Specific.

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
4

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Skills (4-8 selected)

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Not 30 skills. Choose your 4-8 best, work-used skills. Use the auto-fill from your

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Resume Builder Step 2

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
5

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
Cover letter (uploadable)

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
1-page PDF. Templated but customizable. Per-application customization expected.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
6

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Resume (PDF, ATS-friendly)

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Export from

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
SWAHG Resume Builder

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
. No tables, no columns, single-column format.

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
7

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Salary expectation

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Not "negotiable." Use a range like "₱25K - ₱35K/month" or hourly "$5-$8/hour." Filter for serious leads.

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
8

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Availability

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
"Full Time, 40 hrs/week" OR "Part Time, 20 hrs/week, PST overlap." Specific.

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
9

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Verified ID + ID badge

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Upload PH government ID for verification. The badge that comes after is a trust signal.

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
The verification advantage

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
ID-verified profiles get 3-5x more views

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
than unverified. It is not expensive and does not require a fixed appointment. Submit it Day 1.

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
Winning profile signals from real hiring data

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
What hiring managers filter by

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
English proficiency rating

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
Self-rated. Honest about it. 3 stars is better than a false 5 stars.

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
Last login date

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
Active in last 7 days = answered quickly. Inactive = passed over.

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
Test scores

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
The

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
OnlineJobs.ph aptitude tests

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
are free. Take 3-5 in your target niche.

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
Hours overlap

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
"Will work 9pm-5am Manila for US client" is signal. Surface explicitly.

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
Common pattern: a beginner OnlineJobs.ph profile with no photo, no specific skill summary, generic resume.

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
Zero replies for 2 months.

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
Once all 9 sections get fixed, views climb and a first interview lands within ~3 weeks.

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
The warning especially for Fresh Starters

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
The "premium profile" pressure

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
OnlineJobs.ph has free and paid plans.

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
The free plan is enough for applying.

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
Paid plans are mostly for the employer side, not candidates. If you see an "upgrade to premium" prompt, ignore it for v1. Think about upgrading only after you have your first client.

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
Other warning:

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
do not pay anyone to setup your OnlineJobs.ph profile

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
. Some scammers say, "I'll get you a job for ₱5,000." That's not how this works. SWAHG is free. OnlineJobs setup is free. You should not pay anyone for this.
