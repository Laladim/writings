Email Address Expert Secrets

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
The address

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
sexyblue_27@yahoo.com

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
will not work. Clients do not reply to that.

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
Length:

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
15 minutes

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
For:

<!-- wbl-text:0021 -->
Every learner before applying to a single job

<!-- wbl-text:0022 -->
Updated:

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
2026-05-15 (v2)

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
Replaces:

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
2019 video lesson

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
"I have been applying, but no one replies. It has been three months."

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
I can look at your resume. But before we do that, can you show me the email address you use when applying? Sometimes the problem is not the resume itself. The problem is the identity it appears to come from.

<!-- wbl-text:0029 -->
The wrong question vs the right question

<!-- wbl-text:0030 -->
Wrong question:

<!-- wbl-text:0031 -->
"What is the easiest email password for me?"

<!-- wbl-text:0032 -->
Better question:

<!-- wbl-text:0033 -->
"When a hiring manager reads my email address, what is their first impression?"

<!-- wbl-text:0034 -->
The first question gives you an email account. The second question gives you a

<!-- wbl-text:0035 -->
professional identity

<!-- wbl-text:0036 -->
. It is still free. You do not need to buy a domain yet.

<!-- wbl-text:0037 -->
Gmail

<!-- wbl-text:0038 -->
is enough, but we need to set it up properly.

<!-- wbl-text:0039 -->
The 5 rules of a freelancer email address

<!-- wbl-text:0040 -->
Follow this 5-rule framework. It is not difficult, but it needs to be strict.

<!-- wbl-text:0041 -->
Rule

<!-- wbl-text:0042 -->
Why it matters

<!-- wbl-text:0043 -->
Example

<!-- wbl-text:0044 -->
1. Use your real name

<!-- wbl-text:0045 -->
Hiring manager wants to know who you are in 1 second. Nicknames at numbers confuse the search.

<!-- wbl-text:0046 -->
maricel.santos@gmail.com

<!-- wbl-text:0047 -->
2. Gmail only

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Yahoo + Hotmail signal old-school.

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
Gmail

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
works on every tool a client uses.

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
@gmail.com (not @yahoo.com)

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
3. Add a middle initial or last name if taken

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
If maricel.santos@gmail.com is already taken, use maricel.b.santos or maricelsantosva.

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
maricel.b.santos@gmail.com

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
4. No numbers like birth year

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
"maricelsantos1985" gives away age. We do not need to give bias extra ammunition.

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
Avoid: maricel1985@gmail.com

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
5. One email per identity

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Keep personal email and freelance email separate. If you have a client thread, a "[Shopee notification]" should not sit in the same inbox.

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
2 separate Gmail accounts

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Free, with no catch

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
You do not need to buy a domain like

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
yourname.com

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
at the start. That is a Tier 3 upgrade. But

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
aim for that within 12 months

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
. You can use

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Namecheap

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
(~₱500/year) or

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Google Domains

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
. When you have a yourname.com email, it signals that "this person treats freelancing as a business."

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
What my setup looks like now

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
It is not just one email. I use a system.

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
My email stack

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Primary work email

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
shela@cobaltintelligence.com (work, paid by company)

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
Personal freelance brand

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
laladim.work@gmail.com (community + SWAHG + writings)

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
Personal life

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
shelaheramis@gmail.com (family, banks, shopping)

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
Email signature tool

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
HubSpot Email Signature Generator

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
(free)

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
The trick: when a client message comes in, the

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
inbox name

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
immediately tells me which role I need to wear. It is not confusing. No Shopee receipt appears in the middle of a client thread.

<!-- wbl-text:0086 -->
The professional signature

<!-- wbl-text:0087 -->
At the bottom of every email, add a signature with your name, role, link to your

<!-- wbl-text:0088 -->
LinkedIn

<!-- wbl-text:0089 -->
profile, and portfolio link if you have one. Use

<!-- wbl-text:0090 -->
HubSpot's free generator

<!-- wbl-text:0091 -->
if you have not made one yet. It is better than the Gmail default.

<!-- wbl-text:0092 -->
The warning I sometimes forgot to say

<!-- wbl-text:0093 -->
This is not drama

<!-- wbl-text:0094 -->
If you apply using

<!-- wbl-text:0095 -->
cutiepie_maricel_2003@yahoo.com

<!-- wbl-text:0096 -->
, you may not survive three seconds in the hiring manager's inbox.

<!-- wbl-text:0097 -->
Even your resume cannot overcome that.

<!-- wbl-text:0098 -->
The email address is the first filter. If it fails there, the resume never gets a real chance. Before overhauling your CV, fix the email first. It takes two minutes.

<!-- wbl-text:0099 -->
One more thing: do not use the work email from your current or former company (BPO or office) to apply for freelance work. You will lose access when you leave. And if your employer sees it while you are still applying, that becomes a problem.

<!-- wbl-text:0100 -->
Practice. Do this now, just 10 minutes.

<!-- wbl-text:0101 -->
Open

<!-- wbl-text:0102 -->
Gmail signup

<!-- wbl-text:0103 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0104 -->
Create a new account: firstname.lastname@gmail.com. If it is taken, use your middle initial.

<!-- wbl-text:0105 -->
Setup recovery options.

<!-- wbl-text:0106 -->
Add a phone number + recovery email. If you forget your password, you want to recover access quickly.

<!-- wbl-text:0107 -->
Enable 2-step verification.

<!-- wbl-text:0108 -->
In

<!-- wbl-text:0109 -->
Google Account Security

<!-- wbl-text:0110 -->
, turn on 2FA. Your client can notice this, and it signals professionalism.

<!-- wbl-text:0111 -->
Set your profile photo.

<!-- wbl-text:0112 -->
Headshot. Clear face. Plain background. No beach selfie. No anime.

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
Create your signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
Use

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
HubSpot's free generator

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
. Save inside Gmail settings: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
Test it.

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
Send a sample email to yourself from your old email. Check whether the signature works and whether the overall impression feels professional.

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
Audit checklist:

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
New Gmail uses real first + last name

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
No birth year, no nicknames, no underscores with random words

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
2-step verification enabled

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
Profile photo is a clear, plain-background headshot

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
Signature includes: name, "Virtual Assistant" / role title, LinkedIn URL, location (City, Philippines)

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
Personal email separated from this freelance email

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
Action items, based on your archetype

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
Each archetype needs a different level of email setup. Take the

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
Resume Builder quiz

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
if you do not know your archetype yet.

🌟 The Polished Freelancer

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
~25% · proceed ~80%

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
Multi-client roster already. Email is a brand surface, not just a contact channel.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
Upgrade to a custom domain.

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Buy

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
yourname.com

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
from

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
Namecheap

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
(~₱500/year), forward to your Gmail via

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
Google Workspace

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
(₱360/month). hello@yourname.com signals senior immediately.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
Setup canned responses.

<!-- wbl-text:0143 -->
Gmail Settings → Advanced → Templates. Save 3 templates: project inquiry, rate request, scheduling. Save 5 minutes per email.

<!-- wbl-text:0144 -->
Add scheduling link to signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0145 -->
Use the

<!-- wbl-text:0146 -->
Calendly

<!-- wbl-text:0147 -->
free tier. Drop your booking URL right in the email signature. No more "what time works?" ping-pong.

<!-- wbl-text:0148 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0149 -->
Custom domain (Namecheap) + Google Workspace + HubSpot signature + Calendly. Total ~₱500/year domain + ₱4,300/year Workspace.

<!-- wbl-text:0150 -->
💼 The Corporate Transitioner

<!-- wbl-text:0151 -->
~30% · proceed ~60%

<!-- wbl-text:0152 -->
BPO discipline, with corporate email experience first. Now needs a freelance identity.

<!-- wbl-text:0153 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0154 -->
Don't use your BPO/company email for freelance apps.

<!-- wbl-text:0155 -->
You will lose it when you resign. Create a new personal Gmail this week.

<!-- wbl-text:0156 -->
Mirror BPO professionalism in your signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0157 -->
Your corporate work email has a signature with title + department + contact details. Copy that structure for your freelance email. "Customer Support Specialist · 6 years experience · Manila, PH"

<!-- wbl-text:0158 -->
Setup folder system day one.

<!-- wbl-text:0159 -->
Gmail labels: Applications, Active Clients, Admin, Personal.

<!-- wbl-text:0160 -->
Gmail filters

<!-- wbl-text:0161 -->
auto-tag incoming. Use your BPO ticketing discipline here.

<!-- wbl-text:0162 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0163 -->
New Gmail + 2FA + HubSpot signature + Gmail filters for auto-labeling. All free.

<!-- wbl-text:0164 -->
🎨 The Creative Specialist

<!-- wbl-text:0165 -->
~15% · proceed ~70%

<!-- wbl-text:0166 -->
Portfolio is your asset. Email is the bridge to it.

<!-- wbl-text:0167 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0168 -->
Lead with portfolio link in signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0169 -->
For creatives, LinkedIn is not the most important link. Your

<!-- wbl-text:0170 -->
Behance

<!-- wbl-text:0171 -->
,

<!-- wbl-text:0172 -->
Dribbble

<!-- wbl-text:0173 -->
, or personal portfolio matters more. Make it the top link.

<!-- wbl-text:0174 -->
Custom email like contact@yourname.com.

<!-- wbl-text:0175 -->
For creatives, having a branded email matters even more. Get a custom domain ASAP, even before Polished tier. Use

<!-- wbl-text:0176 -->
Namecheap

<!-- wbl-text:0177 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0178 -->
Visual signature, but tasteful.

<!-- wbl-text:0179 -->
Logo + name + portfolio + IG. Use

<!-- wbl-text:0180 -->
HubSpot generator

<!-- wbl-text:0181 -->
or design your own in

<!-- wbl-text:0182 -->
Canva

<!-- wbl-text:0183 -->
. Test it renders on Gmail mobile.

<!-- wbl-text:0184 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0185 -->
Custom domain + portfolio-first signature + Behance/Dribbble link. Optional:

<!-- wbl-text:0186 -->
Notion

<!-- wbl-text:0187 -->
-based portfolio.

<!-- wbl-text:0188 -->
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur

<!-- wbl-text:0189 -->
~15% · proceed ~55%

<!-- wbl-text:0190 -->
Already had business emails, but mixed with personal life and storefront.

<!-- wbl-text:0191 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0192 -->
Hard separate: business, freelance, personal.

<!-- wbl-text:0193 -->
3 emails minimum. Old shop email stays for closing accounts. New freelance email for VA work. Personal for family.

<!-- wbl-text:0194 -->
Migrate vendor contacts before closing old business email.

<!-- wbl-text:0195 -->
Export contacts via

<!-- wbl-text:0196 -->
Google Contacts

<!-- wbl-text:0197 -->
. Do not lose your supplier list when you shut down the shop email.

<!-- wbl-text:0198 -->
Translate business experience into signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0199 -->
"Operations · E-commerce · 4 years scaling a [N]-customer business." Frame the entrepreneur background as a transferable skill in the signature itself.

<!-- wbl-text:0200 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0201 -->
3 separate Gmail accounts + Google Contacts export + bold signature that names the business background as an asset.

<!-- wbl-text:0202 -->
📋 The Generalist Admin

<!-- wbl-text:0203 -->
~10% · proceed ~50%

<!-- wbl-text:0204 -->
Calendar-disciplined, email-organized already. Translate that skill into signal.

<!-- wbl-text:0205 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0206 -->
Pick a wedge in the signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0207 -->
"Bookkeeping VA · QuickBooks + Xero" or "Inbox-Zero VA · Help Scout + Front" or "Executive Assistant · Remote · Calendly + Notion." Mas specific mas mabuti.

<!-- wbl-text:0208 -->
Setup Gmail filters mastery-level.

<!-- wbl-text:0209 -->
On the

<!-- wbl-text:0210 -->
Gmail Filters page

<!-- wbl-text:0211 -->
: auto-archive newsletters, auto-label by sender domain, auto-star from active clients. This is where your admin skill shows.

<!-- wbl-text:0212 -->
Pair Gmail with Google Calendar + Tasks.

<!-- wbl-text:0213 -->
Calendar invites cannot be missed. Master

<!-- wbl-text:0214 -->
Google Calendar

<!-- wbl-text:0215 -->
+

<!-- wbl-text:0216 -->
Tasks

<!-- wbl-text:0217 -->
integration this week.

<!-- wbl-text:0218 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0219 -->
Gmail + advanced filters + Calendar + Tasks + wedge-driven signature. All free.

<!-- wbl-text:0220 -->
🌱 The Fresh Starter

<!-- wbl-text:0221 -->
~5% · proceed ~30%

<!-- wbl-text:0222 -->
First Gmail account ever, or only had a school email. Start clean.

<!-- wbl-text:0223 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0224 -->
Just one email first.

<!-- wbl-text:0225 -->
You do not need 3 accounts. One professional Gmail with your real name is enough.

<!-- wbl-text:0226 -->
Simple signature.

<!-- wbl-text:0227 -->
Name + "Aspiring Virtual Assistant · Currently learning [skill]" + Location. The "currently learning" line signals growth, not inexperience.

<!-- wbl-text:0228 -->
Practice inbox discipline.

<!-- wbl-text:0229 -->
Check email twice daily, not every 5 minutes. Use

<!-- wbl-text:0230 -->
Gmail mobile

<!-- wbl-text:0231 -->
with notifications OFF for the freelance account. It is a focus tool, not a distraction.

<!-- wbl-text:0232 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0233 -->
One Gmail + 2FA + headshot photo + simple HubSpot signature. Come back after Client #1 for the next-tier upgrade.
