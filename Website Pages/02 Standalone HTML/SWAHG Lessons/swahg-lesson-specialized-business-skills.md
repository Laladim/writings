The warning about course collectors

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
The "I'm still studying" trap

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Many SWAHG learners have 4-5 unfinished LinkedIn Learning paths. The idea that 5 half-done paths are better than 1 finished path is not true.

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Pick one. Finish it. Add it to your resume. Apply to jobs with that path's role.

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Then you can choose the next one.

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Practice. Pick ONE path. 12 minutes.
