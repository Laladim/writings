<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Example: a healthy 4-platform online presence

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Example 4-platform setup

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
LinkedIn

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Professional profile · clear role title · connections built deliberately over years

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Facebook

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
Personal page locked down · public surface limited to work-related content · admin of a community group if you run one

<!-- wbl-text:0071 -->
Instagram

<!-- wbl-text:0072 -->
Personal-brand handle · selective posting · Stories for ongoing community

<!-- wbl-text:0073 -->
Portfolio

<!-- wbl-text:0074 -->
Your own URL (writings site / portfolio / case studies). Owned surface, not rented.

<!-- wbl-text:0075 -->
The honest moment

<!-- wbl-text:0076 -->
A common setup: Facebook personal posts locked to "Friends Only." Public surface is limited to work-related content.

<!-- wbl-text:0077 -->
You don't have to share everything publicly

<!-- wbl-text:0078 -->
. Privacy settings are our tools.

<!-- wbl-text:0079 -->
The warning that sometimes makes applicants cry

<!-- wbl-text:0080 -->
The old-photo trap

<!-- wbl-text:0081 -->
Old photos on Facebook, including anything from college or early twenties,

<!-- wbl-text:0082 -->
may still be visible even if you do not remember them.

<!-- wbl-text:0083 -->
Most clients check Facebook before hiring. The pre-onboarding background check is real.

<!-- wbl-text:0084 -->
Spend 2 hours cleaning up before your first major application.

<!-- wbl-text:0085 -->
Practice. Audit + cleanup. 60 minutes (one-time).
