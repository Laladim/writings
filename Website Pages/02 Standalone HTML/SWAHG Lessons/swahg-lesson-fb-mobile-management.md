"I do not have laptop access 100% of the time. Can I do SMM using only my phone?"

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
Friend, yes.

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
You can manage an FB Page 70-80% from mobile.

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
The remaining 20-30%, such as analytics deep-dive, batch scheduling, and paid ads, needs a laptop. But day-to-day operations are mobile-friendly. Today we will walk through the mobile-first workflow.

The warning about "phone-only" framing

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Be honest in interviews

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
If a client asks, "do you have a laptop?", be honest: "I have access to a laptop 2-3x/week, primary device is mobile."

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
If you say "yes" and it is not true, that becomes a problem.

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Phone-only is viable for SOME roles, not all roles.

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
