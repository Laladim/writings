The non-negotiable resume sections

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
1-page structure

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Header

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Name + role title + city + email + LinkedIn + portfolio link

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
Professional Summary

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
2-3 sentences. Wedge-specific. No clichés.

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Skills (4-8)

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
Work-used, not aspirational. From

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Resume Builder tool rating

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Experience

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
Reverse chronological. Bulleted achievements with numbers.

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Education

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
School + degree + year (only if recent or relevant).

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Certifications

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Any cert from Lesson 17-19 work. Even partial.

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
The warning about "design over content"

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
ATS-friendly

<!-- wbl-text:0068 -->
Canva templates sometimes have multi-column layouts + graphics. The client's ATS (Applicant Tracking System) may not be able to parse those.

<!-- wbl-text:0069 -->
Single column. Standard fonts. No icons in skill names.

<!-- wbl-text:0070 -->
Tasteful design is enough.
