Prerequisite:

<!-- wbl-text:0023 -->
Trello Love

<!-- wbl-text:0024 -->
"I already understand Trello, but the new client uses ClickUp. I feel nervous."

<!-- wbl-text:0025 -->
Common question from new SWAHG learners

<!-- wbl-text:0026 -->
No need to panic. All modern PM tools are

<!-- wbl-text:0027 -->
variations of the same 4 concepts

<!-- wbl-text:0028 -->
: items (cards), states (lists), people (assignees), dates (due/duration). If you are fluent in Trello, 70% of the skill transfers. The remaining 30% is polish in the new tool. That is the lesson.

The 5 patterns that transfer across every PM tool

<!-- wbl-text:0048 -->
Pattern

<!-- wbl-text:0049 -->
What it does

<!-- wbl-text:0050 -->
Trello equivalent

<!-- wbl-text:0051 -->
1. Workspace structure

<!-- wbl-text:0052 -->
Workspaces, Spaces, Folders, Lists, Tasks. Hierarchy is the bones. If a client has 50 lists and no folder structure, that is chaos.

<!-- wbl-text:0053 -->
Workspace · Board · List · Card

<!-- wbl-text:0054 -->
2. View modes

<!-- wbl-text:0055 -->
Same data, different lens. Board for visual flow. List for inbox-style. Calendar for deadlines. Timeline (Gantt) for sequencing.

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
Trello board view is the only view in free

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
3. Custom fields

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Tags + due dates are basic. Custom fields add: priority, estimate hours, status, assignee, client. Power user territory.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
Trello labels + checklists

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
4. Automations

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
"When task moves to Done, archive after 7 days." "When due date passes, notify me." Saves 30 min/day if used.

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
Trello Butler

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
5. Reporting

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Weekly velocity, completion rate, hours by client. The data you show clients to justify retainer.

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
Trello free has minimal reports

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->

<!-- wbl-text:0113 -->
The architecture insight

<!-- wbl-text:0114 -->
Multi-tool fluency is not "use all of them constantly."

<!-- wbl-text:0115 -->
It is meeting each client at their existing tool.

<!-- wbl-text:0116 -->
If the client already has Asana, do not migrate them to Trello. If the client has no tool, that is when you propose. Adaptation is the value, not standardization.

<!-- wbl-text:0117 -->
The warnings people usually skip

<!-- wbl-text:0118 -->
Tool sprawl is real

<!-- wbl-text:0119 -->
5 clients, 5 PM tools, 5 notification streams. Burnout pattern.

<!-- wbl-text:0120 -->
Rule: max 3 active PM tools at any time.

<!-- wbl-text:0121 -->
If a 4th enters, decide: drop a client or consolidate. Your bandwidth is not infinite, even if you are organized.

<!-- wbl-text:0122 -->
Do not over-build the client's system

<!-- wbl-text:0123 -->
Common rookie mistake: client gives you a simple ClickUp setup, you "improve" it with custom fields, automations, reports. Client cannot find anything anymore.

<!-- wbl-text:0124 -->
Match the client's complexity ceiling.

<!-- wbl-text:0125 -->
Add only when asked or when you can show clear ROI.

<!-- wbl-text:0126 -->
Practice. 25 minutes, one new tool, real workflow.

<!-- wbl-text:0127 -->
Pick Asana for this practice.

<!-- wbl-text:0128 -->
Sign up at

<!-- wbl-text:0129 -->
asana.com

<!-- wbl-text:0130 -->
with professional Gmail.

<!-- wbl-text:0131 -->
Create a Project: "SWAHG Job Hunt v2."

<!-- wbl-text:0132 -->
Use the List view as default. Asana defaults to List, not Board, unlike Trello.

<!-- wbl-text:0133 -->
Add 5 tasks with these custom fields:

<!-- wbl-text:0134 -->
Company, Role, Application Date (date), Status (dropdown: To Apply / Applied / Interviewing / Closed), Priority (dropdown: High / Medium / Low).

<!-- wbl-text:0135 -->
Switch to Board view.

<!-- wbl-text:0136 -->
See the same data as columns. The three view modes (List, Board, Calendar) are the first big difference from Trello.

<!-- wbl-text:0137 -->
Build one rule (automation).

<!-- wbl-text:0138 -->
When Status changes to "Applied," automatically move the task to the "Applied" section.

<!-- wbl-text:0139 -->
Asana Rules

<!-- wbl-text:0140 -->
are the equivalent of Trello Butler.

<!-- wbl-text:0141 -->
Switch to Timeline view.

<!-- wbl-text:0142 -->
See your applications by date. Visual gap = "you applied to nothing for 5 days." Useful diagnostic.

<!-- wbl-text:0143 -->

Get

Asana Academy certified

Notion templates portfolio.

💼 The Corporate Transitioner

<!-- wbl-text:0166 -->
~30% · process maturity transfers

<!-- wbl-text:0167 -->
Corporate environments often use enterprise PM tools. Your familiarity is an asset. The challenge: smaller teams move faster.

<!-- wbl-text:0168 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0169 -->
Master Asana first.

<!-- wbl-text:0170 -->
Mid-market companies (50-500 employees) often default to Asana. Your BPO/corporate background slots in cleanly.

<!-- wbl-text:0171 -->
Learn the "workspace permissions" concepts.

<!-- wbl-text:0172 -->
Who sees what, who can edit, guest roles. When sensitive client data is involved, this matters.

<!-- wbl-text:0173 -->
Volunteer to "audit" a current employer's PM setup.

<!-- wbl-text:0174 -->
Builds your "before and after" portfolio piece. Use as reference when interviewing.

<!-- wbl-text:0175 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0176 -->
Asana + project audit portfolio piece + clean handoff documentation. Mid-market VA fit.

<!-- wbl-text:0177 -->
🎨 The Creative Specialist

<!-- wbl-text:0178 -->
~15% · creative review flow

<!-- wbl-text:0179 -->
Creative projects need approval cycles. PM tools with built-in proofing beat Trello here.

<!-- wbl-text:0180 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0181 -->
Try Notion +

<!-- wbl-text:0182 -->
Frame.io

<!-- wbl-text:0183 -->
combo.

<!-- wbl-text:0184 -->
Notion for project plan, Frame.io for video review. Built for the creative review cycle.

<!-- wbl-text:0185 -->
Master Asana's Proofing feature.

<!-- wbl-text:0186 -->
Premium feature but trial-available. Drop image, click to comment exact pixel. Similar to Loom + ClickUp Annotations.

<!-- wbl-text:0187 -->
Build a creative project template.

<!-- wbl-text:0188 -->
Concept → Draft 1 → Review → Revisions → Approval → Delivery. Same DNA as Trello but with proper proofing infrastructure.

<!-- wbl-text:0189 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0190 -->
Notion + Frame.io + Asana with proofing. Creative-VA premium tier.

<!-- wbl-text:0191 -->
🛒 The Solo Entrepreneur

<!-- wbl-text:0192 -->
~15% · ops at scale

<!-- wbl-text:0193 -->
Your shop ops + new freelance work. The tool is for scale, not learning theater.

<!-- wbl-text:0194 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0195 -->
Migrate your shop ops to ClickUp.

<!-- wbl-text:0196 -->
Inventory, supplier follow-ups, order issues. ClickUp's custom fields handle SKU + supplier + status cleanly.

<!-- wbl-text:0197 -->
Notion for SOPs.

<!-- wbl-text:0198 -->
Document how you fulfill an order. Your VA, once you have one, can be onboarded in 1 day, not 1 month.

<!-- wbl-text:0199 -->
Pitch ecom clients on "ops consolidation."

<!-- wbl-text:0200 -->
Many shop owners have no time to set up systems. Your $300-500 setup fee saves them 40 hours.

<!-- wbl-text:0201 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0202 -->
ClickUp for ops + Notion for SOPs + Trello for personal tasks. Ecom-VA fit.

<!-- wbl-text:0203 -->
📋 The Generalist Admin

<!-- wbl-text:0204 -->
~10% · pm-tool consultant track

<!-- wbl-text:0205 -->
Tool fluency + admin discipline + reporting skills. PM Tool Consultant is a legitimate VA upgrade path.

<!-- wbl-text:0206 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0207 -->
Master 3 tools deeply: Asana, ClickUp, Notion.

<!-- wbl-text:0208 -->
4 hours per tool. That 12-hour investment can create a $5-10/hr rate bump.

<!-- wbl-text:0209 -->
Build a "PM tool audit" service.

<!-- wbl-text:0210 -->
1-page checklist: structure, custom fields, automation, reporting. Charge $100-200 per audit.

<!-- wbl-text:0211 -->
Pair with Time Tracking.

<!-- wbl-text:0212 -->
Cross-sell: "I will audit your PM tool AND set up time tracking with billable reports." $500 engagement, ongoing retainer follow-up.

<!-- wbl-text:0213 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0214 -->
Asana + ClickUp + Notion fluency · audit service · retainer follow-through. Senior admin VA path.

<!-- wbl-text:0215 -->
🌱 The Fresh Starter

<!-- wbl-text:0216 -->
~5% · master one, mention the others

<!-- wbl-text:0217 -->
Not ready for 3 tools yet. Stick to deep Trello + one secondary. Pick the secondary based on your first client.

<!-- wbl-text:0218 -->
Do this week

<!-- wbl-text:0219 -->
Stay on Trello first.

<!-- wbl-text:0220 -->
Depth beats breadth. Master shortcuts (N, D, L, F), Butler, templates. 80% of jobs accept Trello.

<!-- wbl-text:0221 -->
Add ONE secondary tool

<!-- wbl-text:0222 -->
based on your first client's stack. If client uses Notion, you learn Notion. If Asana, Asana. Do not try everything at once.

<!-- wbl-text:0223 -->
Watch

<!-- wbl-text:0224 -->
3 comparison YouTube videos

<!-- wbl-text:0225 -->
(Asana vs ClickUp, Notion vs ClickUp, Trello vs Asana). Familiarity without mastery is useful for interview small talk.

<!-- wbl-text:0226 -->
Recommended stack:

<!-- wbl-text:0227 -->
Trello deep + 1 secondary tool based on first client. Just-in-time learning.

Universal rule

<!-- wbl-text:0229 -->
For every archetype:

<!-- wbl-text:0230 -->
tool fluency is a means, not an end.

<!-- wbl-text:0231 -->
The tool is the surface. The skill is project structure thinking. Every PM tool may change or die in 5 years. The skill of seeing how a workflow breaks down into steps lasts.
