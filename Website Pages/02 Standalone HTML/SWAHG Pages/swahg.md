How SWAHG Works

<!-- wbl-text:0056 -->
No clickbait. No upsells.

<!-- wbl-text:0057 -->
Just the next concrete step.

<!-- wbl-text:0058 -->
Three rules SWAHG runs on — set by Shela, the SWAHG founder, in 2019 and unchanged since.

<!-- wbl-text:0059 -->
📚

<!-- wbl-text:0060 -->
Free, forever.

<!-- wbl-text:0061 -->
No "and then sell them a $497 course" energy. SWAHG was free in 2019, free today, free tomorrow. If anyone tells you otherwise, that's not us.

<!-- wbl-text:0062 -->
🇵🇭

<!-- wbl-text:0063 -->
Built for Filipino learners.

<!-- wbl-text:0064 -->
Written for Filipino context, in clear English. Faith-warm, not faith-required. PH job market specifically, with listings that change as the job board refreshes. No US-centric "401k" advice.

<!-- wbl-text:0065 -->
⏱️

<!-- wbl-text:0066 -->
Finishable in fragments.

<!-- wbl-text:0067 -->
Lessons are 15–25 minutes. Designed for the mom who has 20 minutes after the kids are asleep. Sustainability beats speed.
