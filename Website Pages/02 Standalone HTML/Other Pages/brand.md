The Mark

<!-- wbl-text:0013 -->
A book opened, two stories held

<!-- wbl-text:0014 -->
The mark is a single book seen from two angles. The standing cover and the lying pages share a corner — a purple cover wrapping into gold pages — with the wordmark integrated on the page face. The page-stack lines that wrap the corner read as one continuous binding:

<!-- wbl-text:0015 -->
two stories, one spine

<!-- wbl-text:0016 -->
.

<!-- wbl-text:0017 -->
Primary · Light

<!-- wbl-text:0018 -->
Use on cream, white, and warm-paper backgrounds

<!-- wbl-text:0019 -->
Primary · Dark

<!-- wbl-text:0020 -->
Cream cover + gold pages on deep navy. Use on rich purple, dark navy, or photographic backgrounds.

<!-- wbl-text:0181 -->
Source Serif 4

<!-- wbl-text:0182 -->
She wrote because the page asked her to — not because anyone was watching. The page is patient. It waits while you find the right word, the right beat, the right turn. And when you have circled the thought enough times, it lets you set it down.

<!-- wbl-text:0183 -->
Optical-sized serif optimized for long-form reading. Body text 16–18 px / 1.65 line-height. Pairs with Cormorant headings for editorial calm.

<!-- wbl-text:0186 -->
Inter

<!-- wbl-text:0187 -->
Library · About — clean, unobtrusive, high-readability for navigation and labels.

<!-- wbl-text:0188 -->
Sans-serif for navigation, captions, eyebrow labels, and metadata. Weight 400–600. Letter-spacing +0.02em for uppercase labels.

<!-- wbl-text:0191 -->
Warm authority. Quiet faith. No corporate.

<!-- wbl-text:0192 -->
Writings by Lala is a personal-brand publication. Every post follows a 7-beat structure:

<!-- wbl-text:0193 -->
vulnerability → scene → inner critic → empathy pivot → lesson via story → faith anchor → warm invitation

<!-- wbl-text:0194 -->
. The voice is the woman who has been there — not the expert who has read about it.

<!-- wbl-text:0195 -->
Specific, not abstract

<!-- wbl-text:0196 -->
Open with a raw sentence — a body, a time, a place. Never a thesis or a question to the reader.

<!-- wbl-text:0197 -->
Quoted, not summarized

<!-- wbl-text:0198 -->
When the inner critic speaks, quote her sentences. Don’t describe what she said — let her say it.

<!-- wbl-text:0199 -->
Invited, not sold

<!-- wbl-text:0200 -->
End with a today-sized action the reader can take. Never “subscribe now,” never “join my community.” A warm invitation, quietly placed.

<!-- wbl-text:0201 -->

Rules

<!-- wbl-text:0237 -->
What protects the mark

<!-- wbl-text:0238 -->
Do

<!-- wbl-text:0239 -->
Place on warm cream, ivory, or natural paper backgrounds for the primary variant.

<!-- wbl-text:0240 -->
Reserve the dark variant for deep navy, rich purple, or photographic dark surfaces.

<!-- wbl-text:0241 -->
Keep at least 1× page-band of clearspace on every side.

<!-- wbl-text:0242 -->
Use the favicon variant below 32 px width.

<!-- wbl-text:0243 -->
Print with Pantone match for foil-stamped or letterpress runs.

<!-- wbl-text:0244 -->
Pair with Cormorant italic headings and Source Serif 4 body.

<!-- wbl-text:0245 -->
Don’t

<!-- wbl-text:0246 -->
Don’t recolor the cover or pages outside the documented palette.

<!-- wbl-text:0247 -->
Don’t stretch, skew, or rotate the mark. The L is the L.

<!-- wbl-text:0248 -->
Don’t add drop shadows, glows, gradients, or 3D bevels beyond the existing rendering.

<!-- wbl-text:0249 -->
Don’t place the mark on busy photographic backgrounds without a cream or navy plate behind it.

<!-- wbl-text:0250 -->
Don’t pair with neon, saturated brights, or corporate blue palettes.

<!-- wbl-text:0251 -->
Don’t separate the wordmark from the mark — they ship together.
