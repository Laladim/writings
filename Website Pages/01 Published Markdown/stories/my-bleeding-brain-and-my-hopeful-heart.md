My brain bled in 2019. It bled again this year.

Cerebral Cavernous Malformation is the name. An abnormal blood vessel that bleeds in my brain. This past week the headaches have been severe. The dizziness comes and goes. Sometimes I lose my balance.

What if one night, everything stops being normal?

The simple act of standing, you can no longer do.

The familiar comfort of lying down, you no longer feel.

When the pain in your head feels like being pummeled with concrete, there is nothing left to do but. Cry. Endure. Wait. For it to end.

Even when you want to cry hard, you cannot. The people in the house will wake up.

Your mind starts saying things.

"This is your fault."

"You do not take care of yourself well."

"You keep staying up late."

"Instead of resting properly, you do all kinds of things."

You cannot stop it, and from your mind it moves to your mouth.

"This is my fault."

I have something to say to you if this is you.

I understand why you wear yourself out. Yes, it is your fault, because you do not take care of yourself.

But I also know it is because of your desire to be successful for the people you love and the people around you. You forget yourself.

I want to hug you tight. I want to cry next to you. I want to hold your hand and say, "It is normal to make mistakes. It is normal to be tired."

I was the same.

There is so much I want to do in this life that I always need someone to put on the brakes for me.

AJ, my family, the SWAHG family. They are the ones who put the brakes on me.

"Sleep already. That can wait until tomorrow."

"Relax for now."

"Rest, Ms La."

Maybe you are like me. When you are focused on finishing something, you will finish it.

Sometimes we need brakes in life. Especially when our earthly body is not made for severe battles.

Sometimes we need to be reminded that we do not need to chase everything, because what is meant for us is for us.

What we need is to always be ready to fight.

Why not push?

Because when you are in "force mode" just to finish something, the output is not good anyway.

All my inspirations are coming from a relaxed mind.

The good outputs come because I was inspired.

Quality over quantity.

Live your life. Be present in every moment.

You can do that even with many things to do. You just have to be ready to fight and ready to rest.

The people who push you to not get tired and to not be afraid to dream, they are also the people who will tell you when you need to slow down.

In the end, they are the ones the Lord placed in your life. You do not have to be perfect. You can dream and you can make mistakes.

I am happy that alongside my loved ones, the SWAHG team is among the people the Lord placed in my life.

If you are in a hard season tonight, do not let it take the love that is already around you. Before you sleep, name one person who reminds you to slow down. They are not an accident.

For the glory of God.

— Lala
