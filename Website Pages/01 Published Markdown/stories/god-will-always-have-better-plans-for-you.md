by: Mary Jean "Jean" Cinence

SWAHG Moderator

I was in the moment of depression earlier this year to the point of losing my self-worth and stopped believing that I have a space in this field.

I prayed and asked for a sign and it was very timely when I saw Lala's live session and announced the opening of the SMM Masterclass. I decided to join her Masterclass, completed the Freelancing Journey, and finally got a one on one coaching last July 7, 2019.

It really helped me rebuild my foundation as a freelancer as she teaches and motivates me a lot. She also gave me a chance to be a part of her group where I found endless support and a new family in this freelancing world. I followed everything that I learned from her and her FJ and started again.

After 3 weeks of working hard, revising and improving my resume, cover letter, etc., and patiently waiting for the right time, I got hired last July 29! A new client, which I believe, was an answered prayer that made me so grateful even more.

I know it's still early to say it was a complete success.

I know it's just a part of another step.

I know that there will still be ups and downs in my journey.

But I know I was one step further because I have a better foundation and I have the support.

This is not to boast, but to inspire others who are losing hope and keep asking if they can make it into the virtual world. You might lose a client or an interview now, but always remember that if a door closes, there will always a new door that will open.

Just keep learning...

Keep trying...

Keep going...

Just keep believing that you can...

Believe that even though your plans don't work,

God has better plans for YOU.

~   Jean Cinence
