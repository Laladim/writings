I dreamed of a work station. A wide desk. A window beside it. A violet-painted room. A computer that did not hang in the middle of a client call.

I worked for three years before I got it.

So I want to encourage you to be patient. It might be faster for you. It might be slower. What matters is we get there.

The day you plant the seed is not the day you eat the fruit.

— Lala
